# Screen Studio argument card

## Buyer

A Mac user who regularly makes short product demos and wants polished movement without a full editor.

## Decision

Choose Screen Studio when its automatic polish cuts total production time and helps the viewer follow the explanation.

## Why

Zoom, cursor movement, and framing can make a raw screen recording presentable quickly. Routine audio, trimming, annotation, and team handoff can still require another tool.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if zoom correction takes too long, common mistakes need a second editor, required files are too large, Windows teammates cannot contribute, or cancelled plans block needed exports.

## Verdict

Fast Mac demos when automatic polish saves real time.
