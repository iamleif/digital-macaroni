# Featurebase spoken review brief

Featurebase puts feedback and support together. A support question can become a product request, move onto the roadmap, and lead to an update for the same customer. That full loop is the reason to buy it.

Fibi can answer basic questions, but wrong answers need a fast route to a person. The bill also includes more than seats because AI resolutions, heavy Copilot use, branding, and other usage can cost extra.

I would test four real requests from inbox to release update. I would skip Featurebase if the team only needs votes or if the current helpdesk is too important to replace without stronger proof.
