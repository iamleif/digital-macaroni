# Featurebase argument card

## Buyer

A growing software team that wants feedback, product updates, help content, and support to share one customer record.

## Decision

Choose Featurebase when the full request-to-update loop removes repeated copying between tools. Skip it for a small suggestion board.

## Why

Connected support and feedback provide more context than votes alone. The suite can replace several handoffs, though AI needs supervision and one outage affects more customer work.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if context gets lost during the loop, Fibi cannot hand off safely, exports are weak, support loses ownership, or usage charges make the busy-month bill poor.

## Verdict

Customer requests become clear decisions and useful updates.
