# Featurebase private argument card

- Buyer: a software team considering one product for customer feedback and support
- Job: collect requests, connect them to customers, plan work, announce updates, answer questions, and keep the full history together
- Thesis: Featurebase is most useful as a connected loop from support question to product decision to customer update, not as a cheaper feedback board alone
- Main reason to buy: several related customer tools share one record and one interface
- Main reason to hesitate: seat and AI usage costs, imperfect AI answers, and greater dependence on one still-expanding suite
- When it is unnecessary: the existing helpdesk and feedback tools work well together, or the team only needs a basic public request board
- Decision rule: import a small real sample and take five requests through support, merging, internal review, roadmap, shipped changelog, and customer notification; test the AI on known hard questions and price a busy month
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on use or migration
