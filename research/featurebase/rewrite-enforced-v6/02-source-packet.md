## Claims

- Featurebase launched publicly as a feedback board on August 29, 2021 and has since expanded into feedback and customer support software. [verified; fact; launch, feedback-docs]
- The product now combines feedback boards, roadmaps, changelogs, surveys, a help center, support inbox, and an AI support agent. [verified; fact; pricing, feedback-docs]
- Annual seat prices are Free for one seat, $29 for Growth, $59 for Professional, and $99 for Enterprise. Month-to-month prices are $37, $75, and $129 for the paid tiers. [verified; fact; pricing-docs]
- Fibi AI costs $0.49 per resolved conversation. Paid Copilot use beyond the included allowance costs $19 per agent monthly, and whitelabeling costs $69 monthly. [verified; fact; pricing-docs]
- Combining feedback and support work, a clean interface, quick setup, customization, and responsive help are the strongest positive themes. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, product-hunt, reddit-positive]
- AI answers can be wrong and need a person to review or take over. [supported; reported-pattern; g2]
- A small group describes stability, billing, and support failures despite support being a broad positive theme. [supported; reported-pattern; trustpilot, reddit-negative, g2]
