# Featurebase spoken review brief

## What is it?

Featurebase puts product feedback and customer support in one system. Customers can submit ideas, vote, read the roadmap and updates, search help articles, or start a support conversation.

## Who will find it useful?

A software team that wants a support question and a product request to share the same customer history.

## What is good?

The interface and setup receive strong praise. Combining the feedback board, roadmap, updates, help center, and inbox can remove several handoffs. Support from Featurebase is also praised often.

## What is wrong?

AI answers still need checking. A smaller group describes stability, billing, and support failures. Per-seat prices plus AI, Copilot, email, translation, and branding charges can make the real bill higher than the headline.

## What does it cost?

Free covers one seat. With annual billing, Growth is $29 per seat, Professional $59, and Enterprise $99. Monthly billing costs more. Fibi AI is $0.49 per resolved conversation.

## Our answer

Do not test the feedback board alone. Take five real requests from support through merging, planning, a shipped update, and a customer message. Give the AI questions with known answers. Then calculate a busy month with every seat and usage charge.
