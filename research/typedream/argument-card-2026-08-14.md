# Typedream private argument card

- Buyer: a non-developer who needs a polished small website quickly
- Job: publish and maintain the real site without creating a design or development project
- Thesis: Typedream is useful when its strong defaults fit the site's needs; the same opinionated editor becomes a limit when mobile layout, search control, commerce, integrations, or ownership matter
- Main reason to buy: fast Notion-like editing with attractive results and little learning
- Main reason to hesitate: responsive limits, publishing and form risk, shallow advanced features, and difficult migration
- Decision rule: build five real pages, connect the domain, submit every form on phone and desktop, check search and sharing metadata, publish and roll back a bad change, then export or rebuild the site on a blank host
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni site
