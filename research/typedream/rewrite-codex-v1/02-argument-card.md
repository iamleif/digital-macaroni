# Argument card

- Thesis: Typedream is worthwhile when its defaults match the site and remove design work.
- Best for: polished landing pages, portfolios, creator sites, and small blogs.
- Skip: strict mobile art direction, large design systems, deep commerce, complex content, or full code ownership.
- Price anchor: Free $0; Launch $15 yearly or $20 monthly; Grow $42 yearly or $49 monthly, all per site during research.
- Decisive test: build, publish, break, restore, and migrate the real site.
- Verdict: Fast small sites work until custom control becomes essential.
