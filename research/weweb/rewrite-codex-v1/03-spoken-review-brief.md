# Spoken review brief

WeWeb visually builds the screens and actions of a web app. The data can live in WeWeb or an outside service such as Supabase or Xano.

Recommend it to a technically curious team that wants control without coding every screen. Tell someone who wants to avoid databases, sign-in, permissions, phone layouts, and debugging to use a simpler product or hire help.

Build risky parts before the dashboard and inspect the export. State that no new hands-on test was run.
