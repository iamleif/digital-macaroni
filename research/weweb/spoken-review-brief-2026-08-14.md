# WeWeb spoken review brief

## What is it?

WeWeb is a visual tool for building web app screens. You can make forms, dashboards, menus, and phone layouts, then connect them to the place that stores users and data.

## Who will find it useful?

A founder or small team that has outgrown a fixed template and is willing to learn how data, sign-in, and permissions work.

## What is good?

The screen design is flexible, it connects to outside services such as Supabase and Xano, and paid users can export the code. It also charges for builders rather than every person using the finished app.

## What is wrong?

A nice dashboard is easy to mistake for a finished product. Each customer must see only their own data. Errors need a useful screen. Phone layouts need separate care. These jobs still require technical thinking.

## What does it cost?

Building can start free. Paid seats start at $20 monthly. A custom domain, WeWeb hosting, outside backend, and more builders can add to the total.

## Our answer

Before polishing the app, build sign-in, one private customer record, one failed-data message, and one phone screen. If those steps make sense, WeWeb can be a strong tool. If they feel like an unwanted engineering course, choose a simpler builder or hire help.
