# Breakcold writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review. Digital Macaroni did not use Breakcold.
- Sources checked August 13, 2026: official site, G2, Trustpilot, Capterra, Product Hunt, and Reddit.
- Main limit: G2 has a much larger and more positive sample. Trustpilot, Product Hunt, and the AppSumo discussion contain fewer but more serious complaints. Collection methods differ.

## Reader and job

The buyer is a solo seller, founder, or small sales team that finds prospects on LinkedIn. They want to watch lead activity, start useful conversations, and keep LinkedIn and email contact in one record. If account access or billing support fails, their sales history may be difficult to reach.

## Verified product scope

Breakcold is a social-selling CRM. It brings LinkedIn activity, email work, prospect engagement, and a sales pipeline together. The evidence packet does not contain a current verified price.

## Reported patterns

- Strengths: LinkedIn-first prospecting, social feeds, combined email activity, responsive support, and the chance to replace several sales tools.
- Limits: account access, slow or absent support, renewals, refunds, and disputes over lifetime-deal update rights.
- Conflict: many customers describe helpful support, while a serious minority describe poor help during access or billing problems. Both can be true for different cases.

## Feature translation

- Social-selling CRM: a contact list that also shows what leads post and how the seller has contacted them.
- Engagement feed: a place to see lead activity that may give the seller a reason to start a conversation.
- Combined email activity: email contact remains near the same lead record as LinkedIn work.

## Missing evidence

No hands-on test covered LinkedIn connection safety, imports, exports, account recovery, support response, billing, refunds, or campaign results. No current price is safe to print.
