# Spoken review brief

Breakcold is a sales contact list built around LinkedIn. It shows activity from leads, keeps LinkedIn contact near email follow-up, and gives a small team one place to track the conversation.

That is useful when LinkedIn is part of the sales day. A founder can see that a lead posted something relevant, reply with a real thought, and keep the later email near the same record. A general CRM often treats that social step as an add-on.

The product gets strong praise for this routine. The concern comes from a smaller group of serious reports about account access, renewals, refunds, support, and old lifetime deals. The sample is too uneven to call those problems common. They still change what I would tell a friend to do.

Start with a short payment term and a small lead list. Ask support a real question, export the records, and confirm what the plan includes. Breakcold is worth trying for LinkedIn-led sales. It is easy to skip when LinkedIn is only an occasional source or when the team needs a long record of stable account controls.
