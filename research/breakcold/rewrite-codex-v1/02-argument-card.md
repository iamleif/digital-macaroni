# Private argument card

- Buyer: a small team that sells through LinkedIn every day.
- Job: notice useful lead activity, start conversations, and keep the follow-up record together.
- Thesis: Breakcold has a sharp and useful sales routine, but access and billing disputes make a short trial and clean export essential.
- Reason to buy: it treats LinkedIn activity as part of the contact record instead of a separate research chore.
- Reason to hesitate: the smaller negative sample concerns paid access, refunds, renewals, and support rather than minor feature wishes.
- Decision rule: use a short plan with a small lead list. Continue only if the LinkedIn routine saves time, exports work, and support answers a real account question.
- Evidence limit: no hands-on use and no verified current price. Campaign outcomes cannot be generalized.
