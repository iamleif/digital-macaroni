6. Breakcold lifetime-deal dispute on Reddit
   - URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/appsumo/comments/1swx4uk/breakcold_in_breach/

## Claims

- Breakcold originally launched on February 22, 2022. The date is private and used only as a publication guardrail. [verified; fact; product-hunt-223, breakcold-224, g2-225]
- LinkedIn-first prospecting, social engagement feeds and combined email activity are the clearest repeated strengths. [supported; reported-pattern; g2-225, breakcold-224, trustpilot-226]
- Many users report responsive support and see the product as a lower-cost replacement for several sales tools. [supported; reported-pattern; g2-225, trustpilot-226, product-hunt-223]
- Account access, slow or absent support, renewals, refunds and lifetime-deal update rights generate serious minority complaints. [supported; reported-pattern; trustpilot-226, reddit-228, product-hunt-223]
- G2 is strongly positive while Trustpilot and AppSumo community reports are materially more negative. Review collection methods also differ, so later scoring must preserve that gap. [supported; reported-pattern; trustpilot-226, g2-225, capterra-227]
- No Digital Macaroni hands-on test was performed. Claims about campaign results cannot be generalized from individual reviews. [supported; reported-pattern; g2-225, trustpilot-226, capterra-227]
