# Lemon Squeezy spoken review brief

## What is it?

Lemon Squeezy sells digital products and software as the merchant of record. It takes the customer's payment, handles covered sales tax and VAT, supports subscriptions and licenses, then pays the seller the net amount.

## Who will find it useful?

A small global seller that would rather pay a higher transaction fee than build tax, billing, licensing, and payment operations itself.

## What is good?

It can remove a large amount of tax and commerce work. The checkout, subscriptions, software licenses, customer portal, payment recovery, and tax handling share one system.

## What is wrong?

Store approval, risk reviews, moved payouts, and slow support form a serious recent complaint pattern. The service controls when net revenue is released. Total fees can be well above 5% plus $0.50.

## What does it cost?

Base fee is 5% plus $0.50 of the tax-inclusive order. Add 1.5% for non-US payments, 1.5% for PayPal, and 0.5% for subscriptions. Some payouts and marketing features add more.

## Our answer

Get approved before setting a launch date. Run several low-value live cases and reconcile payment, product access, tax, fee, refund, webhook, and invoice. Wait for several successful payouts and test urgent support before moving all customers or removing another payment route.
