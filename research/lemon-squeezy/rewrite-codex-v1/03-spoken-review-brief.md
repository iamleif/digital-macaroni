# Lemon Squeezy spoken review brief

Lemon Squeezy sells software on your behalf for covered transactions, handles covered sales tax or VAT, and pays you the net amount. That can remove a lot of work for a small global seller.

The tradeoff is control. Lemon Squeezy approves the store, reviews risk, and releases the money. The base fee also grows with international cards, PayPal, subscriptions, and some payout methods.

I would not set a launch date before live approval. I would test refunds and product access, keep another payment route, and wait for several full payouts before depending on it.
