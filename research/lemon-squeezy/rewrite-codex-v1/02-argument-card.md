# Lemon Squeezy argument card

## Buyer

A small global software seller that wants a merchant of record to handle covered sales tax and billing work.

## Decision

Use Lemon Squeezy only after live approval, verified product access, written fee terms, useful support, and several complete payouts.

## Why

The service can remove major tax and billing work. It also controls approval, account risk, and release of the seller's net revenue.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if approval remains unclear, total fees exceed the saved work, payout timing is too risky, support cannot own a money issue, or webhooks break product access.

## Verdict

Valuable tax help, once approvals and payouts work reliably.
