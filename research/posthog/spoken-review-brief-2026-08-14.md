# PostHog spoken review brief

## What is it?

PostHog shows what people do in a product, reconstructs sessions, controls feature rollouts, runs tests and surveys, tracks errors, and connects data.

## Who will find it useful?

A technical product team that wants to move from a broken funnel to the affected sessions, then release and measure a fix without switching between several vendors.

## What is good?

The parts share events and user context. Setup can be quick, the free allowances are large, and developers praise the documentation and product breadth.

## What is wrong?

The suite is dense. Bad event design creates noise and cost. Replay needs privacy and performance work. Self-hosting is a serious data platform job.

## What does it cost?

The free tier includes 1 million analytics events, 5,000 replays, 1 million flag requests, and other monthly allowances. Pay-as-you-go charges each product separately after its allowance and supports per-product caps.

## Our answer

Take one real feature from question to rollback. Validate the events, mask replay, release to 10%, check the test, model the bill, decide, roll back, and remove unused tracking. Adopt more PostHog only after that route helps the team make a better decision.
