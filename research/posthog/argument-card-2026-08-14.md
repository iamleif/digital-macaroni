# PostHog private argument card

- Buyer: a technical product team that wants analytics, replay, flags, and experiments to share the same user and event context
- Job: find a product problem, understand it, release a change safely, and measure the result
- Thesis: PostHog is most useful as one closed decision route, not as a collection of every product feature the team can turn on
- Main reason to buy: analytics, replay, flags, tests, surveys, and data tools can replace several services and use the same event definitions
- Main reason to hesitate: learning curve, privacy and performance work, noisy instrumentation, many billing meters, and a heavy self-hosted stack
- When it is unnecessary: the team needs only simple traffic analytics or already has a trusted product stack with shared definitions
- Decision rule: take one feature from a written question through event validation, replay masking, a 10% flag rollout, experiment checks, cost estimate, decision, rollback, and event cleanup; hand the route to a second owner
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni project or bill
