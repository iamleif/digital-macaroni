# PostHog argument card

## Buyer

A technical product team that wants analytics, replay, flags, and experiments to share one event language.

## Decision

Choose PostHog when one feature can move from trusted event to safe rollback without exposing private data.

## Why

Its integrated suite can replace several tools and the work needed to connect them. The same breadth creates more settings, costs, and ownership.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if event counts cannot match source records, replay leaks sensitive data, flags fail unsafely, the team cannot model each usage meter, or self-hosted recovery fails.

## Verdict

Powerful product tools once one decision route works.
