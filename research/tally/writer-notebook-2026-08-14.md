# Tally writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no product test
- Current pricing, fair-use, logic, and calculation pages checked on 2026-08-14
- G2: 4.9 from 124; Capterra: 4.9 from 39; Trustpilot: five
- Main limit: reviews lean toward small organizations and current regional prices differ

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a small team that needs contact, intake, signup, order, survey, or application forms
- Plain example: ask different follow-up questions based on the first answer, then send the result to Sheets
- Cost of failure: a complex route silently sends the wrong total, result, or follow-up page

## Product facts

- Editor works like typing a document
- Free includes unlimited forms and responses within fair use
- Free also includes files, payments, signatures, logic, calculations, redirects, and common integrations
- European research page showed Pro €20 and Business €65 monthly
- Tally help lists Pro at $29 for U.S. pricing
- Pro removes branding and adds custom domains, CSS, partial responses, team work, larger uploads, analytics, and 30-day history
- Business adds data-retention controls, email checks, and 90-day history

## Experience records

### Intake form changed based on an answer

- Source: official capability and repeated review pattern
- Result: irrelevant questions can be hidden and qualified leads can go to another page
- Consequence: one form can serve several simple paths without asking everyone everything

### Complex scoring needed helper fields

- Source: detailed G2 review
- Result: ranked scores, gaps, tie-breakers, and multiple outcomes required manual steps
- Consequence: the simple editor does not simplify the business rule itself

### Long calculation failed in a community build

- Source: Reddit community examples
- Result: totals or calculated fields failed until logic was split and checked
- Consequence: test every branch with known answers before public use

## Repeated patterns

- The document-like editor is unusually easy to start
- The free plan is a real product, not a tiny demo
- Common integrations cover many small-team workflows
- Logic remains approachable until the form becomes a small app
- Styling and deep enterprise controls are more limited

## Feature translation

### Conditional logic

- What it does: shows, hides, skips, calculates, or redirects based on an answer
- Main limit: many branches are easy to misread and must be tested separately

### Answer piping

- What it does: repeats an earlier answer later in the form or message
- Example: greeting a person by name or showing a running total

### Partial submission

- What it does: saves what someone entered before they finished
- Main limit: it is a paid Pro feature and may collect sensitive incomplete data

## Conflict ledger

- Tally is simple to operate, but the process being modeled may still be complicated.
- “Unlimited” is subject to fair use and custom pricing at very high volume.

## Spoken language bank

- type the form like a document
- ask the next question only when it applies
- free is enough for most normal forms
- test the hardest branch first
