# Tally spoken review brief

## What is it?

Tally is a form builder that feels like typing a document. Add a question, type the words, and keep going. It can also hide questions, calculate totals, take payments, and send answers to other tools.

## Who will find it useful?

Almost any small team that needs a contact form, client intake, signup, survey, application, order, or simple quiz.

## What is good?

Free covers almost every normal job, including unlimited forms and responses within fair use. The editor is quick, and the useful logic is available without an upgrade.

## What is wrong?

A form with many scores, tie-breakers, and routes is still a small app. Tally can build it, but the rules become manual and each path must be tested.

## What does it cost?

Free is enough for most people. The current European page showed Pro at €20 and Business at €65 monthly. U.S. help lists Pro at $29, so check the local price. Pro is mainly for branding, domains, team work, partial responses, and analytics.

## Our answer

Start with Free. Build the hardest part of the form first and test every result with known answers. Upgrade only when branding, domain, team, analytics, or unfinished-response needs justify it.
