It positions itself as a lighter alternative to traditional form builders by emphasizing simplicity, ease of use, and a free tier with generous limits. The product's core strengths lie in its intuitive interface, which mimics a document editor, and its ability to handle basic form creation and automation with minimal setup. Tally's free plan includes unlimited forms and submissions, along with features like logic, calculations, and integrations, making it a compelling option for users with straightforward needs. The product's pricing page lists Pro at €20 monthly and Business at €65 in Europe, while U.S. users see $29 for Pro. These regional differences highlight the importance of checking local pricing before committing. Tally's fair-use policy outlines thresholds for heavy usage, including 50,000 monthly submissions, 100GB of uploads, and 50,000 notifications, which can necessitate a custom plan for larger teams. The product's onboarding process is straightforward, aided by the free plan and a clean, document-like editor. However, advanced logic and deep customization become more challenging as forms grow in complexity, leading to a noticeable gap between basic and advanced use cases. Support is praised in reviews, though this review did not test it directly. Tally's overall score of 8.7 reflects its effectiveness for most small teams, but its limitations in handling complex workflows suggest it may not be suitable for enterprise-level needs. The review did not conduct hands-on testing, and the available data is skewed toward smaller organizations, which may affect the generalizability of the findings. Tally's success appears to be rooted in its simplicity and accessibility, making it an attractive option for users looking to avoid the complexity of traditional form builders."
