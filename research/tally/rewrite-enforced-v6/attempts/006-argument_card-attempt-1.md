  "content": "Tally is a no-code form builder that simplifies data collection for small teams. Its clean interface, generous free tier, and integration capabilities make it an attractive option for users needing basic form functionality. The free plan allows unlimited forms and submissions, including payments, signatures, and file uploads, which is a major advantage for teams without advanced needs. Onboarding is straightforward, with a document-like editor that feels intuitive for first-time users. However, Tally's handling of complex logic and customization is limited. As forms grow in complexity, users report that advanced features become more manual or constrained, which may hinder scalability. Pricing is also inconsistent, with European regional prices listed differently from U.S. pricing. Support is generally praised, but this review did not test it directly. Tally is a strong choice for small teams seeking simplicity, but its limitations make it less suitable for enterprise-level requirements."
