# Tally argument card

- **Buyer:** A small team that needs useful forms without paying before the process is proven.
- **Job:** Collect answers, change questions based on responses, calculate simple results, and send data to another tool.
- **Thesis:** Tally is the best starting point for most normal form jobs because Free is generous and the editor stays out of the way; complex decision systems still need careful testing.
- **Reason to buy:** It includes logic, calculations, files, payments, and integrations without turning the first form into a software project.
- **Reason to hesitate:** Many scoring rules, tie-breakers, or routes become manual and easy to break.
- **Decision rule:** Build the hardest branch on Free and run a table of known answers through every outcome before considering Pro.
- **Evidence limit:** Digital Macaroni did not test it, and the unusually positive review scores come mostly from smaller organizations.
