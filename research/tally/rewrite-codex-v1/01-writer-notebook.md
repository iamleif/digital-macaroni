# Tally writer's notebook

Research-based review with no new hands-on test. Eight sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026, while the article frontmatter carries six. Tally is a document-like form builder with files, payments, signatures, calculations, branching, redirects, webhooks, and common integrations.

The buyer is a small team making contact, intake, application, survey, signup, or simple order forms. The clean editor and unusually broad Free plan are the strengths. Complex hidden rules, deep customization, fair-use boundaries, regional pricing, high-volume files, and business-critical routing are the concerns.

The recommendation is strong for ordinary forms. Build the hardest branch first, write an answer table, and run every route. Use a real app when the decision becomes too complex to explain and test inside the form.
