# Tally argument card

## Buyer

A small team that wants to build useful forms quickly without paying before the process is proven.

## Decision

Choose Tally for ordinary forms and a few branches; leave when hidden rules become hard to explain and test.

## Why

The editor feels like writing a document and Free includes unusually broad form features. Complex logic eventually removes that simplicity.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if the hardest branch needs brittle helper fields, important routes cannot be tested, heavy use crosses fair-use limits, or the form is acting like a custom application.

## Verdict

The best free form builder until logic gets complicated.
