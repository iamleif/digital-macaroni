# Tally spoken review brief

Tally is one of the easiest ways to make a form. You type the page like a document and add the answer fields where they belong.

The Free plan is the bigger reason to choose it. Normal teams get forms, responses, files, payments, signatures, logic, calculations, and connections without paying.

I would build the hardest route first. Write down the answers and expected result, then test every branch. If nobody can explain the hidden rules, the process needs an app.
