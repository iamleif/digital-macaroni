# OpenStatus writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on monitor, alert, incident, subscriber, status page, self-hosting, export, or support test. Nine sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. OpenStatus combines service checks, team alerts, incident updates, and public status pages. Its code is open and can be self-hosted.

The buyer is a technical team that wants monitoring and customer communication together. Open code, self-hosting, developer tools, and the combined product are the main strengths. Long-term customer evidence and support history are too thin for a broad reliability claim.

The recommendation requires a forced-failure drill on a disposable endpoint. Test a stopped service, wrong content, slow replies, one failed region, one silent alert route, customer updates, recovery, and an outage of the monitor itself. Self-host outside the systems being watched.
