OpenStatus is a developer tool that offers monitoring, status pages, and alerting capabilities. It was launched on July 31, 2023, and is available as a hosted service or self-hosted option. The product includes features such as service checks, incident updates, and multiple alert channels. It also supports status pages with customizable components and notification settings. Pricing starts at $30 per month for the Hobby plan, which includes one monitor and one status page with three components at a minimum ten-minute check interval. Higher-tier plans offer more monitors, status pages, and advanced features like white-labeling and custom branding.
