# OpenStatus spoken review brief

## What is it?

OpenStatus checks whether websites and services work. When something fails, it alerts the team and can show customers a public page with the affected parts and incident updates.

## Who will find it useful?

A technical team that wants monitoring and a status page together, especially if it values open-source code, self-hosting, an API, a command-line tool, or Terraform.

## What is good?

The monitor and customer message are part of the same route. Checks can run from several regions, and the product has strong developer tooling.

## What is wrong?

There are very few independent long-term customer reviews. Self-hosting adds work and can fail with the product it watches if placed badly. Add-ons can be expensive.

## What does it cost?

Hobby is free. Starter is $30 monthly, Pro is $100, and Scale is $500. Annual billing gives two months free. White-labeling, identity controls, IP restrictions, and extra pages may cost more.

## Our answer

Break a disposable service. Check the time to detect, page the right person, update customers, and show recovery. Also create a false regional failure. Keep OpenStatus only if it tells the truth before customers have to report the problem.
