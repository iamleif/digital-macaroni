Senja is a tool that helps businesses collect, organize, and display customer testimonials in a clear and professional way. It allows users to gather text and video reviews through a shareable form, import existing testimonials, and then arrange them into projects. These projects can be embedded on websites or shared publicly. The free version includes 15 testimonials, one form, and basic features, while paid plans offer more capacity and advanced tools like custom branding and integrations with other platforms. Senja is particularly useful for teams that need to manage testimonials across multiple channels without a dedicated content creator. However, its value depends on having a clear plan for which testimonials to collect and how to present them. If a team lacks criteria for selecting quality feedback or needs more advanced customization, Senja may not be the best fit. Some users praise its simplicity and ease of use, while others report issues with integration, support, and pricing. A friend should check whether they have a defined set of testimonials to showcase and whether they need more advanced features beyond what the free version offers before deciding to pay.
