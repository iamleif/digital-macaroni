## Claims

- Senja collects text and video testimonials through a shareable form, imports existing proof, organizes it, and displays it in website widgets or public pages. [verified; fact; official-pricing, official-limits]
- Free includes 15 total text and video testimonials, one form, one project, one seat, Senja branding, and unlimited widgets. [verified; fact; official-pricing, official-limits]
- After the 15th testimonial, forms still accept submissions but later testimonials stay hidden until the account upgrades. [verified; fact; official-over-limit]
- Starter is $29 monthly with unlimited testimonials, three forms, two seats, custom branding, HD export, auto-sync, Zapier, API, and webhooks. Pro is $59 with unlimited forms, five projects, and five seats. [verified; fact; official-pricing, official-limits]
- Easy collection, a simple interface, organization, and website display are repeated strengths. [supported; reported-pattern; product-hunt, g2]
- Pricing, some imports or integrations, and finding the right testimonial are reported limits. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, reddit]
- Login failures, missing testimonials, and poor support appear in a five-review Trustpilot sample and should be treated as test and backup warnings, not a normal outcome. [limited; reported-pattern; trustpilot]
