# Senja writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no product test
- Current pricing and 15-testimonial behavior checked on 2026-08-14
- G2 shows 4.6 from 20; Trustpilot shows 3.1 from five
- Main limit: several G2 reviews are incentivized and the negative sample is tiny

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a small business with customer praise scattered through email, social posts, and calls
- Plain job: ask for a testimonial, keep it in one place, and put selected quotes or videos on the website
- Cost of failure: collecting vague praise that looks nice but tells the next buyer nothing

## Product facts

- Shareable form accepts text and video
- Existing testimonials can be imported from other sites
- Tags and search help find a quote
- Widgets place selected testimonials on a website
- Free holds 15 testimonials, one form, project, and user, with Senja branding
- Later submissions are hidden after 15 until upgrade
- Starter is $29 monthly; Pro is $59; annual billing is 17% lower
- There is no timed paid trial

## Experience records

### Customer recorded a video from one link

- Source: G2
- Result: users value sending a simple link instead of arranging and hosting video files by hand
- Consequence: less work for the customer can improve the chance of a response

### Existing praise became a website block

- Source: Product Hunt and G2
- Result: users organize quotes and place them in a ready-made website widget
- Consequence: useful when proof is already scattered

### Account or testimonial could not be reached

- Source: Trustpilot, five-review sample
- Result: complaints mention login trouble, disappearing reviews, and weak support
- Consequence: export original files and text instead of treating Senja as the only copy

## Repeated patterns

- Collection forms and embeds are easy to set up
- Organization becomes useful once testimonials are scattered
- Paid price feels high to some freelancers and rises when more projects or seats are needed
- The software improves handling, not the quality or truth of the testimonial

## Feature translation

### Collection form

- What it does: gives customers one page to type or record a testimonial
- Main limit: vague questions produce vague answers

### Widget

- What it does: places selected quotes or videos in a block on the website
- Main limit: too many quotes can become noise

### Wall of Love

- What it does: creates a shareable page containing many testimonials
- Main limit: visitors still need proof relevant to their own problem

## Conflict ledger

- Senja reduces the work of collecting proof, but asking only happy customers can still create a selected picture.
- Free is generous for testing, but a growing library crosses the 15-item limit quickly.

## Missing evidence

- No hands-on form completion test
- No website speed or embed test
- No recovery test for a missing testimonial

## Spoken language bank

- send one link and let the customer type or record
- keep the original video somewhere else
- a nice grid does not fix a vague quote
- ask what changed, not whether they liked it
- pay when 15 real testimonials are no longer enough
