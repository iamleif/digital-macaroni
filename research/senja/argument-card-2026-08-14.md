# Senja argument card

- **Buyer:** A small business with customer praise scattered across different places.
- **Job:** Collect specific text or video testimonials, organize them, and place selected proof on a website.
- **Thesis:** Senja makes testimonial handling easy, but the result is only useful when the questions produce specific answers.
- **Reason to buy:** One link handles text and video collection, while widgets remove the work of designing a proof section.
- **Reason to hesitate:** Free stops at 15 testimonials, Starter is $29, and a tiny review sample reports missing content and login problems.
- **Decision rule:** Collect five new testimonials with one focused question, export the originals, and publish one widget before paying.
- **Evidence limit:** No Digital Macaroni test was performed, and both positive incentives and a tiny negative sample limit confidence.
