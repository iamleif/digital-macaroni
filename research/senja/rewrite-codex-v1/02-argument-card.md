# Senja argument card

## Buyer

A small business with customer praise scattered across email, social posts, calls, and support tools.

## Decision

Choose Senja when a focused question produces useful proof and every original remains backed up elsewhere.

## Why

The form, library, and website widgets remove collection and display work. They cannot turn vague praise into a convincing customer result.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if customers abandon the form, important imports fail, testimonials are hard to find, exports lose originals, or the paid price exceeds the value of the proof collected.

## Verdict

Easy testimonial collection still depends on asking useful questions.
