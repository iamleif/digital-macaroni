# Senja spoken review brief

Senja gives customers one link where they can write or record a testimonial. The business can organize those answers and place selected proof on its website.

That removes useful handling work. It cannot fix a weak question. A polished row of people saying the product is great gives the next buyer almost nothing.

I would start with five customers and ask what changed after the purchase. Then I would test the live website block, save the permission, export every original, and keep another copy.
