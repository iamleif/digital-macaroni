# Senja spoken review brief

## What is it?

Senja gives customers one link where they can type or record a testimonial. The business can keep the responses in one place and put selected quotes or videos on its website.

## Who will find it useful?

A small business that already receives praise in emails, social posts, and calls but rarely turns it into clear website proof.

## What is good?

The form, tags, imports, and website blocks solve one focused job. A customer does not need to schedule a call or send a large video file.

## What is wrong?

Senja cannot make vague praise useful. The question must draw out the old problem, what changed, and a real result. A tiny Trustpilot sample also makes backup and export worth testing.

## What does it cost?

Free holds 15 testimonials. Starter is $29 monthly with unlimited testimonials and no Senja branding. Pro is $59 for more projects, forms, and seats. There is no timed paid trial.

## Our answer

Use Free to collect five specific testimonials and publish one widget. Keep original videos and text outside Senja. Upgrade when the 15-item limit blocks a real library, not before.
