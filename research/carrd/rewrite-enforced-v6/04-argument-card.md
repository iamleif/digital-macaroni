Carrd is a one-page website builder designed for simplicity and affordability. It is ideal for small business owners, creative professionals, or beginners who need a clean, single-page site for a portfolio, service page, or contact form. The Pro Lite plan starts at $9 yearly for three sites, while the Pro Standard and Pro Plus plans offer more features at higher prices. Users praise its ease of use and clean templates, but it lacks support for blogs, multi-page navigation, complex selling, or advanced databases. The tool is intentionally limited to one-page structures, making it excellent for straightforward projects but unsuitable for more complex needs. The main conflict is that simplicity is both its strength and its limitation. Carrd excels at what it does but cannot grow beyond its single-page focus. Its low price and ease of use make it a strong recommendation for one-page sites, but not for multi-page or content-heavy projects.
