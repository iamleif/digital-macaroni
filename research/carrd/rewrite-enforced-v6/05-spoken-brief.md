Carrd is a one-page website builder that focuses on simplicity and affordability. It’s best suited for small business owners, creative professionals, or beginners who need a clean, single-page site for a portfolio, service page, or contact form. The Pro Lite plan starts at $9 per year for three sites, while the Pro Standard and Pro Plus plans offer more features at higher prices. Users appreciate its ease of use and clean templates, but it lacks support for blogs, multi-page navigation, complex selling, or advanced databases. Carrd is intentionally limited to one-page structures, making it excellent for straightforward projects but unsuitable for more complex needs. Its low price and ease of use make it a strong recommendation for one-page sites, but not for multi-page or content-heavy projects. A few customers noted that while Carrd works well for static, simple sites, it doesn’t scale with growing needs. A friend should check whether their project truly requires a single-page structure before committing. If they need a blog, multiple pages, or advanced features, Carrd may not be the right choice.
