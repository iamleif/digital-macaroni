# Carrd writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no product build
- Current plans, sections, domains, and forms checked 2026-08-14
- G2: 33 reviews; Reddit: 41 sampled voices across focused and broad communities
- Main limit: the largest writing sample comes from communities rather than major review platforms

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a beginner, freelancer, creator, or small business that needs a profile, portfolio, event page, waitlist, or simple landing page
- Plain example: one page explains the offer, shows examples, answers the main questions, and ends with a contact or signup form
- Cost of failure: the site later needs dozens of articles or products and must be rebuilt elsewhere

## Product facts

- Free supports up to three sites on Carrd addresses with branding and limits
- Pro Lite: $9 yearly, three sites, no branding, premium design features, no custom domain
- Pro Standard: $19 yearly, ten sites, custom domains, forms, widgets, embeds, analytics, and meta tags
- Pro Plus: $49 yearly, 25 sites, advanced forms, redirects, password protection, downloads, and deeper settings
- Sections can switch visible areas and imitate pages, but the site remains one page

## Experience records

### A simple site was published quickly

- Source: repeated G2 and community pattern
- Action: choose a template, replace text and images, add a button or form, and publish
- Result: a clean mobile-friendly page without learning a large website system
- Consequence: strong fit for a narrow job with fixed content

### A one-page site tried to become a content site

- Source: repeated community and review limit
- Result: sections and links could imitate navigation, but articles, categories, search pages, and regular publishing remained awkward
- Consequence: choose another builder if content growth is part of the plan

## Feature translation

### Section

- What it does: hides and shows different parts of the same page when a visitor clicks a link
- Main limit: it does not create a normal page with its own full address and content system

### Custom domain

- What it does: publishes the site at a domain the owner buys elsewhere
- Plan: Pro Standard or higher

### Embed

- What it does: adds outside tools or custom code to the page
- Main limit: several embeds can turn a simple page into a fragile collection of outside services

## Conflict ledger

- Carrd's narrow editor is easier because it does less.
- It can mimic several pages, but that does not remove the content and search limits of one page.
- The annual price is tiny, but rebuilding later can cost more than choosing the right structure now.
