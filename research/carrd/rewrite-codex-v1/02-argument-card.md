# Private argument card

- Buyer: a person or small business that needs one focused page.
- Job: publish a clear message and one next step without learning a large website system.
- Thesis: Carrd is the best cheap builder for a true one-page site, and the wrong foundation for content that will grow into many pages.
- Reason to buy: fast setup, clean templates, and unusually low yearly prices.
- Reason to hesitate: sections can hide the one-page structure, which tempts buyers to force a blog or larger site into the wrong tool.
- Decision rule: write the full site outline first. Choose Carrd only if it still makes sense as one page a year from now.
- Evidence limit: no hands-on build or form test.
