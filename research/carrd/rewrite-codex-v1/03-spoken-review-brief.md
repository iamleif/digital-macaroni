# Spoken review brief

Carrd is a very good tool for one simple website page. Pick a template, replace the words and images, add a form or button, and publish. It is cheap enough that a freelancer can run several small pages without much cost.

The one-page limit is the whole decision. A portfolio, event page, waitlist, or short business page can fit well. A blog, product catalog, large help area, or site with user accounts cannot. Carrd sections may look like separate pages, but they are still parts of one page.

Most businesses need Pro Standard because it is the first plan with a custom domain and forms. The $19 yearly price is excellent. It should not persuade someone to build the wrong kind of site.

I would tell a friend to write the site outline and the next year of planned changes before choosing. If everything supports one message and one action, use Carrd. If new articles or service pages will arrive each month, choose a normal multi-page builder.
