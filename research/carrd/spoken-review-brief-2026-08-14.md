# Carrd spoken review brief

## What is it?

Carrd builds simple one-page websites. Pick a template, replace the words and images, add a button or form, and publish. The page adjusts for phones without much work.

## Who will find it useful?

Anyone who needs a profile, portfolio, event page, waitlist, link page, or landing page. It is especially good for beginners and people running several small sites.

## What is good?

It stays focused, looks clean, and costs very little. Pro Standard is $19 a year for ten sites with custom domains and forms.

## What is wrong?

It is still a one-page builder. Sections can look like separate pages, but they do not turn Carrd into a blog or a normal content system. Stores, databases, user accounts, and app behavior need another tool.

## What does it cost?

Free handles three basic sites. Lite is $9 a year but does not include custom domains. Standard is $19 for ten sites and adds the normal business needs. Plus is $49 for 25 sites and advanced forms and controls.

## Our answer

Write the site outline before opening Carrd. If one page can explain the subject, show proof, answer the main questions, and give one next step, use Carrd. If the plan includes regular articles, many products, or separate topics that should rank in search, start somewhere else.
