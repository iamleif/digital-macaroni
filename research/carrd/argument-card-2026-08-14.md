# Carrd private argument card

- Buyer: someone who needs a simple online page, often with little website experience
- Job: explain one person, offer, product, event, or idea and give the visitor one clear next step
- Thesis: Carrd is excellent when the entire site genuinely fits on one page; its low price does not make it suitable for a future blog, store, or web app
- Main reason to buy: a clean responsive page is fast to build and costs very little each year
- Main reason to hesitate: one-page structure limits content growth and search strategy
- When it is unnecessary: the site needs regular articles, many products, accounts, databases, or complex actions
- Decision rule: write the complete page outline first; if it needs more than one main subject, repeated article publishing, or a product catalog, choose another builder
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on build
