Encharge's pricing starts at $99 per month for the Growth plan and $159 for the Premium plan, with annual discounts available. Both plans scale with the number of subscribers, and some advanced features like API events, event-based segments, and transactional emails are only available with the Premium tier. The visual flow builder and automation range are frequently cited as strengths, with users reporting ease of use. However, there are also reported limitations, including a learning curve, limited template options, and challenges with event filtering and deliverability setup. Some older workflow complaints may no longer apply due to product updates, but the company has faced criticism for offering feature extensions in exchange for honest G2 reviews, which raises questions about the authenticity of high ratings. The overall score of 7.8 reflects the product's value for businesses with clear event-driven messaging needs, though its high starting price and premium feature requirements may make it less suitable for smaller newsletters or general email campaigns. Encharge is best suited for software companies or businesses with clear, behavior-based communication goals."
