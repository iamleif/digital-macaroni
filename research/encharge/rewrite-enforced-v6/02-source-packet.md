## Claims

- Encharge builds automated email paths from triggers, filters, and actions on a visual canvas. [verified; fact; official-flows]
- Product actions such as signing up or creating a task can trigger email, but the app must send those events to Encharge through an API, JavaScript tracking, or an integration. [verified; fact; official-events]
- Growth starts at $99 monthly and Premium at $159 monthly for 2,000 subscribers. Annual starting prices are $79 and $129 per month. Pricing rises with subscriber count. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- API events, event-based segments, transactional email, HubSpot, Salesforce, Segment, Shopify, and WooCommerce require Premium. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- The visual flow builder, automation range, and relative ease are repeated strengths. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, trustpilot, capterra]
- Learning curve, templates, integrations, missing features, event filtering, and deliverability setup appear as limitations. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, reddit]
- Review totals are large, but the founder disclosed a feature-extension offer tied to honest G2 reviews, so high ratings should not be read as an uninfluenced random sample. [verified; conflict; launch-record, g2, trustpilot]
