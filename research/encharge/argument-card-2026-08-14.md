# Encharge argument card

- **Buyer:** A software company that wants emails to react to customer actions.
- **Job:** Send onboarding, sales, and retention emails when a person does or does not do something in the product.
- **Thesis:** Encharge makes complex email paths easier to see, but the useful SaaS setup still needs good product data and often the Premium plan.
- **Reason to buy:** Its visual builder turns triggers, choices, waits, and emails into one readable map.
- **Reason to hesitate:** Growth begins at $99, while product events and several key integrations require the $159 Premium starting tier.
- **Decision rule:** Write down three real customer actions and the exact emails they should cause; buy only if Encharge can receive those actions on the chosen plan.
- **Evidence limit:** Digital Macaroni did not test it, and review incentives reduce confidence in the very high public scores.
