# Encharge spoken review brief

## What is it?

Encharge sends email based on what a customer does. A software company can send help when a trial user signs up but never creates a project, or send an upgrade message after someone uses a feature several times.

## Who will find it useful?

A software business with clear trial, setup, and payment steps. A normal newsletter probably does not need this much setup or expense.

## What is good?

The flow builder shows the whole path as boxes on a page. It is easier to spot a missing wait, wrong branch, or repeated email.

## What is wrong?

The customer actions still have to reach Encharge through tracking code, an API, or an integration. Product events and several major integrations also require Premium.

## What does it cost?

For 2,000 subscribers, Growth starts at $99 monthly and Premium at $159. Annual starting prices are $79 and $129 per month. A 14-day trial is available.

## Our answer

Write three messages tied to three real customer actions. Ask a developer how those actions will reach Encharge. If that works on the selected plan, test the complete path during the trial. Do not buy it just to send a newsletter.
