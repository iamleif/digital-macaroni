# Outrank argument card

## Buyer

A small website owner considering automated SEO research, articles, images, internal links, and link exchange.

## Decision

Skip a paid commitment until a controlled trial proves content quality, useful traffic, link control, price, and support.

## Why

The feature list could remove a lot of content work. The evidence packet contains no accessible customer accounts or independent outcome data.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if drafts need heavy factual repair, published pages gain no useful search visits, exchanged links cannot be reviewed or removed, pricing stays unclear, support lacks ownership, or exports are incomplete.

## Verdict

Skip until traffic, links, pricing, and support prove credible.
