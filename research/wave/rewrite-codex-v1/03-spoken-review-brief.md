# Spoken review brief

Wave puts basic accounting, invoices, and payments together for a freelancer or very small business. The bookkeeping and invoice route can save money and keep cash records clear.

The payment risk changes the recommendation. Detailed accounts include thousands of dollars held or returned with weak explanations. Use Wave for books and invoices only after a real trial, and keep another way to collect money.

State the evidence limits and avoid guessing current plan details.
