# Argument card

- Thesis: Wave can be useful for basic books and invoices, but its payment route needs a cautious trial and a backup.
- Best for: freelancers and micro-businesses with simple records.
- Skip: businesses that cannot tolerate held funds or slow payment support.
- Decisive test: import one month, send invoices, reconcile records, process small payments, refund one, contact support, and export everything.
- Verdict: Useful basic accounting, risky as the only payment route.
