## Spoken Brief
- Wave is a free accounting and invoicing app aimed at freelancers and small businesses.
- It lets you track income and expenses, send invoices, and accept payments.
- Invoicing works reliably; users say it’s easy to set up and follow up on unpaid bills.
- The payment side can freeze funds without warning, and accounts have been disabled for unclear reasons.
- Support is slow and often limited to AI chatbots.
- Good for solo operators who need basic bookkeeping and can tolerate payment‑hold risk; not good for businesses that depend on steady cash flow.
- Before paying, test the payment flow and see how quickly support responds to a problem.
