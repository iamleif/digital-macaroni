## Argument Card
**Product**: Wave – free accounting, invoicing, and payment platform for freelancers and small businesses.
**Judgment**: Works well for basic bookkeeping and invoicing, but payment‑hold risk and poor support limit its suitability for cash‑flow‑sensitive operations.
**Key Evidence**:
- Official site states unified accounting, invoicing, payments.
- Free core features funded by ads (release‑history).
- Positive: Speaker Labs used Wave invoicing for 8 years with no unpaid invoices.
- Negative: Trustpilot users report $4,500 and $15,000 payments held, accounts disabled, and unresponsive support.
**Recommendation**: Try Wave if you need a zero‑cost bookkeeping tool and can tolerate occasional payment issues; keep a backup payment method for critical transactions.
**Scores**: Onboarding 8, Product 7, Support 5, Billing 8.
