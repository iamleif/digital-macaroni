  "content": "Pirsch Analytics is a privacy-first analytics tool designed for businesses and marketers looking to track website traffic and user behavior without relying on cookies. The product is positioned as an alternative to traditional analytics platforms like Google Analytics, with a focus on compliance with data protection regulations, particularly in the European Union. The company, Pirsch Analytics, claims to offer browser and server-side tracking, event tracking, goal setting, funnel analysis, and reporting features. The product is hosted in Germany, and it supports both self-hosted and cloud-based deployment models, though the full self-hosted version requires an Enterprise agreement. Pirsch offers three pricing tiers: Standard, Plus, and Enterprise, with the Standard plan starting at $6 per month for up to 10,000 monthly page views. A 30-day trial is available without requiring a credit card. The product is marketed as cookie-free, which may help with compliance, but it does not automatically mean that no consent is required, as this depends on the legal context and implementation. The tool is described as having a clean dashboard and quick setup, with a focus on simplicity and developer-friendly options. However, some users report limitations in complex filtering, deeper analysis, and paid-media attribution compared to larger analytics platforms. The product is open-source in its core, but full self-hosting is not available for free. The company has received mixed reviews, with some praising its affordability and ease of use, while others note its limitations in advanced analytics capabilities. The product's overall score is 8.1, with onboarding and product features rated higher than support and billing processes. The review highlights that Pirsch is worth testing for teams seeking a simpler, EU-hosted analytics solution, but a side-by-side comparison with existing tools is recommended to understand the trade-offs before switching."
