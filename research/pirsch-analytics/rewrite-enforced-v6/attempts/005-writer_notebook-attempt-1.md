  "content": "Pirsch Analytics is a privacy-focused analytics tool that launched its public beta in December 2020. It is designed for businesses and developers seeking a simpler, EU-hosted alternative to traditional analytics platforms. The product is hosted in Germany and offers both browser and server-side tracking, along with features such as events, goals, funnels, reports, APIs, SDKs, webhooks, and export capabilities. It supports three pricing tiers: Standard, Plus, and Enterprise, with Standard starting at $6 per month for 10,000 monthly page views. A 30-day trial is available without a credit card. Custom events and 10% of session-extension events are counted toward the monthly page-view limit. The open-source core is available on GitHub, though a fully supported self-hosted version requires an Enterprise agreement. The product is marketed as cookie-free and privacy-compliant, though this does not automatically resolve all legal or implementation concerns around consent and data handling. The overall score for Pirsch is 8.1, with onboarding and billing rated highly, while support and product depth receive slightly lower marks. The strongest reported themes include a clean dashboard, quick setup, cookie-free tracking, and low entry price. However, some users note that complex filters, deeper analysis, and paid-media attribution may be limited compared to larger analytics platforms. The product is best suited for teams looking for a simpler, EU-hosted traffic and conversion dashboard. Testing alongside the current tool is recommended to understand important differences before switching. Several limitations remain, including no hands-on testing of tracking, consent, attribution, or support, and the small sample size of independent reviews. The open core is real, but a full self-hosted version is not freely available. Privacy and consent requirements depend on implementation and applicable law, so users should carefully consider their specific needs before adopting Pirsch Analytics."
