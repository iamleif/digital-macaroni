# Pirsch Analytics spoken review brief

Pirsch is a good fit when a small team wants to open one dashboard and understand its traffic. It covers pages, sources, events, goals, and funnels. Developers can also send trusted events from the server.

The clean view does not make the numbers correct by itself. Browser blockers, payment retries, bots, and duplicate events can all change the result. Paid ad platforms may also credit sales differently.

I would run Pirsch beside the current tool for one full campaign. Compare both with real signups and orders. Keep it when a second person can answer the weekly questions and explain the gaps.
