# Pirsch Analytics argument card

## Buyer

A small company that wants private, readable website analytics and can define a short list of useful events.

## Decision

Choose Pirsch when aggregate traffic, goals, and funnels answer the weekly questions and the team can verify them against real orders.

## Why

Pirsch combines a clean dashboard with browser and server collection. It is easier to read than a broad marketing suite, though its numbers still depend on careful event design.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if paid-media attribution drives the business, core reports require spreadsheet rebuilding, browser and server events duplicate conversions, or monthly event volume makes the price hard to predict.

## Verdict

Simple private analytics once your sales numbers agree.
