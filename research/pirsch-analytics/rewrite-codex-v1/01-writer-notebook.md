# Pirsch Analytics writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on tracking, consent, funnel, import, export, billing, or support test. Nine sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Pirsch is a German-hosted, cookie-free web analytics product with browser and server collection, events, goals, funnels, imports, exports, APIs, and SDKs.

The buyer is a small company that wants understandable traffic and conversion reports without the weight of a large analytics suite. The clean dashboard, low entry price, developer controls, and privacy approach are the main strengths. Paid-media attribution, advanced analysis, event counting, and the limited public review sample are the main concerns.

The recommendation is to trial Pirsch beside the current analytics tool for one real campaign and purchase cycle. Compare both products with the order database. Keep Pirsch only if another team member can answer the weekly questions and explain the differences.
