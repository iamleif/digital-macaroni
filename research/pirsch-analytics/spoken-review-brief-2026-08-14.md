# Pirsch Analytics spoken review brief

## What is it?

Pirsch shows website traffic, sources, pages, events, goals, funnels, and revenue in a simpler dashboard. It is hosted in Germany and can collect data from a browser script or the website's own server.

## Who will find it useful?

A small team that wants to answer ordinary traffic and conversion questions without operating a large Google Analytics setup.

## What is good?

The dashboard is clean, the entry price is low, tracking can be cookie-free, and developers can use APIs and SDKs. Plus includes funnels, tests, segments, and agency branding.

## What is wrong?

Deep filters and paid-ad attribution may be too light. The public rating sample is tiny. The open-source core is not a free complete self-hosted application.

## What does it cost?

At 10,000 monthly page views, Standard is $6 per month and Plus is $12. The trial lasts 30 days without a card. Events also use the monthly allowance.

## Our answer

Run Pirsch beside the current analytics system through a complete campaign and purchase cycle. Compare both with the real orders. Keep it only when the team can explain the differences and still answer its five most important weekly questions.
