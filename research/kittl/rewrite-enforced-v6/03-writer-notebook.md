# Kittl writer's notebook

- Buyer: a small shop or creator producing brand, social, or merch graphics.
- Job: move from an idea to a usable design without building every asset from scratch.
- Best evidence: templates, vectors, mockups, background removal, AI models, and a usable free tier.
- Main risks: thin outside evidence, output quality, licenses, and unknown support quality.
- Trial rule: finish one paid-use design and inspect every export outside Kittl.
