# Fathom Analytics writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on install, traffic comparison, event, export, or support test. Eight sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Fathom is a hosted privacy-focused website analytics service with open-source roots. It shows common traffic, campaign, and event data in one main view.

The buyer is a small business, publisher, agency, or content team that wants ordinary website answers without a large analytics system. A clear dashboard, fast understanding, privacy, and simple setup are the strongest patterns. Deep segments, custom reports, detailed behavior analysis, and traffic-based pricing are the limits.

Plans started at $15 monthly for 100,000 pageviews and events, with at least 50 sites. Events count toward the same allowance. The recommendation should rest on rebuilding real reports beside the current tool, not on simplicity alone.
