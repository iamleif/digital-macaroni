# Fathom Analytics spoken review brief

Fathom gives you a short dashboard for visits, popular pages, traffic sources, campaigns, and actions such as signups or sales. It is good when those are the questions your team actually asks.

The simple view is also the limit. It will not replace deep product funnels or a detailed study of one customer's path. Privacy is a strength, but the other scripts and forms on the site still need their own check.

I would run Fathom beside the current tool for two weeks. Rebuild two real events and two important reports. Keep it only if the missing detail would not change a decision.
