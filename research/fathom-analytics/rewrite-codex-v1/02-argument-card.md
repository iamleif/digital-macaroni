# Fathom Analytics argument card

## Buyer

A small team that needs clear traffic, campaign, and event answers across one or several websites.

## Decision

Choose Fathom when its short dashboard covers the questions that lead to action. Keep a deeper tool when a missing report changes product or spending decisions.

## Why

Fathom makes common answers easy to find and uses a privacy-focused tracking model. The same simplicity limits deep segments, customer paths, and custom analysis.

## Walk-away conditions

Skip it if a key report cannot be rebuilt, event volume makes pricing poor, or the team needs detailed product behavior.

## Verdict

Simple private analytics that skip deeper visitor behavior.
