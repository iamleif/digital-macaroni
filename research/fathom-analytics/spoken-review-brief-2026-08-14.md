# Fathom Analytics spoken review brief

## What is it?

Fathom tells you how people find and use a website. It focuses on a clear traffic dashboard rather than the many menus and reports found in Google Analytics.

## Who will find it useful?

A business that mainly needs visits, popular pages, traffic sources, campaigns, countries, devices, and a few important actions such as signups or sales.

## What is good?

The main screen is quick to read, setup is light, and privacy is built into the product's approach. One plan can cover many sites.

## What is wrong?

The same simple design leaves less room for detailed segments, custom reports, behavior paths, and deeper product analysis. There is no permanent free hosted plan, and events count toward the traffic limit.

## What does it cost?

$15 a month covers 100,000 pageviews and events, $25 covers 200,000, and $45 covers 500,000. Annual billing gives two months free. The trial lasts seven days.

## Our answer

Run Fathom beside the current tool for two weeks. Set up the events and campaigns that matter, compare the counts, and try to answer the hardest real question. Keep Fathom if the short dashboard replaces the reports the team actually uses.
