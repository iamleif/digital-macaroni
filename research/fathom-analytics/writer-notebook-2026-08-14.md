# Fathom Analytics writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no hands-on install
- Price and terms checked 2026-08-14
- Customer evidence: 17 G2, 5 Product Hunt, and community discussions
- Referral-linked recommendations were not used as clean praise

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a small business or content team that needs to know how many people visit, what brought them, which pages they read, and whether they completed an important action
- Plain example: check visits from a newsletter, see the landing page, and count demo requests
- Cost of failure: tracking misses conversions, counts bots, or gives too little detail for the decision

## Product facts

- Hosted analytics with a small tracking script
- One main dashboard with pages, referrers, countries, devices, browsers, campaigns, and events
- Custom events count toward the plan limit like pageviews
- Starts at $15 monthly for 100,000 pageviews and events
- 200,000 is $25; 500,000 is $45; annual prices give two months free
- At least 50 sites per plan
- Seven-day trial, no permanent free hosted tier
- Data history remains available while subscribed; exports available

## Experience records

### A site owner replaced a dense analytics screen

- Clear dashboard made daily traffic easier to understand
- Consequence: more people may use the data when it answers the common questions at a glance

### A marketer needed to examine one page in more detail

- Community feedback found the reduced drill-down limiting
- Consequence: validate the hardest report before switching

### Community praise included referral links

- Some strong recommendations had a financial incentive
- Consequence: weight non-promotional and verified feedback more heavily

## Feature translation

### Event

- A chosen action, such as a signup, purchase, file download, or button click

### Referrer

- The site or link that sent a visitor

### UTM campaign

- Labels added to a link so visits and results can be tied to a newsletter, ad, or campaign

## Conflict ledger

- Easy to read means fewer ways to slice the data.
- Paid privacy analytics has a visible bill; free tools may charge in complexity, data use, or staff time.
- A privacy-focused script does not make the whole website compliant.
