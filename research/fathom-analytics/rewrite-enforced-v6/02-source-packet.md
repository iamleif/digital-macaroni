- The first public version of Fathom launched on June 12, 2018. [verified; fact; history, product-hunt]
- Fathom is a hosted, privacy-focused website analytics service with an open-source self-hosted lineage. [verified; fact; history, github]
- Current monthly pricing starts at $15 for up to 100,000 pageviews and events, then $25 for 200,000 and $45 for 500,000. Annual billing gives two months free. [verified; fact; pricing, registration]
