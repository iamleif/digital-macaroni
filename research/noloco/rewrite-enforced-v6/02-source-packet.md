## Claims

- Noloco turns tables and connected business data into internal tools and client portals with forms, views, permissions, comments, and workflows. [verified; fact; official-pricing, g2]
- Free currently includes unlimited apps and team seats, 50 client seats, 2,000 records per app, and community support. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Build is currently $79 monthly with 10 included team seats, up to 1,000 client seats, all listed data sources, 25,000 records, 3,000 workflow runs, and live chat and email support. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Build charges $6 for each extra team seat and $25 monthly for each extra 25,000-record block, up to 250,000 records before a custom plan is needed. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Fast portal and internal-tool setup, data connections, flexibility, and responsive support are repeated strengths. [supported; reported-pattern; product-hunt, g2, capterra]
- Missing features, visual limits, learning effort, price, sync, and record limits appear in user feedback. [supported; reported-pattern; g2]
- One detailed G2 reviewer reports a 50,000-row limit, Xano sync failures, a slow editor, and a refund dispute; Noloco disputes important parts and says the use case exceeded the product's supported scale. [limited; reported-pattern; g2]
