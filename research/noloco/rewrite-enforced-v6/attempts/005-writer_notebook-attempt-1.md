  "content": "Noloco is a no-code platform that transforms business data into internal tools and client portals. It allows users to create forms, views, permissions, comments, and workflows to manage data efficiently. The product is designed for businesses that need secure, customizable solutions for client interactions and internal operations. Noloco's core strength lies in its ability to quickly turn existing data tables into functional applications, making it a practical choice for teams looking to streamline processes without coding.\n\nThe Free plan offers unlimited apps and team seats, 50 client seats, 2,000 records per app, and community support. The Build plan, priced at $79 per month, includes 10 team seats, up to 1,000 client seats, all data sources, 25,000 records, 3,000 workflow runs, and live chat and email support. Additional team seats and record blocks can be added at $6 and $25 per month, respectively, with a maximum of 250,000 records before requiring a custom plan.\n\nUsers frequently highlight the platform's ease of use, flexibility, and responsive support. Many reviewers note that Noloco enables fast setup of portals and internal tools, which can be a significant advantage for teams needing to deploy solutions quickly. However, some users report challenges with record limits, visual constraints, and the learning curve associated with mastering the platform's features.\n\nA few detailed accounts mention issues such as sync failures, slow editors, and record limits that exceeded expectations, leading to disputes and refunds. Noloco disputes some of these claims, attributing them to cases where the product's scale was exceeded. These reports suggest that while Noloco is well-suited for many use cases, it may not meet the needs of all users, particularly those with high data volume or complex integration requirements.\n\nOverall, Noloco is a solid choice for businesses that require a secure, customizable solution for managing data and client interactions. Its strengths in speed, flexibility, and support make it a practical tool for many teams, though its limitations in scalability and customization may be a drawback for more advanced use cases."
