# Noloco argument card

- **Buyer:** An agency or service firm that wants a client portal or internal tool over existing business records.
- **Job:** Give staff and clients different views of the same work, collect updates, and automate repeated steps.
- **Thesis:** Noloco is a fast way to turn a known business process into a working portal, but the buyer must test data size and sync before moving the whole operation.
- **Reason to buy:** It handles screens, logins, record permissions, comments, and workflows without a custom build.
- **Reason to hesitate:** Visual limits and large external databases can push beyond the simple path, and the bill grows with records and team seats.
- **Decision rule:** Load 100 real records, create one staff and one client account, run one complete job, and price the next year of rows before subscribing.
- **Evidence limit:** No Digital Macaroni hands-on test was performed, and the strongest failure account is disputed and tied to older pricing.
