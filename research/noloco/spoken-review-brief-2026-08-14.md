# Noloco spoken review brief

## What is it?

Noloco takes business records and turns them into an app. Staff can manage the work, while each client signs in to see only their own projects, files, invoices, or approvals.

## Who will find it useful?

An agency or service company whose process already works in a spreadsheet or database but needs a cleaner and safer way for staff and clients to use it.

## What is good?

It can produce useful screens, forms, permissions, comments, and repeated actions quickly. Support receives strong praise across reviews.

## What is wrong?

It is not an unlimited custom app. Large outside databases, unusual design needs, and heavy sync can reach limits. A serious G2 dispute shows why real row volume must be tested first.

## What does it cost?

Free includes 2,000 records, 50 clients, and unlimited team seats. Build is $79 monthly with 25,000 records, 10 team seats, and up to 1,000 clients. Extra team seats are $6 and each extra 25,000 records is $25.

## Our answer

Put 100 real records into Free or the 14-day Build trial. Create a staff login and client login. Complete one job and test every permission. Pay only after the yearly row count and workflow use fit the plan.
