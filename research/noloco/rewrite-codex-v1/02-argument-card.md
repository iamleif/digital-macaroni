# Noloco argument card

## Buyer

An agency or service firm that needs staff and clients to work with different views of the same business records.

## Decision

Use Noloco when the process already fits rows, roles, forms, comments, and actions, after sync and access tests pass.

## Why

It can replace months of basic portal building. The ready-made structure becomes a limit for huge data sets or highly custom software.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if one client can reach another client's data, outside records drift, the editor slows at real scale, projected limits raise the bill too far, or the design needs a blank canvas.

## Verdict

Fast private portals after data and permissions pass testing.
