# Formbricks writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on survey, targeting, cloud, self-hosting, upgrade, or support test. Ten sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Formbricks supports link, website, email, and in-product surveys through cloud and self-hosted deployments.

The buyer is a software team that wants feedback tied to product behavior or needs control over survey data. Targeting, self-hosting, open code, a clean builder, customization, APIs, and integrations are the main strengths. Targeting identity, technical setup, cloud price jumps, and open-core feature gates are the limits.

Cloud and self-hosting are separate decisions. The recommendation requires one real survey with repeated events, account switching, dismissal, frequency, mobile, and correct response identity. Self-hosting also needs license mapping, email, access, restore, update, and public-route monitoring.
