# Formbricks argument card

## Buyer

A software team that needs surveys tied to product events or has a firm data-control requirement.

## Decision

Start with Cloud and prove one targeted survey. Self-host only when a named owner accepts the service work and required features fit the license.

## Why

In-product timing and identity can produce more useful answers than a survey link. Bad targeting creates bad data. Self-hosting adds infrastructure work and paid organizational controls.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if identity or frequency rules fail, one paid feature makes Cloud poor value, license gates block needed access, or nobody owns a private deployment.

## Verdict

Powerful targeted surveys with setup and licensing headaches.
