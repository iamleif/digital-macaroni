It offers both cloud and self-hosted deployment options, catering to users who prioritize data control and customization. The product is structured around three pricing tiers: Hobby (free for up to 250 responses), Pro ($74/month for 2,000 responses), and Scale ($325 for 5,000 responses). The self-hosted version is open source under AGPLv3 but includes enterprise features like branding removal and audit logs in a paid Enterprise Edition. Users praise its clean interface and privacy controls but note challenges with advanced integrations and the technical demands of self-hosting. Formbricks is a strong fit for organizations that need flexibility and data ownership, but its complexity may deter those without development expertise."
