## Claims

- Formbricks recorded its public Product Hunt launch on October 31, 2023. [verified; fact; timeline, product-hunt]
- Formbricks supports link, website, email, and in-product surveys through cloud and self-hosted deployments. [verified; fact; pricing, self-host]
- Cloud Hobby is free for 250 responses monthly. Pro costs $74 monthly billed yearly for 2,000 responses, and Scale costs $325 for 5,000. The Pro trial lasts 14 days. [verified; fact; pricing]
- The self-hosted core is AGPLv3 and includes unlimited surveys, responses, and users, while branding removal, roles, segments, advanced feedback, audit logs, SSO, spam protection, and other controls require the Enterprise Edition. [verified; fact; license]
- Privacy control, self-hosting, open code, a clean builder, targeting, customization, APIs, and integrations are the main positive themes. [supported; reported-pattern; product-hunt, g2, reddit-host]
- Advanced integrations and self-hosted changes can require more technical skill than the survey builder suggests. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, github, reddit-host]
- Open-core feature gates and branding limits frustrate some self-hosters who expected all capabilities in the community edition. [supported; reported-pattern; trustpilot, reddit-license, license]
