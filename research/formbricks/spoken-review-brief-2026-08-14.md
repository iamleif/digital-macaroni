# Formbricks spoken review brief

## What is it?

Formbricks makes link surveys and questions shown inside a website or app. It can be hosted by Formbricks or run on your own server.

## Who will find it useful?

A software team asking customers about onboarding, a feature, satisfaction, or cancellation, especially when product events and data control matter.

## What is good?

The builder, targeting, styling, API, integrations, open code, and self-hosting receive strong praise. Product teams can ask a question at the moment that gives it context.

## What is wrong?

Identity and event tracking add technical work. Cloud pricing jumps from free to $74 monthly on an annual plan. The free self-hosted core does not include every role, branding, security, and feedback feature.

## What does it cost?

Cloud Hobby is free for 250 responses. Pro is $74 a month annually for 2,000, and Scale is $325 for 5,000. Community self-hosting is free software with operating cost. Enterprise self-host features need a quote.

## Our answer

Test whether the right person sees one survey at the right time and the answer attaches to the right user. For self-hosting, also map every required feature to the license table and prove email, backup restore, updates, and monitoring.
