# Formbricks private argument card

- Buyer: a software or privacy-conscious team collecting web and in-product feedback
- Job: ask a short question at the right moment, connect the response to context, and use the answer safely
- Thesis: Formbricks is a strong option when in-product targeting or deployment control matters, but the buyer must test identity and licensing before being impressed by the survey builder
- Main reason to buy: open code, self-hosting, strong APIs, and surveys tied to product behavior
- Main reason to hesitate: cloud price jump, open-core gates, technical self-host work, and modest market evidence
- When it is unnecessary: a simple link survey works, or the team lacks a real plan for acting on the responses
- Decision rule: run one live survey with real eligibility and identity rules; trigger events twice, change accounts, decline, return, and test mobile; for self-hosting also prove email, backup restore, upgrades, monitoring, and every required enterprise feature
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on survey or deployment
