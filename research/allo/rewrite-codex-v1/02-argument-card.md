# Private argument card

- Buyer: a small sales, support, or service team that wants shared calls plus automatic notes.
- Job: answer customers, keep a useful record, and let another teammate continue the conversation.
- Thesis: Allo's automation is genuinely useful, but the available reliability account is serious enough that it should begin as a second line, not the only business number.
- Reason to buy: recordings, transcripts, shared notes, and CRM logging can remove repetitive work after every call.
- Reason to hesitate: calls, texts, and CRM records are the basics. One detailed account describes failures in all three, followed by slow support.
- Decision rule: run Allo beside the current phone system. Move the main number only after calls, texts, CRM entries, and support remain dependable.
- Evidence limit: detailed accounts are few and come from one platform. Digital Macaroni did not test call quality or reliability.
