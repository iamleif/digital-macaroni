# Spoken review brief

Allo gives a team business phone numbers, records calls, writes transcripts, and can put the call details into the customer database. It is useful for a small team that spends too much time writing notes after calls or asking a coworker what happened.

The automation is good enough to justify a trial. One team said it replaced RingCentral, took less than five minutes to set up, and gave them good call quality. A different business user had calls and texts fail, saw wrong HubSpot records, and went back to a personal number. Support was slow while the problems continued.

That second account changes the recommendation. A phone system has to handle calls and messages before its summaries matter. Allo should start as a second line while the team tests normal customer traffic.

The AI receptionist may help a solo owner or a small office answer routine calls. It matters less when a person already answers the phone. A business that needs another language should check that before paying, since one charter company still needed Spanish support.

I would tell a friend to connect the CRM, make and receive calls, send texts, and check every customer record. I would also contact support during the trial. Keep the old number until Allo proves that the basic service is dependable.
