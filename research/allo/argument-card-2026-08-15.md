Allo provides AI‑driven call handling and automatic CRM sync at a clear $32 per‑user price. The AI receptionist and call transcription are useful for sales and support teams that need hands‑free note taking. However, frequent app crashes, missed calls, and inaccurate CRM updates force many users to revert to manual processes. Support response times are slow, leaving issues unresolved for days. The product’s promise is strong, but its operational reliability makes it a poor fit for most businesses that cannot tolerate communication gaps.
