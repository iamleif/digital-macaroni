Allo is a phone system that lives on your phone and adds AI call recording, transcription, and an automated receptionist. It also tries to push every call into your CRM automatically. It’s aimed at small sales or support teams that want a RingCentral‑style service without a desk phone. The AI receptionist works well for simple call routing, but it doesn’t handle multiple languages yet. Automatic CRM sync often drops data, so teams still have to enter notes by hand. The app crashes and calls fail for some users, and support is slow to reply. If you need a reliable phone line and can’t afford downtime, look elsewhere. If you’re a solo entrepreneur who just needs basic AI call handling, it might be worth a try.
