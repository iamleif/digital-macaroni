## Evidence summary
- Allo is a mobile‑first phone system with AI call recording, transcription, and an automated receptionist (official site).
- Claims automatic CRM sync and $32 / user flat‑rate pricing (official site).
- Positive Apple App Store review: setup under 5 minutes, good call quality, improved team collaboration.
- Negative Apple App Store review: intermittent call/text failures, wrong chat archiving, HubSpot sync errors, forced use of personal number; vendor support slow.
- Mixed review: AI receptionist useful but lacking Spanish support and spreadsheet integration.

## Judgment
Allo’s AI features are attractive for small sales/support teams, but reliability problems with the app and CRM sync, plus slow support, make it a risky choice for most businesses.

## Scores rationale
- **Onboarding (6/10)**: Quick setup reported, but some users hit signup or activation hiccups.
- **Product (5/10)**: Core AI functions work, but frequent instability and sync inaccuracies lower overall reliability.
- **Support (4/10)**: Multiple reports of slow, unhelpful responses.
- **Billing (8/10)**: Transparent flat‑rate pricing confirmed, no hidden fees.

## Verdict
Allo offers strong AI features but reliability hurts adoption.
