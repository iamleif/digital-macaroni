Allo is a business phone service that uses artificial intelligence to manage calls and automatically records or transcribes them. It also connects calls to customer relationship management (CRM) systems, helping teams keep track of interactions without manual entry. This can save time for small to medium-sized businesses looking to streamline their phone operations. The main feature, AI-powered call handling, allows teams to automate tasks like answering calls, scheduling meetings, and logging details, which can reduce the need for repetitive work. However, there are no direct tests to confirm how well it performs in real situations, and most reviews are limited or anecdotal. Some users have reported problems with the app working properly and difficulties getting support. If the features fit your workflow and you can integrate it with your existing systems, it might be worth trying, especially with the free trial. But before paying, check if the AI features align with your needs and whether the app works reliably with your current tools.
