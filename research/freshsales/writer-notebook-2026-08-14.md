# Freshsales writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no hands-on CRM use
- Current official pricing checked 2026-08-14
- 1,192 G2 reviews, 90 TrustRadius records, recent community counterpoint, commercial editorial test
- Stale free-plan claim removed

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a small or growing sales team that wants contacts, deals, email, calls, tasks, and follow-up in one CRM
- Plain example: import leads, assign them, email and call from the record, move the deal, trigger a task, and produce the weekly forecast
- Cost of failure: duplicate records split history, automation sends the wrong message, a report gives the wrong total, or mobile staff cannot update the deal

## Product facts

- Contacts, accounts, deals, Kanban pipeline, chat, email, phone, tasks, workflows, reports, mobile, AI
- Growth: $9 per user monthly billed annually; basic workflows, fields, templates, lifecycle stages
- Pro: $39; scoring, deal insights, custom sales activities, territories, sequences
- Enterprise: $59; forecasts, field permissions, custom modules, sandbox, audit logs
- Twenty-one-day trial; no permanent free plan on current official pricing
- Freddy AI Agent: $49 per 100 sessions; 500 one-time trial sessions on paid account
- Branded documents listed as an add-on

## Experience records

### A small team brought calls, email, and deals together

- Large review pools praise follow-up, communication, and easy standard work
- Consequence: Freshsales is strongest when it replaces manual updates between phone, inbox, and pipeline

### A manager built a custom report

- Reporting is the clearest repeated pain point, including a recent broken-report account
- Consequence: reproduce the exact weekly report before importing the whole team

### A field seller used mobile

- Mobile can lag desktop in speed or depth
- Consequence: complete the full after-meeting update from a phone

## Feature translation

### Contact scoring

- A number used to rank which lead is most likely or ready to buy

### Sales sequence

- A timed set of email and task steps for following up with a lead

### Territory

- A rule assigning customers or deals to a sales group by place, type, or another field

### Sandbox

- A safe copy used to test changes before they affect the live CRM

## Conflict ledger

- Standard CRM work is easy, while custom work creates most complaints.
- Growth is inexpensive, but Pro gates common scaling features.
- Support is fine in many cases and frustrating in some hard report or workflow cases.

## Missing evidence

- No import, duplicate, email, phone, sequence, workflow, report, mobile, AI, permission, export, billing, or support test
- No deliverability test
- No forecast accuracy test

## Spoken language bank

- build the Monday sales report first
