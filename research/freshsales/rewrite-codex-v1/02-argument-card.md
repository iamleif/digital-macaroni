# Freshsales argument card

## Buyer

A small or growing sales team that wants contacts, deals, email, calls, and tasks in one CRM.

## Decision

Choose Freshsales when the real weekly report stays correct and the team can manage custom rules. Skip it for unusual sales data or reporting needs.

## Why

Everyday pipeline and communication work is approachable at a low Growth price. Custom reports and advanced setup are weaker, while common growing-team features require Pro.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if imports damage history, reports change in unexpected ways, follow-ups fail to stop, mobile work is too limited, or support cannot own a hard problem.

## Verdict

Good sales software if its reports fit your process.
