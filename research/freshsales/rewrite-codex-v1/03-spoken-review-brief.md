# Freshsales spoken review brief

Freshsales keeps contacts, deals, email, calls, tasks, and reports together. It is good for a normal small sales team, and the Growth price is low.

I would build the Monday sales report before polishing the pipeline. Custom reports are where the product receives more mixed feedback. Use dirty records, change old deals, reopen a lost one, and move ownership to see what happens to the totals.

Test inbox sync, phone history, follow-up stopping, and mobile work too. I would skip Freshsales if the manager still rebuilds the numbers in a spreadsheet every week.
