# Freshsales private argument card

- Buyer: a small or growing sales team that wants communication and pipeline work in one CRM
- Job: keep each lead's history, next step, owner, deal stage, and forecast current
- Thesis: Freshsales offers strong value for a standard sales process, but the purchase should be decided by the hardest report and automation, not the easy pipeline board
- Main reason to buy: email, phone, chat, records, deals, and basic automation share one system at a low entry price
- Main reason to hesitate: a large Growth-to-Pro price jump, mixed advanced support, mobile limits, brittle reports, and AI add-on cost
- When it is unnecessary: the team has a simple list with little follow-up, or needs highly specialized enterprise quoting, revenue, or industry workflows
- Decision rule: import dirty records, merge duplicates, sync two inboxes, place and receive calls, build one sequence and one failure-prone workflow, reproduce the weekly report, and complete the after-meeting update on mobile
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on CRM test
