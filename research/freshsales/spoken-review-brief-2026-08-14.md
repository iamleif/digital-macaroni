# Freshsales spoken review brief

## What is it?

Freshsales keeps leads, customers, deals, email, calls, tasks, reports, and sales automation in one CRM.

## Who will find it useful?

A small or growing sales team with a normal pipeline that wants to stop copying activity between the inbox, phone, and deal list.

## What is good?

Standard sales work is approachable. Built-in communication and useful automation offer strong value, especially on Growth.

## What is wrong?

Custom reports and advanced setup create more trouble. Mobile does not always match desktop depth. Support is mixed on hard workflow and reporting problems. Pro costs much more than Growth.

## What does it cost?

With annual billing, Growth is $9 per user monthly, Pro $39, and Enterprise $59. The trial lasts 21 days. Freddy AI Agent costs $49 per 100 sessions. There is no current permanent free plan shown.

## Our answer

Build the Monday sales report first. Then use a dirty import, test duplicate merges, sync email, make a call, break an automation on purpose, and complete the whole after-meeting update from a phone. Buy only after the hard work fits the required plan.
