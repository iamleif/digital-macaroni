# Morgen spoken review brief

## What is it?

Morgen puts several calendars and task lists into one planning screen. A person can see meetings, drag tasks onto free time, share booking links, and use automatic planning rules.

## Who will find it useful?

Someone who opens two or three calendars plus a task app each morning just to work out what can fit into the day.

## What is good?

It gives meetings and unfinished work the same view. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, mobile, and the browser. Linux support is especially uncommon.

## What is wrong?

The whole idea depends on correct updates between Morgen and the connected apps. Speed, recurring tasks, mobile quality, accessibility controls, and price appear as limits.

## What does it cost?

Individual costs $30 month to month or $15 per month billed yearly. Team costs $25 per seat monthly or $10 per seat monthly billed yearly, with at least two seats. The trial lasts 14 days and needs no card.

## Our answer

Use the trial for one real, difficult week. Change meetings and tasks in both places. Test repeated work, time zones, the phone, and a booking link. Keep Morgen only if the original apps can stay closed for most of the day.
