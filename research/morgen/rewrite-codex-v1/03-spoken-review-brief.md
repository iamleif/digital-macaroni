# Morgen spoken review brief

Morgen is for a day split across several calendars and task apps. It brings those sources into one view and lets you place tasks beside meetings. That can answer when work will happen, instead of showing another long list.

The whole idea depends on trust. A moved meeting, completed task, recurring item, or booking must stay right on both sides. Mobile and accessibility needs also deserve a real test.

I would connect the real accounts and plan one messy week. Keep Morgen only if stale and missing items stay rare and the need to check the source apps mostly disappears.
