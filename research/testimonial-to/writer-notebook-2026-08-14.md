# Testimonial.to writer's notebook

## Reader and job

- Small company, creator, agency, or product team that needs customer proof without building a collection system
- Ask ten real customers, collect useful text or video, confirm permission, approve it, place it beside a relevant claim, update it, and remove it
- Failure means vague praise, unclear consent, an awkward video form, stale claims, broken embeds, hidden over-limit submissions, or expensive extra spaces

## Product and price

- Hosted text and video collection pages; customers do not need accounts
- Approval library, social imports, Wall of Love, single-item embeds, links, downloads, and paid automation
- Free: one space, ten text and two video items total
- Starter: $25 monthly billed yearly; unlimited text, two videos total
- Ultimate: $50 monthly per space billed yearly; unlimited text and video, automation, API, webhooks, team members
- Ultimate Plus: $95 monthly per space billed yearly; case studies, NPS, AI video editing, more automation, unlimited team members

## Stance

- Thesis: Testimonial.to is useful when it removes collection and publishing work, but it cannot create specific or credible proof by itself
- Main attraction: one no-code route from request to approved embed
- Main risk: weak questions, unclear permission, stale proof, limited layout control, and per-space pricing
- No hands-on test

## Spoken language bank

- ask about the moment before and after
- permission is part of the testimonial
- put proof beside the claim it supports
- remove one quote and check every copy
- compare it with a form and a normal web page
