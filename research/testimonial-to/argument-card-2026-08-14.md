# Testimonial.to private argument card

- Buyer: a small team that has happy customers but no reliable way to ask, organize, and publish their stories
- Job: move a permitted customer statement from request to the right page without developer work
- Thesis: Testimonial.to is useful collection plumbing; it becomes valuable only when the questions produce specific proof and the team maintains permission, context, and placement
- Main reason to buy: quick text and video collection plus reusable no-code embeds
- Main reason to hesitate: video and space pricing, limited design control, unclear reuse rights, and stale proof
- Decision rule: ask ten customers about one completed outcome, collect both formats, confirm permission, embed proof beside the claim it supports, update and remove an item, check the page on a phone, then compare total work with a plain form and manual page
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni collection page
