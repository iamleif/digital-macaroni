# Argument card

- Thesis: Testimonial.to earns its cost when it simplifies the entire proof workflow, not only collection.
- Strength: fast hosted requests, text and video intake, imports, approval, and no-code embeds.
- Limit: customization, video caps, space pricing, and creator access can become restrictive.
- Buyer: small teams collecting and reusing proof regularly.
- Skip: projects with a few static written quotes.
- Price anchor: Free has ten text and two video testimonials; annual Starter was $25 monthly and Ultimate $50 per space monthly.
- Buying test: collect from ten customers, publish, revise, remove, and export.
- Verdict: Proof collection works when permission and placement stay clear.
