# Spoken review brief

Start with the practical value: a company can collect text and video without building an uploader or asking a developer to publish each quote.

Then narrow the claim. The software cannot turn vague praise into evidence. Questions, context, consent, accurate edits, and placement still belong to the business.

Use a ten-customer test. Ask about one completed outcome, try text and video on a phone, interrupt an upload, approve the best items, place each near the claim it supports, and remove one everywhere.

Explain the plan trap. Free proves the route. Starter still caps video at two. Unlimited video begins at Ultimate, charged per space.

State that no hands-on test was run and that testimonials do not guarantee higher conversion.
