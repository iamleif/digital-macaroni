# Writer notebook

## Buyer and job

Testimonial.to gives a business a hosted page for collecting written and video testimonials, an inbox for approving them, and links or embeds for publishing them.

## Thesis

The tool removes setup work, but the value still depends on specific questions, clear reuse permission, accurate editing, useful placement, and regular removal of stale claims.

## Best fit

A small team that requests customer proof often and wants one route for text, video, approval, storage, and website embeds.

## Poor fit

A tiny project that needs a few written quotes can use a normal form, written permission, and a website section.

## Evidence limit

No collection, recording, import, embed, analytics, export, billing, or support test was run. Vendor examples do not prove a general conversion lift.

## Decisive test

Ask ten customers about one finished outcome. Check permission and each claim, publish the best proof beside the relevant claim, then update, remove, and export one item.
