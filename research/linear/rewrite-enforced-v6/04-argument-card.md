Linear is a project management tool tailored for software teams that value speed, clarity, and keyboard-driven workflows. It positions itself as a simpler alternative to Jira, emphasizing ease of use and minimal setup. The buyer is likely a small to medium-sized software team seeking a streamlined interface for issue tracking, backlog management, and release planning. The thesis is that Linear excels in delivering a fast, focused experience but lacks the depth needed for more complex workflows. The reason to buy is its clean design and performance, which suit teams prioritizing efficiency. The reason to hesitate is its limited reporting and customization options, which may not meet the needs of larger or more intricate teams. The decision rule is to choose Linear if the team’s workflow is straightforward and the priority is speed over flexibility. Evidence is limited to reported user experiences and lacks direct testing, so the assessment is based on what users have shared rather than firsthand evaluation.
