# Linear spoken review brief

Linear feels quick because it makes many project-management choices for you. Creating an issue, finding work, and changing its state should take little effort. That helps a small software team keep the board honest.

The limits come from that same focus. Managers may find the reports thin. Large organizations may miss detailed workflow control. Marketing and sales teams may also find the product too centered on software delivery.

I would choose Linear for a focused product and engineering team. I would first pilot a live project and confirm the reports, required states, integrations, export route, and account recovery process.
