# Linear argument card

## Buyer

A small product and engineering team that wants fast issue tracking and can follow a defined workflow.

## Decision

Use Linear when lower daily friction matters more than deep reporting and extensive process control.

## Why

Its speed and clear defaults make routine updates easier. The same focus leaves less room for complex reports, approvals, and company-wide work.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if managers need reports Linear cannot provide, required workflow states need awkward workarounds, non-software teams cannot use the language, or the account and export plan feels unsafe.

## Verdict

Excellent issue tracking for focused software teams.
