# Aircall writer's notebook

- Buyer: a sales or support team that wants business calls tied to its CRM.
- Job: keep call history, notes, and ownership visible across the team.
- Best evidence: 250+ integrations, shared call work, coaching and transcription tools.
- Main risks: call quality complaints, post-update friction, privacy review, and strict renewals.
- Evidence limit: no hands-on test and no complete public price table in the packet.
- Trial rule: test real devices, networks, call routes, CRM records, and the written cancellation terms.
