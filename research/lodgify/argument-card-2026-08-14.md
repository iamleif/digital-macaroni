# Lodgify argument card

- **Buyer:** An owner-run rental business that wants to add direct bookings without assembling its own booking stack.
- **Job:** Put direct bookings, OTA bookings, payments, guest messages, and the working calendar in one place.
- **Thesis:** Lodgify earns its place through direct bookings, but it has not earned a set-and-forget recommendation for a live multi-channel migration.
- **Reason to buy:** The Starter plan connects a website and booking widget to the same booking records used for channels, payments, and messages. Guided onboarding addresses a task that many small hosts do not want to solve alone.
- **Reason to hesitate:** The detailed failures cluster around existing Vrbo reservations, blocked dates, listing mapping, and support after a live problem. Those are migration risks with guest-facing consequences.
- **Decision rule:** Buy Lodgify when direct bookings justify the switch and you can audit every existing reservation, blocked date, and property mapping before enabling instant booking. Skip it when the business cannot tolerate that migration check or depends on many listings, phone work, and deep reports.
- **Evidence limit:** This is research-based. We did not use Lodgify or run the migration test ourselves.
