It offers direct booking, channel management, reservations, guest messaging, and property operations in one system. The platform is well-suited for small operators seeking simplicity and cost-effectiveness. Onboarding and setup guidance are highly praised, but synchronization and calendar issues can lead to availability errors and double bookings, though these are reported less frequently. The mobile app has credible reports of messaging and reporting problems. The Starter plan includes a 1.9 percent booking fee, and higher plans may increase costs as volume grows. Larger operators may find the platform limited in controls and reporting. Trustpilot shows strong satisfaction, but detailed reports highlight high-impact synchronization failures. Buyers should test channel connections before committing."
