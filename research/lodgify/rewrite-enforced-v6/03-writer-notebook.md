Lodgify is a property management platform designed for independent hosts and small to midsize vacation-rental operators. It offers a direct-booking website, channel management, reservations, guest messaging, and property operations in one system. The platform is backed by a score of 7.7, with onboarding rated highest at 8.5 and billing lowest at 7.1. The review is based on official product information, five independent review platforms, and three community discussions. No hands-on testing was conducted, and no payment was received for coverage. The review highlights both strengths and areas for improvement based on user experiences and reported patterns.

Lodgify is best supported by evidence from independent hosts and small to midsize vacation-rental portfolios. A direct-booking website is a repeated reason hosts choose the platform. Onboarding guidance and setup help receive strong praise across large review samples. However, calendar and OTA synchronization failures can cause wrong availability and double bookings, although these reports are a minority of the total feedback. Support earns praise during onboarding, while some users report slow or fragmented help after urgent operational failures.

The Starter plan includes a 1.9 percent booking fee, and higher-plan feature gates can raise the practical cost as booking volume grows. The mobile app has credible reports of messaging, reporting, and connection problems and does not always match the web product. The platform is a strong fit for small vacation-rental operators who want direct booking in the same system as daily operations. Larger or more complex portfolios should test every channel connection before committing.

Trustpilot shows very strong satisfaction, while detailed community and app-store reports describe high-impact synchronization failures. Small hosts often find the platform simple and cost-effective, while larger operators report more limits in controls and reporting. Current OTA behavior and plan gates can change and require buyer verification. No new hands-on test was completed.
