# Lodgify review: review-skill final

## Our verdict

Lodgify makes the most sense as a direct-booking tool. A host can build a site, take payment, and put the booking in the same calendar used for Airbnb and Vrbo. For a one- or two-property business that wants to rely less on those sites, the package has a point.

That value depends on Lodgify importing the dates already held elsewhere. The detailed failures involve old Vrbo bookings, blocked dates, and links to the wrong rental. One missed date can leave two guests holding bookings for the same home.

We recommend Lodgify to a small host who wants direct bookings and can check the move before turning on instant booking. A business that must move many live listings and trust them on day one should choose another system.

## The website is the reason to buy

Lodgify's plans make its focus clear for their users. Basic centers on the channel calendar and inbox. Starter adds the site builder, booking form, payments, reports, co-host access, and a guided setup call. Lodgify becomes more useful when a host wants guests to book outside Airbnb and Vrbo.

That setup can replace a site builder, booking form, payment service, and separate booking tool. It also cuts admin work. A direct booking enters the same record used for guest messages and open dates. The host has fewer rates, payments, and guest details to copy between accounts.

The website builder might not be for everyone. However we still think it is a pretty cool tool for those who are not tech savvy.  Some hosts describe the designs as basic. One host used Lodgify for a few months, then looked for a simple booking page that would leave the other listings alone. If you already have a site you like and only need calendar sync, much of Lodgify's value disappears.

## Setup help does not remove the risk of moving

Setup help is one of the clearest positives in the research. Recent Trustpilot entries describe one-to-one calls where Lodgify staff walked through the account and answered questions. Many sent notes, recordings, emails, or videos after the call. Customers valued finding settings they had missed and having a recording while they finished the work.

Most of those reviews were written at the start. They tell us Lodgify can guide a new customer through setup. They say less about the response after a booking or channel has failed.

A setup call cannot catch every bad import. One host brought six furnished rentals into Lodgify because Appfolio did not suit the business. A client also wanted listings on more sites. Old Vrbo bookings and blocked dates failed to appear in Lodgify. A new Airbnb listing then took a booking for an occupied date.

Another small host said Vrbo never connected. Bookings still had to be changed by hand across Airbnb and Booking.com, and the account ended in a double booking. Lodgify asked for the account details in the public thread. The thread does not show whether the problem was fixed.

These accounts do not show that Lodgify often loses bookings. They do show which part of setup needs a full check. Before instant booking goes live, compare every old booking and blocked date with the source site. Match each home address to the right listing name. Then create and cancel a test booking on each link. Stop the move if an old date or test change fails to appear.

## Growth brings a different set of limits

A two-site glamping host shows why the product can be worth the work. The business used Lodgify for live channel links and bookings from smaller sites. It also used the task tools to plan cleaning and repairs. Bookings entered once, and the work stayed with them.

The same host later began looking at other tools after Lodgify changed its calendar controls. Default rates could still be set, but the final OTA prices were no longer shown inside Lodgify. The host began making mock bookings on each OTA to check the markups. One of Lodgify's jobs moved back to the channel sites.

Mobile use follows a similar split. Older App Store reviews say the app grew to cover tasks that once required a laptop. A 2026 review came from a host with 20 properties. The app took about a minute to load, owner statements grouped fees the wrong way, and support did not fix the issue after getting a screen recording. Lodgify said a new release fixed loading and stability. We have not checked whether it fixed that account.

The app may be enough for a small owner checking bookings away from a desk. We would want more proof from a manager who needs the app and owner statements to work across 20 properties. Staff, reports, and more channel links give a small fault more chances to become daily work.

## Price and value

Lodgify changed its pricing after our first research pass. The current page lists four plans with a 0 percent booking fee. The old 1.9 percent Starter fee is out of date. Lodgify says the plan price depends on property count and billing term.

The plan limits matter more than one monthly figure. Basic covers one property but leaves out the direct-booking site. Starter is open to hosts with up to two properties. It adds the site, booking form, payments, guided setup, reports, and co-host access. Automated messages and phone support begin with Professional. Owner statements, tasks, and deeper reports sit in Ultimate.

Starter contains the part of Lodgify we find most useful for one or two properties. The choice gets harder when a business needs Ultimate for staff and owner reports. That is also where the detailed reports of app and reporting faults matter most. Ask for a quote based on the real property count, then compare it with the tools Lodgify would replace.

## Who should buy it

Lodgify fits an owner-run rental business that wants direct bookings and prefers one system to a custom stack. The host must also have time to check the channel move before taking new bookings. In that case, the website and booking engine do enough work to justify the limits elsewhere.

Skip it if you only need a channel calendar. Larger teams should wait until they have tested phone access, owner statements, and reports with their own data.

For a small host replacing four tools, Lodgify's site and booking engine justify the move. A host whose current channel manager already keeps live dates in order has little reason to disturb it for a new website.
