# Lodgify argument card

## Buyer

An independent host or small vacation-rental operator that wants direct bookings and channel management in one system.

## Decision

Use Lodgify after every connected calendar, price, message, and blocked date passes a live test.

## Why

The bundled site and setup help can save a small host time. A missed calendar update can create a double booking, so connection quality matters more than the website design.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if old reservations or blocked dates fail to import, channel rates disagree, mobile tasks are too slow, urgent support lacks ownership, or total fees cost more than the tools replaced.

## Verdict

Useful direct bookings after every calendar passes testing.
