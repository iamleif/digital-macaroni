# Lodgify spoken review brief

Lodgify makes the most sense for a small host who still needs a direct-booking site. It places the site, booking form, channel calendars, payments, and guest messages in one system. Setup help also gets strong marks.

Calendar sync is the decision point. A minority of hosts report missing reservations, blocked dates, or wrong availability. One failure can cause a double booking, so the size of that risk is greater than its frequency suggests.

I would use Lodgify only after importing all future stays and blocked dates, then making and cancelling a booking through every channel. I would also model the Starter booking fee and test mobile work before moving a larger portfolio.
