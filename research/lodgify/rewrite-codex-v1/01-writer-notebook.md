# Lodgify writer's notebook

Research-based review with no new hands-on site, channel, calendar, payment, message, mobile, support, or billing test. Twelve sources were checked on August 13 and 14, 2026. Lodgify combines a direct-booking website with channel management and daily vacation-rental work.

The buyer is an independent host or small rental operator that wants direct bookings without joining several tools. Setup help and the direct-booking package are the main strengths. Calendar sync failures are uncommon in the full feedback sample but carry severe consequences. Mobile gaps, urgent support, and the Starter booking fee also affect the decision.

The recommendation is positive only after a controlled channel test. Import every future stay and blocked date, make and cancel a booking through each channel, compare rates, test messages, and keep instant booking off until the calendars agree. Larger portfolios should test mobile work, owner reports, and support with real staff.
