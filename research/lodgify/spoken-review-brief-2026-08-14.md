# Lodgify spoken review brief

## What is it?

Lodgify gives vacation-rental hosts a website where guests can book directly. It also puts those bookings in the same place as bookings from Airbnb, Vrbo, and other sites. Hosts can handle payments, guest messages, open dates, and some property tasks there too.

## Who will find it useful?

A small host who does not have a website yet. The website builder is somewhat limited, but it gets the job done. It puts the properties online, lets guests check dates, and takes a booking without the host having to build that system from separate tools.

## When will the website builder not matter?

If the host already has a website they like, the builder is not much of a reason to switch. Lodgify can add a booking form to an existing site, but the rest of the decision then depends on how well it keeps bookings and dates updated across every site.

## What is the main concern?

When someone books through Airbnb, those dates should close on Vrbo and on the host's own website. Some small hosts say that worked well. The serious problems appeared while people were moving existing Vrbo bookings, blocked dates, or property connections into Lodgify.

One host moved six furnished rentals. Old Vrbo bookings and blocked dates did not show up, and a new Airbnb listing accepted a booking for a date that was already taken. Another host said the Vrbo connection never worked, so calendars still had to be changed by hand and the business ended up with a double booking.

This does not prove that Lodgify commonly loses bookings. It does give us a clear warning: do not connect live listings and assume every old date moved correctly.

## What is support like?

Lodgify seems good at helping new customers get started. Recent accounts mention one-to-one calls, staff answering questions, and useful notes or recordings afterward.

That is different from fixing a booking problem after the account is live. The accounts about broken Vrbo connections, closed tickets, or long waits are less reassuring. We can give Lodgify credit for setup without promising that support will rescue an urgent booking problem.

## Does it work for a larger rental business?

The evidence is better for a small business. A host with two glamping sites used Lodgify for bookings, cleaners, and repairs. A four-property owner liked having booking and payout records together but wanted better comparisons between properties.

At 20 properties, one host said the app took about a minute to load and owner reports put fees in the wrong place. Lodgify said an update improved speed and stability, but we do not know whether it fixed that host's reporting problem.

## What would we tell a friend to check?

If you already have bookings, go through every reservation and blocked date after connecting Airbnb, Vrbo, or another site. Check that each property is linked to the right listing. Make a test booking and cancel it on every connection before turning on instant booking.

Ask Lodgify for the real price for your number of properties. The current page lists four plans and a 0 percent booking fee, but it changes the plan price by property count and billing term.

## Our answer

Lodgify is worth considering for a small host who needs a direct-booking website. The builder is limited, but useful. The setup help is another good reason to choose it.

We would not switch to Lodgify just to put several live booking calendars in one place. If that is the main reason for buying it, the reported problems with old bookings, blocked dates, and Vrbo connections matter too much.
