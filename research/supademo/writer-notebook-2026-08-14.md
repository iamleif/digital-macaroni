# Supademo writer's notebook

## Reader and job

- Product marketer, salesperson, trainer, or support team explaining software repeatedly
- Capture one real workflow, guide a viewer through it, embed it in three places, update it after a product change, and see where viewers stop
- Failure means a missed click, broken scroll, stale screen, confusing branch, exposed customer data, or too few people allowed to edit

## Product and price

- Guided screenshot and video demos; collections, links, embeds, MP4, GIF, PDF, and PNG
- Free: one creator, five guided demos, 50 4K recordings, unlimited external views
- Scale: $38 monthly per creator, five view-only collaborators
- Growth: $350 monthly for five creators, $50 for each extra creator, unlimited viewers
- 14-day paid trial without a card

## Stance

- Thesis: Supademo is useful when a short interactive walkthrough replaces repeated explanation and remains easy to update
- Main attraction: fast creation and reuse
- Main risk: fragile capture and a growing maintenance library
- No hands-on test

## Spoken language bank

- show one job, not the whole product
- make a new user complete it without help
- change a button and time the repair
- count creators before choosing a plan
- analytics should lead to a specific fix
