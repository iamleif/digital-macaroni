# Supademo private argument card

- Buyer: a team that explains the same software workflow in sales, onboarding, support, or training
- Job: let a viewer complete a short guided product story without a live presenter
- Thesis: Supademo earns its cost when one maintained demo replaces repeated explanations; quick recording is irrelevant if dynamic screens break and nobody owns updates
- Main reason to buy: fast interactive demos that can be reused and measured
- Main reason to hesitate: capture glitches, editing limits, stale content, and creator-seat cost
- Decision rule: record one difficult workflow, give it to five new viewers, embed it in three real places, update it after changing the interface, verify analytics, and export or archive the result
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni demo
