# Supademo argument card

## Buyer

A team that repeatedly explains the same short software task in sales, support, training, or onboarding.

## Decision

Choose Supademo when one difficult walkthrough works without help and remains easy to repair after the product changes.

## Why

Interactive demos can be faster to create and reuse than another call or video. Every reused demo becomes a maintenance job.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if difficult screens require rerecording, viewers cannot finish alone, stale embeds are hard to find, analytics change no decision, or too few paid creators can make urgent fixes.

## Verdict

Fast product demos need an owner for every update.
