# Supademo spoken review brief

Supademo records a software task and turns it into a demo someone can click through. That is useful when sales, support, or onboarding explains the same thing every week.

The first demo can be fast. The real cost appears when a page scrolls badly, a click is missed, or the product changes after the demo is embedded in several places.

I would build one awkward three-minute task, give it to new viewers, publish it in three places, and move a button. The repair must be quick and have a clear owner.
