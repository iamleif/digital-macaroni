# Supademo writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on capture, editing, embedding, analytics, billing, export, or support test. Six sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Supademo turns recorded software steps into clickable demos, videos, guides, and collections.

The buyer is a sales, support, training, or onboarding team that explains the same short software task often. Fast creation and reuse are the strengths. Dynamic pages, scrolling, modals, rapid clicks, editing, voice work, organization, stale screens, analytics, and creator-seat costs are the concerns.

The recommendation is to build one difficult task, publish it in three places, change the product, and time the repair. Choose Supademo only when a new viewer finishes alone and an available owner can keep every copy current.
