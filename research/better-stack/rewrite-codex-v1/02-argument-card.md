# Private argument card

- Buyer: a small technical team choosing monitoring and incident tools.
- Job: move from a failed check to the right person, a cause, and a customer update.
- Thesis: Better Stack is an easy and useful place to start, but the paid product should be judged as one incident chain and priced with real data.
- Reason to buy: monitoring, on-call work, technical records, and status updates can stay in one product.
- Reason to hesitate: rare alert failures matter more than routine setup, and the bill grows across several components.
- Decision rule: cause a planned failure and follow it through the entire chain. Buy the joined stack only if the alert arrives, the data explains the problem, the status update works, and the forecast stays sensible.
- Evidence limit: no Digital Macaroni incident test and no reliable estimate for the buyer's data volume.
