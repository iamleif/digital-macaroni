# Better Stack writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review. Digital Macaroni did not run an outage, receive an alert, search logs, or update a status page.
- Sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. The packet has three official sources, four independent review listings, and two community discussions.
- The review pool is unusually positive. Ordinary setup happens often, while rare missed alerts may be underrepresented.

## Reader and job

The buyer is a small technical team that needs to know when a service fails, reach the person on duty, find the cause, and update customers. A personal-site owner may only need the free monitor and status page. A larger buyer needs to forecast responders, checks, data volume, and retention.

## Verified facts

- Better Stack combines uptime checks, on-call alerts, incident work, status pages, logs, traces, metrics, errors, and session replay.
- Free included ten monitors or heartbeats, one status page, Slack and email alerts, plus limited technical data.
- Paid incident response started at $34 monthly or $29 per responder with annual billing.
- Extra pages, security options, subscribers, faster checks, data ingestion, and retention could add cost.

## Reported patterns

- Strengths: quick setup, clean interface, alerts, integrations, and a useful free plan.
- Limits: missing features, price jumps, plan limits, and friction while working with logs.
- Conflict: setup can be easy while serious incident handling remains unproven. These are different jobs.

## Feature translation

- Uptime check: Better Stack asks a site for a valid response on a schedule.
- Heartbeat: a scheduled job sends a message when it finishes; silence can trigger an alert.
- Escalation: if the first person does not accept the alert, Better Stack contacts the next person.
- Logs and traces: records used to find the error behind an alert.
- Status page: a public page where customers can see current problems and updates.

## Missing evidence

No live incident established alert speed, delivery, false-alarm rate, search quality, support under pressure, or the cost of a real data load.
