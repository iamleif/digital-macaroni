The company previously operated as Better Uptime before rebranding and expanding its offerings under the Better Stack name. The product is available in both free and paid versions, with the free tier offering basic monitoring capabilities and limited data storage. The paid version provides advanced features such as incident response, status pages, and enhanced data retention options. The product's overall score is 8.3, indicating a generally positive reception among users. The onboarding process is rated highly, with users noting that setting up the basic features is straightforward. However, more complex configurations, such as setting up on-call schedules or integrating with multiple tools, can be time-consuming. The product itself is praised for its clean interface and the ability to centralize various monitoring functions. However, the effectiveness of the tool during real incidents depends on the integration and reliability of each component. Billing is a mixed area, with the free plan being generous but the paid options being difficult to predict in terms of cost due to the variability in data usage and additional features. Users generally praise the product for its simplicity and the value of the free tier, but there are repeated complaints about missing features, unexpected cost increases, and limitations in log management. The review highlights that while Better Stack is a good starting point for monitoring and a plausible tool for combining several operational tasks, it is important for teams to test the product in real scenarios and carefully evaluate the full cost of using the platform.
