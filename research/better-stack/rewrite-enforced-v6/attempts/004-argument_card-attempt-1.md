The free plan includes ten monitors, one status page, email and Slack alerts, and limited access to logs, traces, and metrics. Paid plans start at $34 monthly or $29 per responder monthly, with additional costs for features like security options, data retention, and ingestion. The product is praised for its quick setup, clean interface, and useful free plan. However, users report missing features, unpredictable pricing, and limitations in log management. These are common themes across independent reviews and community discussions. The lack of hands-on testing means that some aspects, like alert escalation during an actual incident or data retention costs, remain unverified."
