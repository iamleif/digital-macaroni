## Claims

- Better Uptime launched publicly on May 13, 2020 and later became part of Better Stack. [verified; fact; product-hunt]
- Better Stack combines outside uptime checks, alerts, on-call schedules, incident work, status pages, logs, traces, metrics, errors, and session replays. [verified; fact; official-pricing, official-monitoring]
- Free includes ten monitors or heartbeats, one status page, Slack and email alerts, and limited logs, traces, metrics, errors, and session replays. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Paid incident response starts at $34 monthly or $29 per responder monthly with annual billing. Other pages, security options, subscribers, checks, ingestion, and retention can add cost. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Quick setup, a clean interface, alerting, integrations, and the useful free plan are common strengths. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, product-hunt, gartner, reddit]
- Missing features, price jumps, limits, and log-management friction are repeated complaints. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, reddit]
