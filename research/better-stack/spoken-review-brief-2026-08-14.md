# Better Stack spoken review brief

## What is it?

Better Stack checks whether a site or service is working. When it fails, it can call the person responsible, move to someone else if they do not answer, show the technical records around the failure, and give customers a public page for updates.

## Who will find it useful?

Developers and small operations teams that want those jobs together. The free plan is also useful for a personal site that only needs basic checks.

## What is good?

Setup and the interface are widely praised. Keeping the alert, incident, logs, and customer update together can remove a lot of searching during a failure.

## What is wrong?

Easy setup does not prove that every alert will work during a rare serious incident. Logs and advanced tools also have limits. Paid cost can spread across responders, pages, checks, data volume, and retention.

## What does it cost?

Free includes ten checks, one status page, and limited data. A paid responder costs $34 monthly or $29 per month on annual billing. The full bill depends on pages, people, data, retention, and extra checks.

## Our answer

Cause a planned failure. Let the first person miss the alert and check that the next person is contacted. Find the failed request in the logs, post a public update, and close the incident. Then measure a normal week of data and build the full price. Buy Better Stack if that complete test is faster and clearer than the team's current setup.
