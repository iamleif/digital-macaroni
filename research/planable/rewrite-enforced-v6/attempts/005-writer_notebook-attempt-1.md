Its primary focus is on the approval process, making it a specialized tool for workflows that require client feedback before publishing. The product is available in two main pricing tiers: Basic and Pro, with additional add-ons for analytics and inbox capabilities. The Basic plan starts at $33 per workspace monthly and includes 60 posts and four social pages, while the Pro plan costs $49 and offers more posts, pages, and required approvals. Analytics and inbox features are available as add-ons with separate pricing. The inbox, listening, and analytics features are considered less comprehensive than those found in broader social media management suites. Overall, Planable is well-suited for users who need a focused approval workflow and value simplicity in their social media management process. The product’s strengths lie in its approval and preview capabilities, but its limitations may make it unsuitable for more complex or large-scale social media operations.
