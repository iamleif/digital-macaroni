Its main appeal is its streamlined approval workflow, which allows teams to send drafts to clients or stakeholders for review before posting. However, Planable lacks the full range of social media management features found in more comprehensive platforms. If your main goal is to simplify the approval process and you don’t need advanced analytics or inbox capabilities, Planable can be a good fit. But it may not be sufficient for teams that require broader social media management functions. Some users have reported that the limited inbox and analytics features are a drawback, especially when working across multiple platforms. It allows teams to draft content, send it to clients or stakeholders for review, and then schedule it for publication. This process is designed to be simple and efficient, reducing the need for back-and-forth communication. Users report that the ability to send drafts for approval through email, chat, or screenshots is a key advantage. However, the approval process is not fully automated, and teams must still manage the flow of feedback and revisions manually. While this can be a strength for agencies that want to maintain control over the creative process, it may also be a limitation for those who prefer more hands-off approval systems. Overall, the approval workflow is effective for its intended purpose but requires careful management to ensure smooth collaboration.\n\nPlanable’s scheduling and calendar view are relatively basic compared to other social media management tools. The platform allows users to schedule posts in advance and view them in a calendar format, which can help with planning and organizing content. However, the scheduling features are not as robust as those found in more comprehensive platforms. Users have noted that the calendar view is useful for visualizing the posting schedule but lacks advanced features such as recurring posts or detailed analytics. While this may be sufficient for some teams, others may find the limitations in scheduling and planning to be a drawback. If your workflow relies heavily on advanced scheduling capabilities, you may need to look for a more feature-rich alternative.\n\nThe limited inbox and analytics features are among the most frequently cited drawbacks of Planable. The inbox is not as comprehensive as those found in other social media management tools, and it lacks the ability to track engagement or monitor conversations in real time. Similarly, the analytics provided by Planable are basic and do not offer the same level of insight as those from more advanced platforms. Users have reported that these limitations can be a significant issue, especially when managing multiple social media accounts or analyzing campaign performance. While the basic analytics may be sufficient for some teams, others may find the lack of detailed reporting to be a major drawback. If your workflow requires in-depth analytics or a more robust inbox, Planable may not be the best fit.\n\nPlanable’s pricing model is straightforward but can be expensive for agencies that require multiple workspaces or add-ons.
