Planable is a social media management tool designed for brands and agencies that need a streamlined way to handle content approval and scheduling. It focuses on making the process of drafting, approving, and publishing content as simple as possible. The main feature that sets Planable apart is its approval workflow, which allows teams to send content drafts to clients or stakeholders for review before posting. This is especially useful for agencies that need to keep clients involved in the creative process. The tool also includes scheduling, a calendar view, and basic analytics, but it doesn’t offer the full range of social media management features found in more comprehensive platforms. If your main goal is to simplify the approval process and you don’t need advanced analytics or inbox capabilities, Planable can be a good fit. However, it may not be sufficient for teams that require broader social media management functions. Some users have reported that the limited inbox and analytics features are a drawback, especially when working across multiple platforms. A friend should check whether their workflow revolves around client approval and whether they can operate within the tool’s more limited feature set before committing to Planable.
