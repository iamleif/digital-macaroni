# Planable argument card

## Buyer

An agency or marketing team whose social approval process is scattered across email, chat, documents, and screenshots.

## Decision

Choose Planable when client feedback stays in the workspace and the approved version matches the live post.

## Why

Planable makes social drafts easy for clients to see, comment on, and approve. It is less complete as a listening, inbox, and reporting suite.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if clients keep approving in chat, key formats need unclear manual steps, account failures go unnoticed, or per-workspace costs erase the time saved.

## Verdict

The best approval tool when feedback stays inside.
