# Planable writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on drafting, approval, publishing, reconnection, analytics, inbox, billing, or support test. Nine sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Planable puts social drafts, previews, comments, approvals, and schedules into workspaces for each client or brand.

The buyer is an agency or marketing team losing time to feedback in email, chat, and screenshots. Clear previews, simple client approval, unlimited users, and a central calendar are the main strengths. Workspace pricing, channel limits, connection failures, add-ons, and lighter social management are the main concerns.

The recommendation is strong when Planable actually ends approval outside the workspace. Run a real campaign with the least technical approver, publish every important format, disconnect an account, and count escaped comments. Price every client workspace and needed add-on before buying.
