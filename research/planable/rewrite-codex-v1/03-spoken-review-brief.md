# Planable spoken review brief

Planable solves a very specific mess. The post sits in one place with its preview, comments, version, approval, and date. That is much cleaner than chasing feedback across email and chat.

It works only if the client uses it. I would invite the least technical approver and run a real week of content. Change a post after approval, publish every format, and disconnect an account.

Then I would count every comment that escaped into chat and price all client workspaces. Planable earns its fee when those outside comments disappear and the live post matches the approved one.
