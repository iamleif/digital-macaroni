# Planable spoken review brief

## What is it?

Planable gives each client or brand a place to draft social posts, preview them, comment, approve the final version, and schedule it.

## Who will find it useful?

An agency or marketing team currently sending screenshots and spreadsheets to clients for approval.

## What is good?

Clients can see a close preview and leave feedback beside the post. Approval status, versions, calendars, and separate workspaces keep several brands from becoming mixed together.

## What is wrong?

Each client or brand normally needs a paid workspace. Analytics and the inbox cost extra. Network disconnections and format limits still exist, and it is not as deep as a full listening and reporting suite.

## What does it cost?

The first 50 posts are free. Basic is $33 per workspace monthly and Pro is $49. Analytics is $14 monthly per workspace, and the inbox is $9, with lower displayed annual rates.

## Our answer

Use one real campaign. Let the least technical client request a change and approve it. Disconnect an account, publish every important format, and compare each live post with the approved version. Keep Planable if feedback stops arriving elsewhere.
