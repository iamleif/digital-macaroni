# Planable writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no hands-on workspace
- Pricing checked 2026-08-14

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: an agency or brand whose social drafts are approved through email, chat, documents, and screenshots
- Plain example: show a client how a post will look, receive one clear correction, record approval, and publish the right version
- Cost of failure: the client approves an old version, a private note becomes visible, the network rejects the post, or a disconnected account misses its slot

## Product facts

- Separate workspace for each client, brand, or location
- Feed, calendar, grid, and list views depending on plan
- Comments, suggestions, version history, internal notes, optional, required, and multi-level approvals
- Basic $33 per workspace monthly: 60 posts, four pages, unlimited users
- Pro $49: 150 posts, ten pages, required approval, API
- First 50 posts free
- Analytics $14 monthly or $12 at annual rate per workspace
- Inbox $9 monthly or $7.50 at annual rate per workspace

## Experience records

### A client approved the preview without a spreadsheet

- The clearest and broadest praise
- Consequence: test with the least technical real approver

### A scheduled format looked different after publishing

- Platform-specific publishing limits are a normal and reported risk
- Consequence: test every important format on every network

### Five clients made the price jump

- Workspace pricing is a common concern
- Consequence: calculate brands, locations, add-ons, and growth, not user seats alone

## Feature translation

### Workspace

- A separate room for one client, brand, or location with its own pages, people, calendar, and approval rules

### Required approval

- The post cannot be published until an assigned approver accepts it

### Multi-level approval

- Approval moves through an ordered chain, such as writer, brand manager, client, then legal

### Social inbox

- One place to read and answer comments and direct messages from connected networks

## Conflict ledger

- The strong approval route does not guarantee complete reporting, listening, or inbox work.
- The preview reduces ambiguity, but the social network owns the final published format.
- Unlimited people keep client access simple, while each client workspace and add-on costs more.
- Central feedback works only if people stop replying in email and chat.
