# Planable private argument card

- Buyer: an agency or brand with several people reviewing social content
- Job: move one draft through feedback and recorded approval into the correct published post
- Thesis: Planable is one of the clearest tools for client approval, but it should be bought for that job rather than treated as a complete listening, inbox, and analytics suite
- Main reason to buy: realistic previews and simple approval keep the latest version and client decision together
- Main reason to hesitate: cost grows by workspace and add-on, while social API limits and disconnections still affect publishing
- When it is unnecessary: one person creates and publishes content without a review chain
- Decision rule: run one real campaign across every required post format, invite the least technical approver, force a revision after approval, disconnect an account, publish, compare the live post with the approved preview, and count every correction made outside Planable
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni workspace or publication test
