# TypingMind spoken review brief

## What is it?

TypingMind gives you one chat screen for several AI services. Instead of paying TypingMind for the AI itself, you connect accounts from companies such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or OpenRouter.

## Who will find it useful?

Someone who already switches between models and wants the chats, prompts, and projects in one place.

## What is good?

The interface is cleaner and more organized than keeping several AI tabs open. Users repeatedly praise its folders, saved prompts, model choice, and steady updates.

## What is wrong?

The pricing is easy to misunderstand. The one-time license pays for TypingMind's interface. The AI companies still charge for usage, and larger cloud storage can add another payment. Setup also requires creating and connecting API keys.

## What does it cost?

The current one-time prices are $39 for Standard, $79 for Extended, and $99 for Premium. API use and additional cloud storage are separate.

## Our answer

TypingMind is worth considering if you already use several models. Start with its basic access and connect one provider before buying. If you only use one AI service and want a predictable monthly bill, use that service's own app.
