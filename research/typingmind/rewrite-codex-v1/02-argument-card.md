# Argument card

- Thesis: TypingMind is a strong daily workspace for people who already know why they need several models.
- Best for: experienced AI users who want control and organization.
- Skip: beginners who want one account, one bill, and a simple chat app.
- Price anchor: Standard $39, Extended $79, Premium $99 one time during research.
- Cost warning: provider API use and optional larger cloud storage are separate.
- Verdict: Powerful multi-model chat, with costs and setup kept separate.
