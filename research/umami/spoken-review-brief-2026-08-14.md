# Umami spoken review brief

Umami tells a website owner how many people visited, where they came from, which pages they opened, and whether they did something useful. It is much easier to read than Google Analytics for ordinary website questions.

It is especially useful for a developer or small team that wants to keep the data on its own server. People who do not want to run a server can use Umami Cloud instead.

The clean dashboard is good. It gets through traffic, campaigns, goals, and basic conversion paths without asking someone to learn an analytics product first. It becomes limited when a product team wants detailed behavior, unusual grouping, or complex credit for a sale.

Self-hosting is manageable for someone already comfortable with Docker and databases. It is not a free managed service. The owner must back it up, update it, monitor it, and repair it.

Before paying or moving the main reports, send known traffic and events through Umami. Compare signups and sales with the actual account and payment records. If self-hosting, delete a test copy and restore it from backup.
