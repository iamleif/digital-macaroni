# Umami writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review. Digital Macaroni did not use Umami.
- Official documentation, repository, two review platforms, three Reddit discussions, the founder's launch post, and one recent hands-on comparison were studied.
- The formal review sample is small. Community material strongly favors developers and self-hosters.

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a site owner or small software team that wants understandable traffic and conversion numbers without Google Analytics.
- Main job: see visits, sources, pages, campaigns, and chosen actions without building a detailed profile of each visitor.
- Failure cost: marketing choices are made from wrong counts, an important event is missing, or a self-hosted instance loses data.

## Product facts

- Umami can be self-hosted or used through its managed cloud service.
- Standard tracking uses no cookies or fingerprinting and the script is under 2KB.
- Reports include ordinary traffic data, custom events, goals, funnels, journeys, retention, campaign tags, revenue, sessions, and an API.
- The Cloud Hobby plan is free. Paid trials last 14 days. Usage counts page hits, custom events, and stored event properties.
- Cloud data can be exported and cloud servers are available in the United States and European Union.

## Experience records

- G2 and Product Hunt reviewers repeatedly value a dashboard that is quicker to understand than GA4. Both samples are small, and Product Hunt is unusually favorable.
- Self-hosting discussions describe Docker setup and day-to-day use as manageable for technical users. Those users already understand servers and databases.
- Recent production accounts praise low resource use and a clean dashboard. They also say detailed product analysis and some API documentation fall short.
- A recent independent comparison supports Umami for people who want the full product under their control, while finding another product easier for some reports.

## Repeated patterns

- Simplicity helps a small team answer ordinary website questions quickly.
- The same simplicity becomes a limit when a company needs deep product behavior, complex attribution, or unusual reports.
- Self-hosting is not difficult by infrastructure standards, but the customer owns backups, upgrades, security, monitoring, and recovery.
- Privacy is a strong reason to choose Umami. The rest of the website can still collect personal data through other tools or careless custom events.

## Feature translation

- Web analytics: counts visits and shows which pages and links brought people to the site.
- Custom event: a chosen action such as submitting a form, starting signup, or buying.
- Funnel: the number of people who complete each step in a fixed route.
- Self-hosting: running Umami and its database on servers the customer controls.
- Attribution: deciding which campaign or source deserves credit for a signup or sale.

## Conflict ledger

- “Umami is easy to understand” and “Umami is too shallow” can both be true because different teams ask different questions.
- “Self-hosting is manageable” and “self-hosting creates work” can both be true because installation is only one part of operating a service.
- “Umami is privacy focused” and “a site may still need consent or legal review” can both be true because Umami is only one part of a website.

## Missing evidence

- No independent count comparison against server logs, signups, or payments.
- No backup restoration or version upgrade.
- No direct cloud billing or support experience.
- No legal review of a specific installation.
