# Spoken review brief

Umami answers normal website questions in a clean dashboard and tracks chosen actions without cookies or visitor fingerprints in its standard setup.

Recommend it for a small team that wants traffic, campaigns, forms, signups, and sales without a large analytics product. Tell a product team with detailed account history and feature-use questions to look elsewhere.

Run it beside trusted records. Cloud removes server work. Self-hosting adds backups, upgrades, security, and recovery. State that no new hands-on test was run.
