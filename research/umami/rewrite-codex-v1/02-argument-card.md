# Argument card

- Thesis: Umami is a strong choice when a team wants understandable private analytics and can live with lighter reports.
- Best for: company sites, newsletters, help sites, and small products.
- Skip: teams that need long user histories, complex behavior groups, deep attribution, or advanced product analysis.
- Hosting choice: Cloud for low upkeep; self-host for a clear control need and a team able to restore it.
- Verdict: Clear private analytics, limited for deep product questions.
