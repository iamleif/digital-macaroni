Cloud usage includes page hits and custom event data, with export options available. Self-hosting requires a database and production setup, but deployment is manageable for technical users via Docker. The product is designed for websites that prioritize privacy and simplicity, with a clean dashboard and low resource use. It is suitable for small to medium sites that do not require complex reporting or attribution analysis. The open-source community and documentation cover basic use cases, but advanced API guidance is mixed. No hands-on testing was conducted, and no support requests were evaluated. The product is not ideal for teams needing detailed behavior analysis or complex attribution tracking. Self-hosters must consider backup, restore, upgrade, and monitoring before relying on Umami as a primary analytics tool. The tool is best used alongside existing analytics systems to compare real-world outcomes. The product is not a complete replacement for more advanced analytics platforms but offers a private, easy-to-read alternative for simpler tracking needs."
