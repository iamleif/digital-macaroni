# Umami argument card

- Buyer: site owners and small technical teams that want private, understandable web analytics.
- Job: count traffic, sources, campaigns, and selected actions without Google Analytics or visitor profiles.
- Thesis: Umami is one of the better simple analytics choices when data control matters, but it should not be mistaken for a deep product analytics system.
- Reason to buy: clean reports, light tracking, cloud and self-hosted options, and control over the data.
- Reason to hesitate: complicated reports run out of room, and self-hosting transfers every production responsibility to the customer.
- Decision rule: run a known set of visits and events through Umami, compare the important counts with real business records, and prove restore and upgrade if self-hosting.
- Evidence limit: no Digital Macaroni hands-on test; small formal review samples and a technically skewed community sample.
