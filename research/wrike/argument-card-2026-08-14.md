# Wrike argument card

- Buyer: a growing service, marketing, operations, or project team with several kinds of work.
- Job: collect requests, assign work, manage review, and report progress in one place.
- Thesis: Wrike is useful for complex work shared across teams, but it needs a clear setup owner and can be expensive for a small group.
- Reason to buy: forms, proofing, flexible work steps, dashboards, and reports can replace scattered tracking.
- Reason to hesitate: the product is easy to overbuild, and the final seat quote can be much higher than the headline price suggests.
- Decision rule: run one complete job from request through approval with real users, then price the exact seats and add-ons.
- Evidence limit: no hands-on trial; public support feedback is mixed and current packaging is new.
