# Dialpad writer's notebook

- Buyer: a sales or service team that needs calls, texts, and useful follow-up records.
- Job: turn conversations into notes and next actions without losing phone reliability.
- Best evidence: clear-call accounts, transcripts, CRM links, and stated security controls.
- Main risks: blocked texts, slow support, contract confusion, and billing after cancellation.
- Evidence limit: no hands-on test and incomplete price evidence.
- Trial rule: run real calls and texts, open a hard support ticket, and rehearse cancellation.
