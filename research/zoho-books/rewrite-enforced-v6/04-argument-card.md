# Private argument card

- Buyer: a growing small business willing to set up its books carefully
- Job: gain accounting depth without paying a premium price
- Thesis: Zoho Books offers excellent depth and value, but setup, local tax fit, and skilled bookkeeping help decide whether it succeeds

- Reason to hesitate: migration work, learning curve, complex tax edge cases, and accountant availability
- Decision rule: keep it only when a closed month matches the old system and the needed staff and local tax steps fit one plan
