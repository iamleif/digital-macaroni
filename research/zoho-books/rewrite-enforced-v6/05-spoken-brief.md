# Spoken review brief

Zoho Books gives a small business much more than invoices. It can handle bank matching, reports, bills, purchasing, stock, and local tax work, often for less than better-known rivals.

The tradeoff is setup. Country editions differ, opening balances must be right, and the business may need a bookkeeper who already knows Zoho Books. Some unusual tax cases can still require hand work.

I would move one closed month into a trial and match every balance. Keep it when the local tax steps work and all required staff fit one sensible plan.
