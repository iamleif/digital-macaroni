# Zoho Books writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Review type: research-based; no hands-on product access
- Official sources: product site, local pricing, and documentation
- Independent sources: G2, Capterra, and a migration discussion
- Main limit: editions and tax features differ by country, and support was not tested directly

## Reader and job

- Buyer: a growing small business that needs real accounting depth at a fair price
- Job: manage invoices, bills, bank matching, reports, tax work, purchasing, or stock without an early migration
- Cost of failure: wrong opening balances, a poor local-edition fit, or no bookkeeper able to maintain the setup

## Decision evidence

- Reviewers praise invoices, expense tracking, bank matching, local tax work, and the clean dashboard.
- Complex GST cases may still require manual work.
- A migration discussion highlights the need to find a bookkeeper familiar with Zoho Books.
- The US free plan serves firms below $50,000 in revenue; Standard is $20 monthly and Professional is $50.
- Plan limits and local editions are published clearly enough to model before purchase.

## Evidence boundary

The review uses US pricing only where labeled and tells readers to confirm their local edition.
