## Claims

- Publer schedules content for Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Google Business, YouTube, WordPress, TikTok, Telegram, Mastodon, Threads, and Bluesky. [verified; fact; official-networks]
- Free supports three social accounts, ten scheduled posts at a time per account, and 24 hours of post history. X requires a paid plan. [verified; fact; official-networks, official-pricing]
- Professional starts at $5 monthly and Business at $10, but the total changes with social accounts, members, and billing period. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- A clean calendar, broad network support, bulk scheduling, and repeated-post tools are dominant strengths. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, product-hunt, capterra, gartner]
- Reporting is often described as less capable than publishing, while agency permissions and approvals can be limited. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, capterra]
- Some post types require a phone reminder or have network-specific restrictions imposed by the social network. [verified; fact; official-post-types]
