Publer is a social media scheduling tool that lets you plan and post content across multiple platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and X. It’s aimed at individuals and small teams who want to manage their social media without complicated software. The main feature is its simple calendar interface, which lets you schedule posts in bulk and repeat content automatically. This makes it good for people who post regularly but don’t want to spend a lot of time managing their accounts. It’s also affordable, with a free plan that supports three accounts and basic scheduling. However, it lacks advanced analytics, team collaboration tools, and detailed reporting, which are important for businesses or agencies. Some users have noted that certain post types require manual checks or are restricted by the social platforms themselves. If you need a straightforward tool for basic scheduling, Publer is good enough. But if you need deeper insights or more control over your content, you might want to look elsewhere. Before paying, check the pricing carefully, as the total cost depends on the number of accounts and team members. Also, be aware that some features may be limited by the social networks themselves.
