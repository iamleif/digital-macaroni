# Publer spoken review brief

## What is it?

Publer lets you prepare posts for several social profiles in one place and choose when each one goes live. You can see the week on a calendar, change the wording for each network, upload many posts at once, and repeat posts that stay useful.

## Who will find it useful?

Creators, freelancers, and small marketing teams that plan posts in batches. It matters less if you run one profile and normally post in the social app.

## What is good?

Scheduling is the strongest part. The calendar is easy to understand, network coverage is broad, and bulk or repeated posting can remove a lot of weekly clicking.

## What is wrong?

Some posts cannot be published fully from any outside tool, so Publer may send a phone reminder instead. Reports are more basic than the scheduling tools, and larger agencies may want stricter permissions and approvals.

## What does it cost?

Free covers three profiles with ten posts waiting per profile and no X account. Professional starts at $5 monthly and Business at $10, but profiles and teammates change the total. Build the price with the real account list.

## Our answer

Use Free or a trial to schedule one real week. Include every network, a video, and one post type that often has platform limits. Change the words for each network instead of copying one caption everywhere. If a team is involved, run one approval and export one report. Choose Publer when the publishing works cleanly; do not assume the reports are equally strong.
