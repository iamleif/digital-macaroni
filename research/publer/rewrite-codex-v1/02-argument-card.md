# Publer argument card

## Buyer

A creator or small team that plans a week or month of social posts in one sitting.

## Decision

Choose Publer when batch scheduling saves time and every important format reaches the live network correctly.

## Why

Its calendar, network range, bulk tools, and repeated posts cover publishing well at a low starting price. Reporting and agency controls are lighter.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if key formats need missed phone steps, failed connections go unnoticed, client approval lacks control, reports need rebuilding, or profile pricing grows too far.

## Verdict

Strong batch publishing with lighter agency controls.
