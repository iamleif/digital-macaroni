# Publer spoken review brief

Publer is at its best when someone prepares a week of social posts at once. The calendar, broad network list, bulk upload, and repeat rules can remove a lot of routine work.

The live networks still decide which formats publish directly. Some posts need a phone reminder, and account links can fail. Reports and approvals also need a closer look from agencies.

I would schedule one real week, disconnect a profile, and run a client approval. Then I would build the expected report and price every profile plus teammate.
