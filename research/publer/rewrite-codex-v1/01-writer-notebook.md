# Publer writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on publishing, mobile, approval, report, billing, or support test. Ten sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Publer schedules posts across a wide set of social networks and adds a calendar, bulk upload, repeated posts, analytics, and team features.

The buyer is a creator, freelancer, or small team that prepares social posts in batches. The calendar, wide network support, bulk scheduling, repeated-post tools, and low entry price are the main strengths. Network rules, reminder posts, reports, agency permissions, approvals, and variable account pricing are the main concerns.

The recommendation is strong for batch publishing. Schedule one real week with every important format and profile. Test a failed connection, one client approval, and the report people expect. Price all accounts and team members before buying.
