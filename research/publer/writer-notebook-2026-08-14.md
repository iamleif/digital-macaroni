# Publer writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no publishing test
- Pricing and network rules checked 2026-08-14
- G2: 4.7 from 500; Product Hunt: 4.9 from 55; Capterra sample: 20
- Promotional community participation was discounted

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a creator, freelancer, or small marketing team that prepares posts for several profiles at once
- Plain example: write next week's posts on Monday, adapt the text for LinkedIn and Instagram, see each date on a calendar, and let Publer publish them
- Cost of failure: a post fails, uses the wrong format, repeats too soon, or goes to the wrong client profile

## Product facts

- Supports 13 named network or publishing services
- Can schedule, queue, bulk upload, recycle, and customize a post for each network
- Free: three profiles, ten scheduled posts waiting per profile, 24-hour history, no X
- Professional starts at $5 monthly before account and member additions
- Business starts at $10 monthly and adds deeper reports, best-time suggestions, competitor and hashtag details, and repeated-post controls
- Some post types publish directly; others use a phone reminder because the network API does not allow full automation

## Experience records

### A week of posts was prepared at once

- Source: dominant G2, Product Hunt, and Capterra pattern
- Action: choose profiles, adapt each version, and place posts on the calendar
- Result: less daily switching between social apps
- Consequence: Publer is strongest for people who already work in batches

### The same useful post was repeated

- Source: repeated review pattern and official feature
- Action: set a post to recycle or fill an empty time slot
- Result: evergreen material can return without being rebuilt
- Consequence: useful for recurring tips, events, or offers; poor for news or dated posts

### Reporting or approval needed more control

- Source: G2 and Capterra criticism
- Result: basic performance numbers were useful, but custom reports, roles, or approval steps were not enough for some larger teams
- Consequence: agencies should trial one client approval and one monthly report before moving

## Feature translation

### Queue

- What it does: keeps a list of posts and sends the next one at a chosen time

### Recycling

- What it does: schedules the same lasting post again later
- Main limit: set an end date and do not recycle news, expired offers, or personal announcements

### Bulk scheduling

- What it does: uploads and schedules many posts from one file or work session
- Main limit: each network version still needs a visual and text check

## Conflict ledger

- Publer covers many networks, but each network still decides what outside apps may publish.
- Publishing is the strongest part; reporting and agency permissions are less deep.
- Low starting prices are real, but the bill grows with profiles and people.
