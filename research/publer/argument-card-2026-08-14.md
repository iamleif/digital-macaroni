# Publer private argument card

- Buyer: a creator, freelancer, or small team that publishes to several social profiles
- Job: prepare posts in batches and publish them at the right time without opening every social app
- Thesis: Publer is a strong, affordable scheduler when publishing is the main job; its reporting and agency controls deserve a separate test
- Main reason to buy: broad network support, a clear calendar, bulk scheduling, and safe use of repeated posts
- Main reason to hesitate: platform limits still apply, and reports or approvals may be too basic for larger teams
- When it is unnecessary: only one profile is active or posts are mostly made live inside the social app
- Decision rule: schedule one real week across every important profile, customize each network version, test one video and one special post type, run one approval, and build one report before paying
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on publishing test
