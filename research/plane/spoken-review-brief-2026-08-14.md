# Plane spoken review brief

## What is it?

Plane tracks tasks, bugs, projects, fixed work periods, feature groups, documents, requests, and progress. Teams can use its cloud or run versions on their own infrastructure.

## Who will find it useful?

A software or product team that wants a cleaner alternative to a heavy issue tracker, especially when hosting control matters.

## What is good?

The interface is widely described as fast and modern. It has enough breadth for issues, planning, timelines, knowledge, intake, and developer connections without requiring a large first setup.

## What is wrong?

Reporting, mobile use, integrations, and customization may be lighter than mature rivals. Self-hosting adds upgrades, backups, recovery, and commercial feature boundaries.

## What does it cost?

Cloud Free supports up to 12 users. The displayed annual rates are $6 per seat monthly for Pro and $13 for Business. Self-hosted commercial and enterprise terms need a separate check.

## Our answer

Import one complete real project and run a full work cycle. Build the reports and use the phone. If self-hosting, upgrade a clone and restore both data and files after a failure. Choose Plane only after both the workflow and deployment survive those tests.
