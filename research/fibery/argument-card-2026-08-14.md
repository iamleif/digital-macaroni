# Fibery private argument card

- Buyer: a team with connected work that does not fit a rigid project or CRM tool
- Job: keep operational records, knowledge, and plans linked in one place
- Thesis: Fibery can replace several awkward handoffs, but only when the team treats workspace design as a real internal project with an owner
- Main reason to buy: relations connect customers, research, projects, tasks, goals, and documents without forcing one fixed process
- Main reason to hesitate: setup, teaching, maintenance, permissions, and reporting can consume the savings from tool consolidation
- When it is unnecessary: the team needs a normal task board, CRM, or wiki and does not need records linked across those jobs
- Decision rule: build one workflow that crosses two teams, including the hardest report and permission case, run it for two weeks, and ask whether the links removed real copying without confusing normal users
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on setup or migration
