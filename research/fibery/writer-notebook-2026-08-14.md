# Fibery writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no hands-on workspace
- Price and current plan limits checked 2026-08-14
- Review pools: 125 G2, 52 Capterra, 37 Product Hunt, 49 Software Finder, and community context

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a team whose projects, customers, research, goals, and documents need to link to one another
- Plain example: connect a customer interview to the customer, requested features, a product project, related tasks, and the final release note
- Cost of failure: the team builds a clever system nobody understands, permissions expose the wrong records, or ongoing changes turn the workspace into clutter

## Product facts

- User-defined databases and links between records
- Documents, whiteboards, tables, boards, calendars, timelines, charts, dashboards, forms, and automations
- Free for up to ten users with ten databases
- Standard €12 per paid user monthly billed yearly
- Pro €20; Enterprise €40 with at least 25 paid users
- Observers and guests free on paid plans
- Fourteen-day Pro trial
- Plan limits differ for automations, synced records, AI credits, history, permissions, and sharing

## Experience records

### A team connected work that lived in separate tools

- Large pools praise relational data and flexible workflows
- Consequence: Fibery is strongest when a link removes a real copy or handoff

### A team faced the blank-workspace problem

- Setup and learning curve are the main repeated complaint
- Consequence: prototype one workflow with a named owner before migration

### A specialist needed reporting, sharing, or planning depth

- Narrow complaints appear around dashboards, external sharing, permissions, and resource planning
- Consequence: build the hardest view and permission case during the trial

## Feature translation

### Database

- A list of one kind of thing, such as customers, projects, tasks, or interviews

### Relation

- A direct link between records in two lists, such as a task linked to its project and customer

### Entity

- One record in a database, such as one customer or one task

### Observer or guest

- A free limited user; observers see broadly while guests work with selected records

## Conflict ledger

- Flexibility solves unusual workflows and creates design work.
- One workspace can replace tools and become a hard-to-maintain internal product.
- Templates speed setup but should not decide the team's language or process.

## Missing evidence

- No model, import, automation, permission, share, report, AI, mobile, export, or support test
- No large-workspace performance test
- No migration time estimate

## Spoken language bank

- start with one link that removes real work
- do not move the whole company during the trial
- name the person who owns the design
- build the hardest permission case
- a flexible tool will not decide the process for you
