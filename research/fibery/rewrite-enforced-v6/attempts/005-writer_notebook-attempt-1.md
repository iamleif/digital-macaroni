  "content": "Fibery is a project management platform designed for teams that need a flexible, connected workspace. It allows users to build custom systems by integrating databases, documents, whiteboards, views, automations, and integrations. The product is positioned as a configurable solution, which means it can be tailored to fit a wide range of workflows, but this also introduces a learning curve. Fibery launched publicly on April 1, 2020, and offers a free tier with up to ten users and ten databases. Paid plans start at €12 per user for Standard, €20 for Pro, and €40 for Enterprise. Observers and guests are free on all paid plans, which is a notable feature for teams that need to share information without full access. The free tier is generous, but it lacks some advanced features like automation, sync, AI, and permission limits, which are available in higher-tier plans. The platform is praised for its flexibility and the ability to replace multiple narrow tools with a single integrated system. However, this flexibility comes at a cost, as users report a steep setup and learning curve. Teams must design their own work system, which can be time-consuming. The strongest repeated positives are the ability to link data, adapt workflows, and connect information across different formats. These features are particularly useful for teams that handle complex, interconnected tasks. On the other hand, some users point out that reporting, external sharing, permission clarity, notifications, and resource planning are weaker areas. These are narrower issues that may not affect all teams but are worth considering. Support is consistently mentioned as a strength, with users praising the responsiveness and quality of customer service. The product is best suited for teams that are willing to invest time in designing a shared operating system. Before adopting Fibery, it’s recommended to prototype one cross-team workflow to see if the platform meets the team’s needs. While the product is excellent for connected work that does not fit a rigid tool, it may be excessive for simpler tasks. Overall, Fibery is a strong option for teams looking for a flexible, integrated solution, but it requires careful planning and a commitment to customization."
