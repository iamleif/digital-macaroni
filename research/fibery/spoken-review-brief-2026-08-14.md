# Fibery spoken review brief

## What is it?

Fibery lets a team build its own connected work system. You create lists for things such as customers, projects, tasks, and research, then link the records so information does not sit in separate tools.

## Who will find it useful?

A product, operations, agency, or software team with a real cross-team process that ordinary task software cannot model cleanly.

## What is good?

The linked data is powerful, workflows can match the team, and one workspace may replace several narrow tools. Support is praised often.

## What is wrong?

The team must design, teach, and maintain the system. Reporting, sharing, permissions, notifications, and resource planning need careful testing. A simple task list does not justify this much setup.

## What does it cost?

Free supports up to ten users. With annual billing, Standard is €12 per user, Pro €20, and Enterprise €40. Observers and guests are free on paid plans. The Pro trial lasts fourteen days.

## Our answer

Choose one workflow that crosses two teams. Build the real records, hardest report, and hardest permission case. Run it for two weeks. Move further only if the links remove copying and normal teammates can use the result without its designer beside them.
