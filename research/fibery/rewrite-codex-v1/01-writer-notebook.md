# Fibery writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on workspace, migration, automation, permission, report, or support test. Seven sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Fibery connects databases, documents, whiteboards, views, rules, and integrations in a workspace a team designs.

The buyer has important work crossing a project tool, CRM, wiki, or research store. Linked data, adaptable processes, connected information, tool replacement, support, and product improvement are repeated strengths. Setup and learning are the main cost. Reporting, outside sharing, permissions, alerts, and resource planning are narrower weak spots.

The recommendation is to prototype one cross-team process with real records, a useful rule, the hardest report, and the hardest access case. Normal users must succeed without the designer beside them.
