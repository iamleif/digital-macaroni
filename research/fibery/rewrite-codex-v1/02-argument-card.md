# Fibery argument card

## Buyer

A team whose customers, projects, research, and decisions are copied between focused tools.

## Decision

Use Fibery when one connected cross-team process proves that links remove copying. Skip it for a standard task list.

## Why

Fibery can model unusual work and keep related records together. The team must design, name, govern, and teach the system it creates.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if normal users depend on the builder, a key report or access rule is weak, exports are hard to use, or the links do not remove real work.

## Verdict

Flexible connected workspaces that need a dedicated owner.
