# TidyCal spoken review brief

TidyCal gives a person or team a booking page that shows open times from connected calendars. Its low one-time prices are the attraction. Free costs nothing, Individual Lifetime is $29, Agency Lifetime is $79, and Pro is $12 monthly or $99 yearly. That price does not matter if a blocked hour appears as open. Make twenty test bookings across every calendar, time zone, meeting link, payment, team route, cancellation, and reschedule. Keep it only if the booking page never offers a time that should be closed and every person receives the right notice.
