# TidyCal private argument card

- Buyer: a solo operator or small team that needs a simple public booking page
- Job: turn true availability into a confirmed meeting without manual email or calendar repair
- Thesis: TidyCal is unusually cheap and capable, but the recommendation depends on calendar reliability rather than the lifetime price
- Main reason to buy: free or one-time pricing for useful booking pages and calendar connections
- Main reason to hesitate: sync failures, limited customization, team routing risk, and changing plan boundaries
- Decision rule: make twenty bookings across every calendar, time zone, meeting type, payment state, team route, cancellation, and reschedule; deliberately block a slot elsewhere and confirm it can never be booked
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni booking test
