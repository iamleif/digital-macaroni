# Writer notebook

## Buyer and job

TidyCal creates booking pages, checks connected calendars, adds events, takes paid bookings, and supports basic team scheduling. Its low free and lifetime prices appeal to solo operators and small teams.

## Thesis

The low price matters only after calendar sync, time zones, buffers, payments, messages, and team routing survive a real booking test.

## Best fit

A solo operator with a straightforward calendar and a small team with simple round-robin or collective meetings.

## Skip

Deep qualification, complex routing, strict service promises, or any setup that fails the twenty-booking test.

## Evidence limits

No calendar, booking, payment, routing, billing, or support test was run. Public reports cover different product versions.

## Decisive test

Make twenty bookings across every real calendar, time zone, meeting type, payment state, host, cancellation, and reschedule. Try to book a blocked slot.
