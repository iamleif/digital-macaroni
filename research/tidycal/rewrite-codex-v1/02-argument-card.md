# Argument card

- Thesis: TidyCal is excellent value only when it protects unavailable time without human checking.
- Strength: inexpensive booking pages with useful solo and basic team features.
- Risk: sync faults, booking failures, time zones, and team rules can create costly calendar mistakes.
- Buyer: solo operators and small teams with clear scheduling rules.
- Skip: complicated qualification, routing, or strict reliability needs.
- Price anchor: Free $0, Individual Lifetime $29, Agency Lifetime $79, Pro $12 monthly or $99 yearly during research.
- Extra cost: paid bookings add a 1% platform fee plus processing fees.
- Verdict: Cheap scheduling works only when closed time stays closed.
