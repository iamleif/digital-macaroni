# Spoken review brief

Open with the booking tool's one hard job: unavailable time must never be offered. Explain that TidyCal is unusually cheap and still needs the same reliability test as a costly scheduler.

Build the review around twenty bookings. Cover calendar holds, buffers, daily limits, two calendars, time zones, video links, payment failures, team assignment, cancellations, and rescheduling.

Explain the buyer split. Free and Individual Lifetime suit a simple solo calendar. Agency can suit a small team. Pro earns its cost through branding, domains, and priority support only when those jobs matter.

State the 1% paid-booking fee and the lack of a new hands-on test.
