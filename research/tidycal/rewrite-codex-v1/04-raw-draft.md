TidyCal lets customers book open time from a public page. It checks connected calendars, creates the event, and sends the details. The free and lifetime plans make it much cheaper than many schedulers.

That price is useful only when closed time stays closed. I recommend TidyCal for a simple solo calendar after twenty clean test bookings.

## Test the calendar first

Add private, all-day, repeating, tentative, and last-minute events. Decide which ones block time. Create a 30-minute meeting with buffers, one-day notice, and a daily limit.

Open the public page while signed out. Book beside every hold. Add a second calendar, move an event, delete it, and check how fast the booking page changes.

## Cross time zones

Book between two real regions. Test daylight-saving weeks, a device with the wrong zone, and working hours around midnight. The page, email, event, reminder, video link, cancellation, and reschedule must show the same moment.

## Treat each booking type separately

A free call, paid consultation, class, and recurring session need different rules. Check duration, place, notice, buffer, questions, price, seats, and cancellation.

Keep deep qualification outside the scheduler. Test the last seat, a failed payment, and one change inside a recurring series.

## Check links and team routes

Book through each video service the business uses. Reconnect access, reschedule, and cancel. The host and guest should receive one matching link.

For round robin, create hosts with different calendars and priorities. Book ten times, remove a host, and disconnect one calendar. Ownership must stay clear.

## Paid bookings add a fee

TidyCal adds a 1% platform fee to the booker's total, alongside processing fees. Pay, fail, reschedule, cancel, and refund a safe test. The calendar, payment account, messages, and final total must agree.

## Plans are cheap for different reasons

Free includes one calendar connection and unlimited booking types. Individual Lifetime was $29 once and added more calendars, video links, groups, questions, limits, and analytics.

Agency Lifetime was $79 and added team scheduling and more calendars. Pro was $12 monthly or $99 yearly, with reduced branding, a custom domain, and priority support. Confirm today's plan table before buying.

## Who should choose TidyCal?

Choose it for straightforward solo scheduling. Consider Agency for a small team whose routing stays simple. Skip it when qualification, routing, or service promises are complex.

Digital Macaroni did not connect a calendar, book a meeting, take payment, test a team route, change a plan, or contact support.

Make twenty real-route bookings. Keep TidyCal when blocked time remains blocked and every person receives the correct event.
