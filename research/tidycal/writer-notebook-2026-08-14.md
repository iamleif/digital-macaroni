# TidyCal writer's notebook

- Reader: solo operator or small team that wants people to book without email back-and-forth
- Job: show only real open times, collect the right details, send the right notices, take payment if needed, and update every calendar
- Failure: double booking, wrong time zone, missing meeting link, paid booking without notice, wrong team member, or failed cancellation
- Free: unlimited bookings and types, one calendar, paid and recurring bookings
- Individual Lifetime: $29 once, ten calendars, meeting links, group bookings, custom mail and analytics
- Agency Lifetime: $79 once, 25 calendars, teams, round robin, collective meetings, SMS in US and Canada, reduced branding
- Pro: $12 monthly or $99 yearly, Agency features, no branding, custom domain, priority support
- Thesis: TidyCal is good value only after the real calendars prove that closed time stays closed
- Decision language: make twenty bookings; block the slot elsewhere; cross midnight; pay and refund; remove a team member
