## Claims

- TidyCal 2.0 launched publicly on August 18, 2021. [verified; fact; product-hunt]
- TidyCal creates booking pages, checks connected calendars, accepts paid bookings, and supports individual and team scheduling. [verified; fact; pricing]
- Low cost, basic setup, calendar sync, and simple booking links are the main positive patterns. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, capterra, trustpilot]
- Customization, team depth, integration reliability, and booking failures are the key reported limits. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, reddit-google, reddit-limits]
- Free costs $0. Individual Lifetime costs $29 once. Agency Lifetime costs $79 once. Pro costs $12 monthly or $99 yearly. [verified; fact; pricing]
- Paid bookings add a 1% TidyCal platform fee to the booker's total, in addition to payment processing fees. [verified; fact; pricing]
