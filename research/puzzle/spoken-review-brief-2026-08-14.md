# Puzzle spoken review brief

## What is it?

Puzzle is the accounting ledger and founder dashboard for a startup. It connects banks, cards, payments, payroll, and other systems, then helps categorize entries, reconcile balances, close the month, and produce statements plus cash, burn, runway, and revenue views.

## Who will find it useful?

An early-stage startup with fairly straightforward books and an accountant who is willing to review a newer ledger.

## What is good?

Founders get a clear view of the business. Modern startup connections and automatic work may reduce copying and make the close faster.

## What is wrong?

A clean dashboard can still rest on a wrong category or date. Accountants report mixed gaps in controls, corrections, and reporting. Public independent evidence is thin for the risk.

## What does it cost?

Yearly list rates are $25 per month for Starter, $60 for Core, $100 for Complete, and $300 for Scale. Monthly rates are higher. The 14-day trial includes Complete, and current discounts have conditions.

## Our answer

Let the accountant close two real difficult months in Puzzle while the old ledger remains active. Reconcile every balance, test corrections and locked periods, match revenue to source systems, build tax and board reports, and export everything. Do not switch because the runway chart looks right.
