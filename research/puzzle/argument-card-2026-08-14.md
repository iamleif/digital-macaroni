# Puzzle private argument card

- Buyer: an early-stage startup with modern bank, card, payroll, and payment systems plus an accountant willing to review a new ledger
- Job: keep correct books, close the month, produce statements, and show founders cash, burn, runway, and revenue
- Thesis: Puzzle can make startup finance much easier to see, but founder clarity is not proof of accounting correctness
- Main reason to buy: connected startup systems, automatic categorization, real-time statements, revenue recognition, and founder metrics share one ledger
- Main reason to hesitate: thin independent evidence, category and correction errors, accountant control gaps, and less proof for complex entities
- When it is unnecessary: the current ledger closes cleanly and produces trusted startup reports at an acceptable cost
- Decision rule: have the accountant migrate and close two real ugly months in parallel; reconcile every bank and control account, test corrections and locks, match revenue schedules and source reports, produce tax and board packages, then export the whole ledger and audit history
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni books and no accounting or tax advice
