# Puzzle argument card

## Buyer

An early-stage startup that wants clear founder reporting and has a qualified accountant responsible for the books.

## Decision

Choose Puzzle only after an accountant closes and reconciles two hard months in both ledgers.

## Why

Modern startup connections and a clear dashboard can reduce copying and make cash, burn, runway, and statements easier to read. Clean presentation does not prove correct entries.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if categories repeat errors, balances do not reconcile, revenue schedules drift, locks and history are weak, required reports fail, or the full ledger cannot leave cleanly.

## Verdict

Clear startup books only after an accountant proves them.
