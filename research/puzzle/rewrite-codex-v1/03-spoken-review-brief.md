# Puzzle spoken review brief

Puzzle makes startup finance easier for a founder to read. It connects modern banks, cards, payroll, and payment tools, then shows cash, burn, runway, revenue, and statements.

The dashboard is the last thing I would trust. A wrong category or revenue date can flow into every clean chart. I would ask the accountant to close two ugly months in Puzzle and the old ledger.

Every account must reconcile and every report must export. Keep Puzzle only when the accountant can explain the differences and sign off on the result.
