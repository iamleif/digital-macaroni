## Feature translation

### General ledger

- The central record where every accounting entry lands and the statements are built

### Reconciliation

- Matching the books to an outside statement so the ending balance and every difference are explained

### Accrual accounting

- Recording income when earned and costs when owed, not only when cash moves

### Revenue recognition

- Spreading paid subscription or contract revenue across the period in which it is earned

## Conflict ledger

- Real-time reports are faster than month-end and only as correct as current transactions and policies.
- High automatic category rates reduce work but do not remove review.
- Startup-specific design helps founders and may omit controls a complex company or accounting firm needs.
- A time-saving guarantee measures customer-reported close time, not accounting correctness.
