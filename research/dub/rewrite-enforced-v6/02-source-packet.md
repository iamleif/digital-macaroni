## Claims

- Dub creates short links, changes where they send visitors, groups them, and records click data. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Free includes 25 new links and 1,000 tracked events each month, three custom domains, one user, and 30 days of data. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Pro is currently $30 monthly and adds 50,000 tracked events, 1,000 new links, three users, and one year of data. Business is $90 monthly and adds conversion tracking, partner payouts, and higher limits. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Conversion tracking needs an analytics script and server or app work to connect a click to a signup or sale. [verified; fact; official-tracking]
- Users repeatedly praise fast link creation, the modern interface, changing destinations, and readable analytics. [supported; reported-pattern; product-hunt, trustpilot]
- A few Trustpilot reviews report affiliate payout or support problems, but at least one complaint involved a reward set by the outside program owner rather than Dub. [supported; reported-pattern; trustpilot]
- Dub's core code is public and can be self-hosted, while a small set of enterprise features uses a commercial license. [verified; fact; github]
