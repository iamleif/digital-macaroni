Dub is a tool for creating short links, changing where they send visitors, grouping them, and tracking how many times they're clicked. It’s designed for people who need to manage links easily, especially for marketing or sharing content online. The main feature is the ability to create and customize short links quickly, which makes it useful for small teams or solo users who want to share links without making them long or complicated. If you need basic analytics and simple link management, Dub is good enough. However, if you rely on detailed tracking or advanced customization, the free version might fall short. Some users have reported issues with support and payout problems, but these are often linked to the affiliate programs rather than the service itself. A friend should check whether they need advanced tracking features or detailed analytics before deciding to pay for a higher tier. If they only need to manage and track basic link activity, Dub is a solid choice.
