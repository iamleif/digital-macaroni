# Dub argument card

## Buyer

A creator or marketing team whose public links remain in circulation and affect campaign or revenue decisions.

## Decision

Use Free to prove that destination changes and click reports matter. Pay only when volume, history, conversion tracking, or partner payouts have a clear business use.

## Why

A lasting redirect protects links that cannot be edited later. Clear analytics can answer useful campaign questions. Business features need developer setup and careful payout ownership.

## Walk-away conditions

Skip paid Dub when links are temporary, reports do not change decisions, conversion data cannot be checked, or nobody owns partner support and payout rules.

## Verdict

Worth using when every shared link affects revenue.
