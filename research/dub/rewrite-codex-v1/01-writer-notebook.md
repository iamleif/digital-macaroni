# Dub writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on redirect, analytics, or payout test. Seven sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Dub creates short links, changes their destinations, groups them, and records clicks. Business adds conversion tracking and partner payouts. The core code is public and can be self-hosted.

The buyer is a creator or marketing team with links in places that are hard to edit. The strongest reason to choose Dub is keeping one public address while changing the page behind it. Fast link creation, a modern interface, destination changes, and readable reports are the strongest patterns.

Free is enough for light use. Pro adds volume and history. Business is the real starting point for sales tracking and partner programs, but conversion tracking needs site and server work. Affiliate complaints must separate Dub's platform duties from reward rules set by the outside program owner.
