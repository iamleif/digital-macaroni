# Dub spoken review brief

Dub is useful because it lets you change the page behind a short link after that link has spread. That matters for links in videos, printed material, old posts, and paid campaigns. The click reports can also show where useful traffic came from.

Free is enough to test the main job. Share a real link, change its destination, and see whether the report answers a business question. Pro buys more volume and history.

Business adds sales tracking and partner payouts. Sales tracking needs developer work, and payout disputes may belong to the company running the program instead of Dub. I would pay only when those jobs have a clear owner.
