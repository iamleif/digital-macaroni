# Dub spoken review brief

## What is it?

Dub makes short links. More importantly, it lets you keep the same short address, change the page behind it, and see where people clicked.

## Who will find it useful?

Creators and teams that put links in videos, profiles, ads, QR codes, or printed material that will be hard to change later.

## What is good?

The redirect and clear click report solve real problems. A creator can show a sponsor where an audience came from. A team can fix an old campaign link without editing every post.

## What is wrong?

The paid plans become hard to justify for light use. Sales tracking also needs the $90 Business plan and code on the website or server.

## What does it cost?

Free includes 25 new links and 1,000 tracked events each month. Pro is $30 monthly. Business is $90 and adds sales tracking and affiliate programs. A 14-day trial is listed for paid plans.

## Our answer

Start on Free with actual links. Upgrade only when the history, team, click, or sales limits stop a real campaign. Do not pay just to make a web address shorter.
