# Dub writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no Digital Macaroni product test
- Current Links pricing and tracking docs checked on 2026-08-14
- Product Hunt: 23 reviews at 5.0; Trustpilot: 22 reviews at 3.7
- Main limit: small samples and some affiliate reviews concern outside program owners

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a creator or marketing team sharing links in places that will be hard to edit later
- Plain example: a short printed link can keep the same address while its final page changes
- Wrong reader: someone who needs five ordinary links and does not care about click data
- Cost of failure: paying $90 for sales tracking before a developer connects it

## Product facts

- A short link points visitors to a longer page and can be redirected later
- Dub records when, where, and on what device a link was opened
- Free: 25 new links and 1,000 tracked events per month, one user, three custom domains, 30-day history
- Pro: $30 monthly, 1,000 links, 50,000 events, three users, one-year history
- Business: $90 monthly, sales and signup tracking, 250,000 events, affiliate program, 10 users
- Business partner payouts have a 5% fee and a $2,500 monthly payout limit
- Core code is open source and can be hosted by the user

## Experience records

### Destination changed after the link was shared

- Source: Trustpilot and Product Hunt
- Result: users value updating where a short link goes without replacing it everywhere
- Consequence: useful for old videos, social profiles, printed material, and sponsor campaigns

### Creator proved where clicks came from

- Source: Trustpilot
- Result: creator with a large YouTube audience used location and source data to show brands the audience quality
- Consequence: analytics matter when click evidence affects a deal

### Affiliate payout caused confusion

- Source: Trustpilot
- Result: one user reported a missing invoice and slow reply; another blamed Dub for a reward amount that Dub said the outside program owner had changed
- Consequence: separate platform support, program rules, and payout documents before assigning blame

## Repeated patterns

- Fast link creation and destination changes are practical strengths
- The interface and readable click data receive repeated praise
- Revenue and affiliate tracking need higher plans and more setup
- Price is hard to justify for a few long-lived links

## Feature translation

### Redirect

- What it does: keeps the short address the same while changing the final page
- Who cares: anyone who already printed or published the short link
- Main limit: the link depends on Dub and the custom domain staying active

### Conversion tracking

- What it does: connects an earlier click to a later signup or sale
- Main limit: requires Business and work on the website or server

### Partner program

- What it does: gives affiliates their own links, records sales, and pays rewards
- Main limit: fees, payout limits, tax steps, and the outside company's reward rules all matter

## Conflict ledger

- Product Hunt shows perfect sentiment while Trustpilot contains a small group of sharp complaints.
- Open source offers control, but self-hosting creates maintenance work.

- keep the same short address and change the final page
- useful after a link has already been printed or shared
