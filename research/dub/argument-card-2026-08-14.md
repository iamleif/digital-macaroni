# Dub argument card

- **Buyer:** A creator or marketing team that needs to control and measure links after they are published.
- **Job:** Shorten links, change their destination, see where clicks came from, and possibly connect clicks to sales.
- **Thesis:** Dub is useful when a shared link becomes a business asset; it is unnecessary when the only problem is a long URL.
- **Reason to buy:** A link can keep the same public address while its destination changes, and the click data is easy to read.
- **Reason to hesitate:** Sales tracking and affiliate payouts begin on the $90 Business plan and require more setup.
- **Decision rule:** Put five real links on Free and change one destination after sharing it; pay only if the limits or missing sales data block real work.
- **Evidence limit:** No Digital Macaroni hands-on test was performed, and the service-review sample is small.
