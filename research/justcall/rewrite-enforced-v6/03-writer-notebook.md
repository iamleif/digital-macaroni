# JustCall writer's notebook

- Buyer: a remote sales or support team sharing calls, texts, and WhatsApp.
- Job: keep customer conversations together across staff and devices.
- Best evidence: shared channels, AI voice agent, migration help, and one positive porting account.
- Main risks: device sessions, network-change logouts, manual checks, and extra call charges.
- Evidence limit: no hands-on test and incomplete public price detail.
