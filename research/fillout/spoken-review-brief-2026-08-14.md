# Fillout spoken review brief

## What is it?

Fillout builds online forms, booking pages, payment flows, and PDFs. It can write answers directly into tools such as Airtable, Notion, and Google Sheets.

## Who will find it useful?

A small team collecting applications, requests, client details, orders, or updates that need more logic than a basic contact form.

## What is good?

The builder is approachable, the logic is capable, the Airtable and Notion links are strong, and every plan allows unlimited teammates. The free plan allows 1,000 responses a month.

## What is wrong?

Brand removal needs Pro. Analytics, partial responses, a custom domain, and unlimited responses need Business. A complex form can look easy while still sending data to the wrong place on one rare path.

## What does it cost?

Free is useful. With annual billing, Starter is $15 a month, Pro $40, and Business $75. Monthly billing costs more.

## Our answer

Use a copy of the real database. Test every branch, record creation and update, a duplicate, a half-finished form, a failed payment, all emails, and the final PDF. Choose Fillout if every result can be reconciled without manual cleanup.
