Fillout is a no-code platform that lets users create forms, schedules, and workflows without writing code. It integrates with tools like Airtable and Notion, which makes it useful for small businesses or teams that need to collect data or manage tasks without technical expertise. The main feature is its conditional logic, which allows users to show or hide form fields based on previous answers. This helps streamline data collection and makes forms more user-friendly. Fillout is good enough for basic automation needs, especially for users who value simplicity and integration. However, it may not be ideal for those who require advanced support or more complex features. Some users have reported issues with pricing and formatting, so it’s worth checking if the free plan meets your needs before upgrading. A friend should look into whether the free tier covers their use case and whether the platform’s integration with their existing tools is seamless.
