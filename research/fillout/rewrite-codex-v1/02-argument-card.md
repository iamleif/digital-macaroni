# Fillout argument card

## Buyer

An operations team or small business that needs forms to create, update, or link records in Airtable or Notion.

## Decision

Try Fillout with copied real data. Move the live form only when every branch, database update, payment, email, and PDF matches.

## Why

The builder is approachable, database connections are deep, and Free supports a real trial. Easy setup can hide mistakes in branching, hidden IDs, resumed forms, and payment records.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if the wrong record can change, payment and order records disagree, a required control forces poor pricing, or the team cannot explain each failure path.

## Verdict

Excellent forms with database connections that save real work.
