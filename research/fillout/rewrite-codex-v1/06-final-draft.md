Fillout is a form builder for work that goes beyond a contact page. It can handle bookings, payments, signatures, PDFs, approval steps, and forms that write into Airtable or Notion. We recommend Fillout when a form needs to create, update, or link real business records. Its builder is easy to approach, and the free plan is strong enough for a proper trial.

The finished form still needs a hard test. A successful submit screen does not prove that the correct row changed, the charge matches the order, or every email arrived.

## Airtable and Notion are the strongest reason to choose it

A basic form saves each answer as a new row. Fillout can work with records that already exist.

A client intake form can create a customer in Airtable and link that customer to a project. An existing client can open a private link with known details already filled in, change an address, and update the original record.

This gives customers and occasional staff a safer front door to the database. They do not need direct access to Airtable or Notion.

Known answers in an open form are called pre-filled fields. A hidden field can pass a customer ID without showing it on the page. Both features save typing, though a wrong link or ID can update the wrong person.

Test with a copy of real data. Create a record and update an old one. Submit the same form twice, then open a private link in another browser. Check the exact row after every submit. Deep Airtable and Notion connections receive consistent praise, along with quick setup and strong logic. That depth will not matter for a simple contact form that only sends an email.

## Every branch needs its own test

Fillout can change questions based on an earlier answer. It can also skip pages, work out a score, show a different ending, or send someone to another page.

This makes a long form easier to finish. It also creates paths that are easy to miss during setup.

Draw the branches before launch. Test a valid answer and a blank required field. Try an invalid date and a large upload. Go backward, then return to saved progress after the underlying data changes.

Payments need another check. The charge at the payment service must agree with the order or application saved by the form. Make a normal payment and a declined one. Close the browser during checkout, then submit twice. The team needs a clear way to find and fix a charge with no matching record.

Use phones and desktop browsers for every ending. Confirm the saved record, email, PDF, and payment after each route. Fillout makes complex forms accessible to a non-technical team, which makes a written test list more important.

## The free plan supports a real trial

Free included unlimited forms, unlimited teammates, and 1,000 responses each month when the evidence was collected. It also included multi-page forms, payments, scheduling, file uploads, PDFs, saved progress, calculations, hidden fields, and most connections.

That is enough to build the real form and run it with a small group. The free plan receives broad praise alongside responsive support. Free forms showed a Fillout badge. They did not include every field type, signatures, CAPTCHA, custom endings, or a redirect after submit. CAPTCHA matters for a public form that may attract spam.

Starter was $15 per month with yearly billing and allowed 2,000 responses. It added all field types, login forms, custom endings, redirects, signatures, CAPTCHA, and more design control.

Pro was $40 per month and allowed 5,000 responses. It added custom emails, removed Fillout branding, and included more control over shared previews and styling.

Business was $75 per month with unlimited responses. It added form reports, a custom domain, custom code, partial answers, data pulled in before submit, larger uploads, and priority support.

Fillout did not charge per seat on these plans. One required feature can still force a large jump. A team may need Business for partial answers or a custom domain even when it receives few responses.

Choose the plan from the controls the form needs. Response count is only one part of the price.

## Fillout is best when the saved record matters

Fillout suits an operations team or small business whose form writes into Airtable or Notion. It is also useful for bookings, approvals, payments, or PDFs when one form needs several steps.

Skip it for a basic newsletter signup or contact page when a simpler free tool already works. Sensitive or regulated intake needs a separate check of security, access, data retention, and applicable rules.

Build the form against copied data before replacing a live process. Complete every branch on a phone and desktop. Create and update records, resume halfway, fail a payment, and inspect the emails and PDFs.

We recommend moving to Fillout when every result matches the system behind the form. Its generous free plan and strong database links can save real work. A fast build is only the start.
