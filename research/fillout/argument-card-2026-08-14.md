# Fillout private argument card

- Buyer: a team that needs flexible public or internal forms, often connected to Airtable or Notion
- Job: collect the right answers, route each person, update the right system, and confirm what happened
- Thesis: Fillout is a strong form choice because the free plan supports a real workflow and the database links go beyond simple exports, but it should be judged by error paths and record updates
- Main reason to buy: capable logic and integrations without per-seat charges
- Main reason to hesitate: business controls and analytics require higher plans, while payment and database mistakes can have high impact
- When it is unnecessary: a basic contact form or survey already works, or the team needs a specialized regulated intake system
- Decision rule: copy a real form and database, test every branch on phone and desktop, create and update records, submit duplicates, resume halfway, fail a payment, check emails and PDFs, then reconcile every result
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on build or submission test
