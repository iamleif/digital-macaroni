Twenty is a CRM platform built on open-source principles, designed for teams that want control over their system and the ability to customize it extensively. It offers a clean interface and a flexible data model, which means users can tailor the CRM to fit their specific needs. The main feature that sets Twenty apart is its open-source architecture, allowing developers to modify and extend the system as needed. This makes it a good choice for organizations that value customization and self-hosted solutions. However, it lacks mature marketing automation tools and has a limited mobile-first design, which may not suit users looking for out-of-the-box ease of use. Twenty is good enough for teams that are comfortable managing the operational complexity and have internal support. It excels in environments where customization and open-source flexibility are non-negotiable. However, its trade-offs in support and feature completeness should be carefully weighed. A friend should check whether their team has the resources to maintain and extend the system before paying for it.
