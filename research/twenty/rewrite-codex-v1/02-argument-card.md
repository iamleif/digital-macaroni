# Argument card

- Thesis: Twenty is compelling for technical teams only after the chosen cloud or self-hosted route passes its own test.
- Strength: clean interface, flexible records, APIs, open code, and active development.
- Risk: self-hosted upgrades and missing mature CRM depth can create hidden work.
- Buyer: small technical teams with unusual data needs and appetite for control.
- Skip: teams needing deep marketing, mobile, reporting, permissions, or low-maintenance self-hosting.
- Buying test: run the sales process, migration, backup, upgrade, failure, and restore.
- Verdict: Clean open CRM, demanding when self-hosted upgrades go wrong.
