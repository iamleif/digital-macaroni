# Plausible Analytics private argument card

- Buyer: a small site, publisher, agency, or software company that wants clear aggregate traffic and conversion data
- Job: answer ordinary weekly questions without a large analytics implementation
- Thesis: Plausible is one of the best choices when the decision can fit on one dashboard; it is the wrong replacement when the team needs individual product behavior, deep advertising work, or flexible exploration
- Main reason to buy: a fast, privacy-focused, easy dashboard with more goals and funnel depth than its simple appearance suggests
- Main reason to hesitate: intentional analysis limits, traffic-based pricing, duration behavior, and self-hosting work
- When it is unnecessary: the current tool already gives a trusted short report without privacy or usability problems
- Decision rule: run it beside the current system through one complete campaign; compare pageviews, one-page sessions, goals, revenue, bots, blockers, and source records; ask a normal teammate to make the weekly decision from Plausible alone
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni tracking or legal test
