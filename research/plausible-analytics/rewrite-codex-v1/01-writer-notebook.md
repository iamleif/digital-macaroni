# Plausible Analytics writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on script, event, funnel, import, self-hosting, billing, or support test. Nine sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Plausible offers a one-page, cookie-free analytics view with campaigns, goals, events, scroll depth, funnels, journeys, revenue, reports, and imports.

The buyer is a marketing site, publisher, agency, or small software company that needs a few trusted traffic and conversion answers. The clear view, low learning curve, privacy design, and light script are the main strengths. Deep product analysis, flexible shop reports, pageview pricing, one-page duration, and self-hosting work are the main concerns.

The recommendation is to run Plausible beside the current tool for one campaign. Compare goals with signups and sales, test a long one-page visit, and give the weekly decision to a normal teammate. Keep it only when the shorter view contains enough truth.
