# Plausible Analytics argument card

## Buyer

A small team that values clear aggregate traffic and conversion reports over deep visitor-level analysis.

## Decision

Choose Plausible when one dashboard answers the weekly questions and its goals agree with real signups and sales.

## Why

The simple, cookie-free view is easy to use. That same simplicity limits detailed product behavior, shop analysis, and some advertising work.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if a serious question always needs the old tool, long one-page visits look wrong, traffic pricing strains the budget, or self-hosted recovery fails.

## Verdict

Excellent simple analytics when the shorter view is enough.
