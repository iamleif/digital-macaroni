# Plausible Analytics spoken review brief

Plausible is for a team that wants to open one page and understand its website. It covers traffic, campaigns, events, goals, funnels, and revenue without building a lasting visitor profile.

The shorter view is both the appeal and the limit. I would run one real campaign beside the current tool, compare goals with sales, and test a long article visit. Then I would hand the dashboard to someone who did not set it up.

Keep Plausible when that person can make the weekly decision and explain the gaps. If every hard question sends the team back to the old system, the dashboard is too small.
