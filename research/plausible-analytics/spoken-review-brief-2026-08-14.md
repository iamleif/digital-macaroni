# Plausible Analytics spoken review brief

## What is it?

Plausible puts website visits, pages, sources, campaigns, goals, events, funnels, journeys, and revenue on a simple dashboard. The cloud service is EU-hosted, and a community edition can be self-hosted.

## Who will find it useful?

A site owner or client who wants to understand traffic and conversions without learning a large analytics product.

## What is good?

The single-page dashboard and light script are the main attractions. Cookie-free aggregate tracking, goals, funnels, bot filtering, and imports cover more than a basic counter.

## What is wrong?

Deep product behavior and flexible analysis are limited. One-page visit time needs care. Cloud price rises with traffic, and self-hosting adds real operating work.

## What does it cost?

At 10,000 monthly views, the displayed yearly rates are $9 per month for Starter, $14 for Growth, and $19 for Business. The cloud trial lasts 30 days without a card.

## Our answer

Run it beside the current tool through one campaign. Compare the dashboard with signups and sales. Test a long one-page read, blockers, bots, goals, and a traffic spike. Keep Plausible only if a normal teammate can make the weekly decision from its simpler data.
