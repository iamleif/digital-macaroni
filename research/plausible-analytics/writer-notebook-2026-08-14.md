# Plausible Analytics writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no hands-on site
- Pricing checked 2026-08-14
- Eight Capterra reviews, small G2 pool, three practitioner discussions, repository, and official documentation

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a site owner who wants understandable traffic, campaign, and conversion numbers
- Plain example: see which pages and campaigns bring visitors and whether those visitors sign up or buy
- Cost of failure: a key action is not recorded, one-page readers appear to leave immediately, a bot spike changes the report, or traffic price jumps

## Product facts

- Single-page aggregate dashboard, campaigns, Search Console, goals, events, revenue, scroll depth, funnels, journeys, bot filtering
- EU-hosted cloud and open-source community edition
- Starter annual display at 10,000 views: $9 monthly, one site, three-year retention
- Growth: $14, three sites and people, shared dashboards
- Business: $19, ten sites and people, five-year retention, funnels, journeys, revenue, API
- 30-day trial, no card

## Experience records

### A client understood the dashboard without training

- Simplicity is the strongest and most repeated praise
- Consequence: the trial should be judged by an ordinary decision, not report count

### A reader spent ten minutes on one page

- Duration can be understated when there is no later event or pageview
- Consequence: test one-page articles and use scroll or engagement events when needed

### A high-traffic simple site moved into a higher tier

- Traffic-based price is a mixed complaint
- Consequence: forecast normal traffic, bot spikes, campaigns, and growth

## Feature translation

### Aggregate analytics

- Totals and grouped patterns rather than a profile and history for each person

### Goal

- An action the site owner treats as success, such as signup, purchase, or download

### Journey

- The actual pages people visit before or after a chosen page or goal

### Heartbeat

- A small repeated message that confirms a page remains active and can improve time measurement

## Conflict ledger

- The single page is easy because it leaves out much of a full analytics system.
- Cookie-free design improves privacy but does not audit every other script on the site.
- Lightweight tracking reduces page work and can need extra events for some engagement questions.
- Self-hosting avoids a cloud subscription and creates an analytics infrastructure job.

## Missing evidence

- No script, blocker, bot, internal visit, pageview, duration, scroll, event, goal, funnel, journey, campaign, import, export, usage limit, self-host, upgrade, or support test
- No privacy or legal audit

## Spoken language bank

- one page should answer the question
- compare the count with the signup table
- a long read is not a zero-second visit
- simplicity is the feature and the limit
- forecast the traffic spike
- self-hosting does not host itself
