# Dorik spoken review brief

Dorik is good at getting a normal business site from blank page to first draft. Templates, blocks, and AI make the start quick, and large review pools back up its reputation for ease and value.

The last round of work decides the purchase. Build the hardest real page first, put in the longest copy, and check every screen size. Then connect a spare domain, submit the forms, and publish a change.

I would use Dorik for a simple business site, portfolio, blog, or client site. I would skip it for a serious store, a web app, or a design that needs exact control. Pay for a long term only after the live test works.
