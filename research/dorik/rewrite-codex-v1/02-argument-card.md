# Dorik argument card

## Buyer

A small business, freelancer, or agency that needs a familiar marketing site and values speed over exact design freedom.

## Decision

Try Dorik by building the hardest real page first. Commit to a long plan only after the live site, domain, forms, and updates work.

## Why

Large review pools support the ease and value claims. The editor is strongest on normal site patterns. Advanced layouts and the final responsive polish can expose limits, while a small complaint group makes publishing checks necessary.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if a key section needs constant workarounds, the live domain or forms fail, a required integration is weak, or the site needs store or app features outside Dorik's range.

## Verdict

Fast simple sites work until design needs get exact.
