# Dorik writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no hands-on site build
- Current pricing checked 2026-08-14
- Review pools: 255 G2, 44 Capterra, 36 Product Hunt, plus small community threads

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a solo business owner, creator, or small agency that needs a normal marketing site without learning code
- Plain example: publish a home page, service page, blog, contact form, and mobile version on a custom domain
- Cost of failure: the site looks wrong on phones, a form fails, the domain breaks, or the editor cannot make a needed layout

## Product facts

- No-code editor, templates, reusable blocks, AI generation, blog and custom content collections
- Seven-day trial
- Personal: $29 monthly, one custom domain, 25 pages, 200 blog posts
- Business: $59 monthly, three custom domains, unlimited pages, ten collaborators
- Agency: $99 monthly, unlimited domains and collaborators
- Lower effective prices on yearly and two-year terms; current lifetime promotions
- Dorik supplies hosting but not domain registration

## Experience records

### A simple site went live quickly

- Large review pools repeatedly praise fast setup and a light editor
- Consequence: Dorik is useful when the site follows familiar page patterns

### AI produced the first version, but not the final version

- Community discussion describes more work on spacing, mobile layout, and custom details
- Consequence: judge the last 20 percent, not the first generated screen

### A live site or domain had trouble

- Smaller Product Hunt group describes bugs, loading, rendering, domain, and outage problems
- Consequence: test forms and domain behavior and keep an export or recovery plan

## Feature translation

### CMS collection

- A repeatable group of content, such as team members, projects, or locations, entered once and shown through one page design

### Reusable block

- A saved page section, such as a testimonial row or call to action, that can be used again without rebuilding it

### Responsive design

- The same page adjusts for phone, tablet, and desktop widths

## Conflict ledger

- Simple is Dorik's strength and its limit.
- Support is usually praised, but urgent cases create the strongest criticism.
- Promotional prices improve value but can push buyers toward a long term before the site is proven.

## Missing evidence

- No editor, AI, publish, domain, SEO, form, speed, export, or support test
- No complex store test
- No migration test from another builder

## Spoken language bank

- build the hardest page first
- the first draft is not the finished site
- useful for a normal business website
