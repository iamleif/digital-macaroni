# Recall argument card

## Buyer

A reader or learner who saves articles, videos, podcasts, PDFs, and notes for later use.

## Decision

Choose Recall when saved material returns during real work and every useful answer links back to its source.

## Why

Summaries, automatic tags, a knowledge graph, chat, and review can make a personal library easier to reuse. Partial capture can make every later layer wrong.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if key content is missing, answers hide their source, the main browser slows down, fair-use terms block expected volume, or exports lose important context.

## Verdict

Useful knowledge recall when every answer keeps its source.
