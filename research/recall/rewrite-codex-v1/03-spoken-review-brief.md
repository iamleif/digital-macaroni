# Recall spoken review brief

Recall turns saved articles, videos, podcasts, PDFs, and notes into a connected library. It can summarize them, answer across them, and bring old material back during new work.

The whole system rests on complete capture. A clean summary of a missing table or bad transcript is worse than an obvious error. I would build a varied 50-item library and verify every useful answer against the source.

Then I would leave it alone for two weeks, return with a real question, and test the export. Recall earns its place when the right old item comes back with proof attached.
