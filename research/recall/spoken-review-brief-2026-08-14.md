# Recall spoken review brief

## What is it?

Recall saves articles, videos, podcasts, PDFs, and notes. It creates summaries, tags, links, a visual map, chat, audio playback, and memory quizzes around the saved library.

## Who will find it useful?

Someone who saves a lot to read later and wants to recover ideas across formats instead of managing folders by hand.

## What is good?

A source can become an organized card quickly. Library-wide chat and automatic links can bring old material back when it becomes useful.

## What is wrong?

Some difficult pages are only partly captured. Firefox, language handling, and large-library reliability have mixed evidence. A confident summary can still be wrong. Unlimited paid summaries have a fair-use boundary.

## What does it cost?

Free gives unlimited saves and notes plus ten summaries per month. Plus is $10 per month billed yearly. Max is $38 and adds model choice, bulk work, and onboarding.

## Our answer

Save 50 real varied items. Check every summary against the source, including tables and footnotes. Ask questions with known and conflicting answers. Return after two weeks, then export everything. Keep Recall only if it helps recover the source, not merely generate another summary.
