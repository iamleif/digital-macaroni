# Recall writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no hands-on library
- Pricing checked 2026-08-14
- Product Hunt pool, extension reviews, three paid-user or community accounts, one week-long editorial test
- Long-term large-library evidence is weak

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a researcher, learner, writer, or curious person who saves more material than they can find again
- Plain example: save a podcast, report, video, and article about one topic, then recover the exact claim and source two weeks later
- Cost of failure: partial capture, false summary, source lost, wrong language, slow browser, or a library that cannot be exported

## Product facts

- Articles, YouTube, podcasts, PDFs, notes, browser, web, iOS, and Android
- Summary, tags, library chat, graph, listen mode, quizzes, spaced review
- Free: unlimited saves and notes, ten AI summaries monthly, API and MCP
- Plus: $10 monthly billed yearly, normal-use unlimited summaries and main AI tools
- Max: $38 monthly billed yearly, model choice, 100-item bulk actions, founder onboarding
- 30-day refund; 20% student discount; fair-use policy

## Experience records

### A podcast became a useful card in minutes

- Summary and organization are strong praise
- Consequence: test against the transcript, not the attractive card

### A page with a table was only partly captured

- Partial capture appears in a detailed paid-user account
- Consequence: compare first, middle, last, table, caption, and footnote

### The saved idea returned during later browsing

- Graph and augmented browsing are praised
- Consequence: count useful resurfacing instead of graph size

## Feature translation

### Knowledge graph

- A visual map of automatically linked notes and saved items

### Spaced repetition

- Reviewing a fact again after longer gaps to help remember it

### Whole-library chat

- Asking one question across all saved sources rather than opening one document

### Fair use

- A contract limit that allows normal high use but lets the service review or restrict unusually costly activity

## Conflict ledger

- A fast summary can save time and hide a missed qualification.
- Automatic links remove filing and may create graph clutter.
- Unlimited summaries suit typical use and do not cover every automated bulk pattern.
- One growing library improves recall and raises export plus long-term reliability risk.

## Missing evidence

- No article, paywall, dynamic page, table, PDF, transcript, podcast, language, tag, graph, chat, citation, quiz, review, browser, phone, import, export, quota, refund, or support test
- No large-library search or speed test

## Spoken language bank

- save 50 things you would really return to
- compare the card with the middle and footnotes
- ask a question with a known answer
- a summary is a map, not the source
- count useful returns, not graph lines
- export before the library feels permanent
