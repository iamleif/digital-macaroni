# Product research notes

## Product and audience

What the software does, who it is for, and the problem it claims to solve.

## Hands-on log

Record dates, plan, setup path, tasks attempted, screenshots, failures, and limitations. Leave this section explicit when no product access occurred.

## Customer evidence

Summarize repeated themes. Keep source IDs beside each note so they can be traced back to `evidence.json`.

## Contradictions

Record claims that disagree across official documentation, pricing pages, reviews, or testing.

## Editorial angle

The original point of view that makes this review more useful than a stitched summary.
