# Claap writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no product test
- Current plan limits checked 2026-08-14
- G2: 4.6 from 406; Product Hunt: 28 reviews
- Main limit: paid prices were not visible in readable public page content

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a remote product, sales, support, or agency team that records meetings or screen explanations
- Plain example: record a product walk-through, let a teammate comment at the exact point that needs a change, and keep the result for someone who was absent
- Cost of failure: a slow recording or weak summary leaves the team watching the full meeting anyway

## Product facts

- Records screen and camera clips as well as scheduled calls
- Transcribes 99 languages and creates an AI summary
- Supports comments, mentions, reactions, shared notes, and time-specific feedback
- Basic: ten videos per user, three uploads, 300 recorded minutes total, and three months of storage
- Pro: unlimited recordings, ten uploads monthly, 1,000 recorded minutes monthly, 500 AI credits, and two years of storage
- Business: unlimited recording time, 50 uploads monthly, 1,000 AI credits, sales features, and three years of storage
- Workspaces can mix free and paid licenses

## Experience records

### A short video replaced a meeting

- Source: repeated G2 and Product Hunt pattern
- Action: record the screen, explain the work, and share it
- Result: teammates watch when ready and comment on the relevant moment
- Consequence: useful for visual explanations that are awkward in a long message

### A longer recording took time to become usable

- Source: repeated G2 complaint
- Result: upload, processing, or playback delay interrupted the handoff
- Consequence: test with the normal video length and connection, not a one-minute sample

### A growing library became harder to use

- Source: detailed G2 criticism
- Result: organization and search felt limited with many recordings
- Consequence: decide where final decisions live; do not assume a pile of searchable video becomes documentation

## Feature translation

### Time-stamped comment

- What it does: attaches a coworker's note to the exact second in a video
- Who needs it: design review, product demos, call coaching, and visual feedback

### AI summary

- What it does: turns a transcript into a shorter note and possible action items
- Main limit: the team still needs to check names, numbers, promises, and owners

### Video workspace

- What it does: stores recordings in team areas and channels
- Main limit: storage is not the same as a maintained guide or decision record

## Conflict ledger

- Claap can save the time of a meeting, but slow processing can give some of it back.
- Searchable recordings help absent teammates, but a large library needs naming and ownership rules.
- AI notes can speed follow-up, but accuracy was not independently tested.

## Missing evidence
