# Claap writer's notebook

Research-based review with no Digital Macaroni hands-on test. Seven sources were checked on August 13 and 14, 2026. Claap records meetings or short screen videos, creates transcripts and summaries, and lets teammates comment at a specific point. Basic includes ten created videos and 300 recorded minutes in total. Pro includes unlimited recordings, 1,000 minutes monthly, and 500 AI credits monthly. Current paid prices were not readable on the public page.

The buyer is a remote product, sales, or support team that repeats visual explanations or writes meeting notes. The useful result is fewer live meetings and faster follow-up. Easy recording, sharing, summaries, and time-stamped comments are repeated strengths. Slow processing, playback delays, recording limits, and weak organization in a large library are repeated limits.

Plain terms: a time-stamped comment is a note attached to the exact second it concerns. Processing is the wait before a recording, transcript, or summary is ready. An AI credit pays for an automated task such as a summary.

The main conflict is that recording can save time while a growing video library creates another place to search. No transcription accuracy, privacy, CRM, support, or normal-length processing test was completed.
