# Spoken review brief

Claap records a meeting or a short video of your screen. It writes a transcript and summary, then lets coworkers leave a comment at the exact moment they mean.

That is useful for a remote team when the recording replaces a call. A product manager can explain a change in five minutes, and a designer can answer later beside the right screen. Someone who missed a meeting can read the summary and open only the part that matters.

The risk is turning every update into another video that nobody watches. Slow processing and playback also weaken the handoff. A recording made before one person signs off needs to be ready when the next person starts work.

I would tell a friend to test one normal meeting and one real screen explanation. Time the processing, check key facts in the summary, and ask a teammate to find one decision. Claap is worth paying for only when this saves a meeting or makes follow-up clearly easier.
