## Claims

- Salesflare launched publicly on September 14, 2016. [verified; fact; launch]
- Salesflare connects with Gmail, Outlook, calendars, mobile devices, and LinkedIn-assisted workflows to collect contact and activity data. [verified; fact; pricing, g2, reddit-gmail]
- Automatic data capture, ease of use, email integration, and responsive support are the strongest positive patterns. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, capterra, trustpilot]
- Reporting and extensive customization are less suitable for teams with complex CRM requirements. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, reddit-choice]
- Growth costs $29 per user per month billed annually or $39 monthly; Pro costs $49 annually or $64 monthly; Enterprise costs $99 annually or $124 monthly. [verified; fact; pricing]
- Growth includes five lead credits monthly, Pro 100, and Enterprise 250. Extra credits can be purchased. [verified; fact; pricing]
- Salesflare prorates added users and applies credit for disabled users to future invoices. [verified; fact; billing]
