# Salesflare spoken review brief

## What is it?

Salesflare is a shared sales record for small B2B teams. It collects contacts, emails, meetings, notes, tasks, and possible deals, then shows what happened and what should happen next.

## Who will find it useful?

A small team that sells through Gmail or Outlook and regularly forgets to update a larger CRM.

## What is good?

Automatic contact and activity capture can remove a lot of typing. The product is easier to approach than a heavily customized corporate CRM, and support receives repeated praise.

## What is wrong?

Automatic capture still needs checking. Reporting and customization may be too limited for a complex sales process. Permissions, email sequences, and custom dashboards require Pro.

## What does it cost?

Growth is $29 per user monthly with yearly billing or $39 month to month. Pro is $49 or $64. Enterprise is $99 or $124.

## Our answer

Import 100 real records and follow one lead from first email to a handoff. Check wrong matches, missing history, duplicates, reminders, mobile work, the weekly report, and export. Keep it only if the record is accurate enough that the team stops maintaining a second version elsewhere.
