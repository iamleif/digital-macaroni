# Salesflare writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on import, email connection, pipeline, automation, mobile, export, billing, or support test. Eight sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Salesflare is a focused CRM that builds contact and activity records from email, calendars, mobile work, and related tools.

The buyer is a small B2B sales team working mainly from email. Automatic data capture, ease, email integration, and responsive support are the main strengths. Wrong contact matching, duplicates, limited customization, reporting, permissions, tracking, and per-user pricing are the main concerns.

The recommendation is to follow one real lead through email, a meeting, proposal, handoff, silence, and export. Choose Salesflare when the record stays correct and another salesperson always knows the next step.
