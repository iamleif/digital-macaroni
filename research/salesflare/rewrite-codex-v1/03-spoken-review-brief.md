# Salesflare spoken review brief

Salesflare is for a small sales team that spends its day in email. It can pull contact details, messages, and meetings into the CRM so people type less.

That only helps when the record stays correct. I would import awkward contacts, follow one real lead, and hand it to another salesperson without a briefing. The second person should know the promise and next step.

Then I would test a reply inside an email sequence, build the weekly report, and export the account. Salesflare earns its place when automation removes entry without hiding mistakes.
