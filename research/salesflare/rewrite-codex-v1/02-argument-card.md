# Salesflare argument card

## Buyer

A small B2B sales team that lives in email and wants less manual CRM entry.

## Decision

Choose Salesflare when automatic capture stays correct and a lead can move between salespeople without a private briefing.

## Why

Email and calendar activity can build a useful shared record with less typing. The focused design has a ceiling for deep reports, changes, and permissions.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if contact matching creates hidden errors, pipeline stages need workarounds, replies fail to stop sequences, required reports cannot be built, or exports lose history.

## Verdict

Smart small-team CRM when automatic records stay honest.
