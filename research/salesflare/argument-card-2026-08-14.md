# Salesflare private argument card

- Buyer: a small B2B sales team that lives in Gmail or Outlook and does not keep its CRM up to date
- Job: collect the relationship history, show the next action, and let another person take over without asking for a private recap
- Thesis: Salesflare is a sensible CRM for a small email-led team when its automatic capture is accurate; its simplicity stops helping when reporting, permissions, or the sales route become complicated
- Main reason to buy: less manual contact and activity entry
- Main reason to hesitate: incorrect capture, duplicate records, limited customization, and the higher Pro price for permissions, sequences, and custom reports
- When it is unnecessary: one person has a short lead list and can reliably manage it in an inbox or simple table
- Decision rule: import 100 representative records, follow one real lead from first email through a team handoff, audit every captured event, test an unusual deal, build the weekly report, work from a phone, then export the result
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni account
