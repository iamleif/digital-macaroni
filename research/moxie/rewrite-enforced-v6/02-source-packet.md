## Claims

- The product lineage launched as Hectic FreelancerOS on June 21, 2022, before adopting the Moxie name. [verified; fact; launch]
- Moxie combines client records, sales leads, proposals, contracts, invoices, payment tools, time tracking, scheduling, calendars, forms, projects, and basic accounting. [verified; fact; pricing, capterra]
- Starter costs $12 monthly or $10 per month billed yearly. Pro costs $25 monthly or $20 per month billed yearly. Teams costs $40 monthly or $385 yearly and includes up to five team members. [verified; fact; pricing]
- Moxie offers a 14-day trial. Pro adds a white-labeled client portal, workflow automation, phone line, API connections, QuickBooks, and unlimited project collaborators. [verified; fact; pricing]
- Replacing several separate freelancer tools with one client-to-invoice workflow is the strongest positive theme. [supported; reported-pattern; trustpilot, appsumo, g2, app-store, client-ready-writer]
- Freelancers commonly describe the basic workflow as intuitive and time-saving, with frequent praise for support and product updates. [supported; reported-pattern; trustpilot, appsumo, g2, app-store]
- Project planning is lighter than a dedicated project tool, while navigation, mobile polish, advanced sales tracking, packages, and automation have narrower complaints. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, appsumo, app-store, client-ready-writer]
