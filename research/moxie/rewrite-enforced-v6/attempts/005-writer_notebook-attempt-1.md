It was previously known as Hectic FreelancerOS before rebranding to Moxie. The product is positioned as an all-in-one solution for managing the full lifecycle of a freelance project, from initial contact to final payment. Moxie offers three pricing tiers: Starter, Pro, and Teams. The Starter plan costs $12 per month or $10 annually, while Pro is $25 monthly or $20 annually. Teams is $40 monthly or $385 annually and includes up to five team members. A 14-day free trial is available for all plans. Pro adds features like a white-labeled client portal, workflow automation, phone line, API connections, QuickBooks integration, and unlimited project collaborators. The product is available on iOS and Android, and it emphasizes a streamlined workflow for solo freelancers and small teams."
