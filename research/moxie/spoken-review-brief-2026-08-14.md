# Moxie spoken review brief

## What is it?

Moxie runs the paperwork and routine work around a freelance business. It keeps client details, leads, proposals, contracts, projects, time, appointments, invoices, and payments together.

## Who will find it useful?

A solo freelancer or small service team that keeps copying the same client name, job details, and price between several cheap apps.

## What is good?

One client record can follow the work from first inquiry to final invoice. The core workflow is widely described as easy, and support gets repeated praise.

## What is wrong?

The project area is lighter than a dedicated planning tool. Complex dependencies, team capacity, advanced sales work, packages, and some automations may still need another system.

## What does it cost?

Starter is $12 monthly or $10 per month billed yearly. Pro is $25 monthly or $20 per month billed yearly. Teams is $40 monthly or $385 yearly for up to five team members. The trial lasts 14 days.

## Our answer

Run one realistic client from inquiry to payment. Include a changed scope, a late invoice, a phone check, and an export. Keep Moxie if the workflow makes sense to the client and lets the freelancer cancel enough other tools.
