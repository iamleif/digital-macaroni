# Moxie argument card

## Buyer

A solo freelancer or very small service team that repeats client details across separate sales, contract, project, and invoice tools.

## Decision

Use Moxie when one full client job proves it can remove enough copying, subscriptions, and admin.

## Why

The connected client-to-invoice flow is the main value. Its project planning and some specialist areas are lighter than dedicated products.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if clients cannot use the portal alone, complex work needs to be rebuilt elsewhere, payments or exports fail, automation hides errors, or the remaining stack erases the savings.

## Verdict

Strong freelance admin when one workflow replaces several tools.
