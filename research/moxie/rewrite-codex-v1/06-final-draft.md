Moxie links the daily work of a freelance business. A lead can become a proposal, signed contract, project, tracked time, invoice, and payment without retyping the client in each tool.

That flow is the reason to buy. Moxie will rarely beat a purpose-built app at every job it covers. It can still save time when its linked work is good enough to replace several accounts.

I recommend Moxie for a solo freelancer or very small team with simple client work. Avoid it when deep project plans or sales controls drive the business.

## The handoffs create the value

The client record links contact details with messages, proposals, contracts, appointments, tasks, time, invoices, and payments. A signed offer can feed the next step, while tracked time can reach the final bill.

This basic path receives the clearest praise in the evidence. Freelancers report less admin, an easy daily flow, and useful support. The appeal comes from removing repeat entry rather than one rare feature.

Measure that promise with a real-shaped job. Start with an inquiry, offer two choices, send a contract, collect a small safe deposit, create the project, track time, change the scope, and issue the final invoice. Record each spreadsheet and outside app you still open.

## The portal must make sense to a client

The Pro plan adds a branded portal for messages, files, forms, appointments, tasks, and bills. A client should be able to sign, pay, find a request, and understand the next step without a guided tour.

Open the portal in a private browser on a laptop and phone. Ask someone unfamiliar with Moxie to find a contract, invoice, file, appointment, and open request. Test an email reply and a forgotten password.

Brand colors matter less than fewer questions. If clients still ask where to sign or how to pay, the portal has not reduced admin. That makes the upgrade harder to defend.

## Project depth sets the upper limit

Moxie can hold projects, tasks, dates, time, and collaborators. That may cover an article package, a small design job, or a clear series of consulting sessions.

Agency work can ask for more. Task links, staff capacity, deep access rules, and many handoffs are where project tools go further. Public comments also mention smaller gaps in menus, packages, sales tracking, rules, and phone polish.

Build the hardest normal project in the trial. Add recurring work, an approval, a delayed handoff, a scope change, and a collaborator. Check that the collaborator sees the needed work without private business data.

Moxie can still own sales, contracts, and billing while another tool runs delivery. Count the duplicate updates and second subscription honestly. A broad suite loses much of its value when every project must be rebuilt.

## Test money, automation, and exit routes

Payments deserve more than a clean invoice preview. Check current processing charges, currencies, payout timing, refunds, disputes, taxes, and what reaches the accounting system. We did not test those paths.

Use one automation for a dull repeated step, such as creating a project after a signed contract. Run it twice, then trigger it with missing information. The failure should be visible, and no client should receive two welcome messages.

Export the client, signed documents, project, and financial record before committing. A useful operating system should also offer a clear way out. Send support one deadline-sensitive question and judge the ownership of the answer.

## Price the suite against the tools it removes

When our evidence was collected, Starter cost $12 monthly or $10 per month with annual billing. Pro cost $25 monthly or $20 per month annually. Teams cost $40 monthly or $385 yearly for up to five members. Confirm current terms before buying.

Starter covered much of the solo workflow. Pro added the branded portal, automation, outside connections, QuickBooks, and unlimited project collaborators. A fourteen-day trial was available.

The public ratings are highly positive, though their context matters. Moxie asks customers for Trustpilot reviews, and AppSumo includes lifetime-deal buyers. One detailed external review used affiliate links. The praise supports a trial, while the dry run must carry the decision.

## Verdict

Moxie is a strong product to test for a solo freelancer who wants one path from inquiry to payment. Its breadth can remove real copying and several small subscriptions. Project depth and specialist gaps make it a weaker agency system. Follow one hard client through the full job, let the client use the portal alone, and export the record. Choose Moxie when enough other tools can leave. Skip it when the work still lives elsewhere.
