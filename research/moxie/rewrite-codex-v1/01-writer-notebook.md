# Moxie writer's notebook

Research-based review with no new hands-on client, proposal, contract, invoice, payment, project, portal, mobile, export, support, or billing test. Nine sources were checked on August 13 and 14, 2026. Moxie joins the main parts of a freelance service business from first lead through payment.

The buyer is a solo freelancer or very small service team that copies client details between too many tools. Connected records, broad coverage, value, and helpful support are the main strengths. Project depth, navigation, mobile polish, advanced sales work, and automation limits are the main concerns.

The recommendation depends on one complete dry run. Follow a realistic client from inquiry through proposal, contract, deposit, project, scope change, final invoice, export, and portal use. Count every outside app and manual copy that remains. Complex agencies should keep a deeper project tool.
