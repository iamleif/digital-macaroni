# Moxie spoken review brief

Moxie is for freelancers who keep copying the same client from a form to a proposal, contract, project, time sheet, and invoice. It connects those steps around one client record.

That breadth is the strength and the limit. The basic flow gets strong praise, while project planning, navigation, mobile work, advanced sales tracking, and some automation feel lighter.

I would follow one realistic client from inquiry to paid during the trial. Keep Moxie only if the client can use the portal, exports work, and enough other apps can truly leave.
