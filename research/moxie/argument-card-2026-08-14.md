# Moxie private argument card

- Buyer: a solo freelancer or very small service team piecing together client work in several apps
- Job: move a client from first inquiry through agreement, delivery, and payment without copying the same information everywhere
- Thesis: Moxie is useful when it replaces a messy set of small tools, but it should not be chosen as a deep project-management system for a growing agency
- Main reason to buy: the client, documents, schedule, work, time, and invoice remain connected
- Main reason to hesitate: some built-in areas, especially project planning and advanced automation, are lighter than dedicated products
- When it is unnecessary: the current small stack already works, or complex capacity, dependencies, approvals, and sales reporting are essential
- Decision rule: run one realistic client from inquiry to final payment, include a scope change and late invoice, ask the client to use the portal unassisted, test mobile and export, then count every manual copy and outside tool that remains
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni hands-on workflow
