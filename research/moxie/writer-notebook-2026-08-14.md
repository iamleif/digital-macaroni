# Moxie writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no Digital Macaroni account
- Pricing checked 2026-08-14
- 536 Trustpilot reviews, 83 AppSumo reviews, 20 App Store ratings, four G2 reviews, one Capterra voice, and one six-month affiliate review
- Trustpilot is solicited; AppSumo is a lifetime-deal cohort

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a solo freelancer or small service business using separate tools for leads, documents, projects, time, and invoices
- Plain example: turn a website inquiry into a proposal, signed contract, deposit, scheduled work, tracked time, final invoice, and client history
- Cost of failure: unsigned terms, missed scope change, wrong invoice, lost client message, duplicate data, or a project that needs another system anyway

## Product facts

- Client list and sales pipeline
- Proposals, quotes, contracts, e-signatures, invoices, payments, and basic accounting
- Projects, deliverables, tasks, time tracking, forms, calendar, inbox, scheduling, and AI assistant
- Starter: $12 monthly or $120 yearly
- Pro: $25 monthly or $240 yearly; white-label portal, automation, phone line, API connections, QuickBooks, unlimited collaborators
- Teams: $40 monthly or $385 yearly; up to five team members
- 14-day trial; priority support on all plans

## Experience records

### A freelancer stopped copying client details across apps

- Consolidation and time saving are the strongest themes
- Consequence: compare the whole stack cost and manual work, not one feature at a time

### A project became too complicated for the built-in planning

- Project management is a mixed limit
- Consequence: test dependencies, repeated work, workload, approvals, and the view used in weekly planning

### A client used the portal from the phone

- Client portal is widely praised while mobile polish has narrower complaints
- Consequence: test the client side with a person who has no instructions

## Feature translation

### CRM

- The client list and history: contact details, conversations, deals, documents, projects, and money connected to that client

### Sales pipeline

- Columns showing which inquiries are new, discussing a job, waiting on a proposal, won, or lost

### White-labeled portal

- A client page that uses the freelancer's name and domain instead of showing Moxie as the main brand

### Project collaborator

- A person invited to one project who can work on deliverables and track time without receiving full business access

## Conflict ledger

- One suite removes app switching but each built-in area is lighter than some dedicated products.
- High ratings support a trial, but the two largest samples have strong selection effects.
- Solo service work fits the design; complex agency resource planning may not.
- A low plan price matters only if payment and other transaction costs are also understood.

## Missing evidence

- No inquiry, proposal, contract, deposit, invoice, refund, tax, recurring payment, client portal, project, automation, mobile, export, cancellation, or support test
- No comparison of processing fees or accounting treatment
- No team permission or project-capacity test
