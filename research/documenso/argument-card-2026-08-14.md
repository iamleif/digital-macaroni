# Documenso private argument card

- Buyer: a team seeking open-source document signing, either hosted or operated on its own infrastructure
- Job: send, complete, track, and retain signed documents at a manageable cost
- Thesis: Documenso is a credible open signing option, but hosted use and self-hosting should never be treated as the same purchase
- Main reason to buy: inspectable code, self-hosting choice, clear hosted plans, and no document cap on paid tiers
- Main reason to hesitate: weak customer-review depth and substantial production duties for self-hosting
- When it is unnecessary: a mature signing provider already meets cost and compliance needs, or the team cannot operate a secure document service
- Decision rule: complete a real low-risk document with several recipients and inspect the final record; for self-hosting also test certificate failure, email delivery, backup restoration, upgrades, and network restrictions
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on test or legal opinion
