# Documenso argument card

## Buyer

A small team seeking open electronic signing, or a technical organization with a firm deployment-control need.

## Decision

Test the hosted service with one low-risk document. Consider self-hosting only after the signing flow passes and someone accepts the operating work.

## Why

Open code and flexible hosting are meaningful strengths. Formal customer evidence is thin. Self-hosting adds certificate, email, database, security, backup, and upgrade duties that a hosted buyer avoids.

## Walk-away conditions

Skip it if the hosted flow confuses signers, the finished record is insufficient, or a self-hosted team cannot prove signing, email, recovery, restore, and upgrades.

## Verdict

Open signing works best hosted unless control justifies operations.
