# Documenso writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on signing or deployment. Eleven sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Documenso offers hosted electronic signing and self-hosted core software under AGPL-3.0. Hosted Free allows five documents monthly. Paid plans range from Individual to Platform.

The buyer has two distinct decisions. A normal small team needs to judge the signing flow. A technical organization must also decide whether server and data control justify operating PostgreSQL, email, secure routing, file storage, a signing certificate, backups, and updates.

The recommendation is hosted first. Self-hosting is for a clear control requirement with a named operator. Formal customer reviews are scarce. GitHub interest and community activity show developer attention, not signing reliability or legal suitability. No claim of legal fitness should be made.
