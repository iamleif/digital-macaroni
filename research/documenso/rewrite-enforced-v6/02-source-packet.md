## Claims

- Documenso 0.9 launched publicly on May 28, 2023. [verified; fact; launch]
- Documenso offers a hosted signing service and self-hosted core software under AGPL-3.0. [verified; fact; official-docs, official-self-host, github]
- Hosted Free allows five documents monthly. Individual costs $25 monthly billed yearly, Teams $40 monthly billed yearly for five users, and Platform $250 monthly billed yearly. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- A production self-hosted setup needs PostgreSQL, an email service, a public domain with secure routing, and a signing certificate. Without the signing certificate, the app can open while signing fails. [verified; fact; official-requirements, official-tips]
- Open code, self-hosting, data control, and lower signing cost are the clearest positive themes. [supported; reported-pattern; launch, reddit-one, reddit-two, github]
- Certificate, file-permission, container, environment, and email setup create repeated self-hosting friction. [supported; reported-pattern; reddit-one, reddit-two, official-tips, official-requirements]
