# Documenso spoken review brief

## What is it?

Documenso sends documents for electronic signatures. A business uploads a PDF, places fields for each person, sends the request, and downloads the completed file and activity record. It can be hosted by Documenso or run on the business's own servers.

## Who will find it useful?

A small team seeking a simpler open alternative, or a technical organization that has a specific reason to control the server and data. Self-hosting is not for a business without an experienced operator.

## What is good?

The source code is public, the hosted prices are clear, and paid plans allow unlimited documents. People who finish self-hosting often describe the signing screen as simple.

## What is wrong?

There is almost no formal customer-review depth. Self-hosting needs a database, email delivery, a signing certificate, secure networking, backups, and updates. The app can appear healthy while signing fails if the certificate is wrong.

## What does it cost?

Free covers five documents a month. Individual is $25 a month billed yearly. Teams is $40 a month billed yearly for five people. Platform is $250 a month billed yearly. Self-hosting has infrastructure and staff costs even when the core software has no subscription.

## Our answer

Use hosted Documenso first unless the team can state a clear reason to self-host. Complete a real low-risk document with two signers, reminders, a decline, and a corrected email. Inspect the PDF and audit record. For self-hosting, also break the certificate, restore a backup, test email delivery, and update a copy of production. Ask qualified counsel what the real documents require.
