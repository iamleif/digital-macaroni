## Claims

- CompanyHub was publicly available as an Android CRM by September 27, 2016. [verified; fact; android-history]
- CompanyHub combines contacts and deals with custom fields, custom record types, reports, email sync, forms, automation, and detailed access rules. [verified; fact; official-start, official-email]
- Productivity costs $21 per user monthly or $15 with annual billing. Higher plans add automation and advanced sales tools, with monthly prices reaching $64 per user. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Email is synced every few hours, sent mail may not appear in contact history until the next sync, and the initial import covers six months. [verified; fact; official-email]
- Ease, flexible custom records, follow-up focus, and responsive support are common strengths. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, capterra, trustpilot]
- The interface can feel dated or less polished, while integration and installed-app friction appears in a small number of reports. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, capterra]
