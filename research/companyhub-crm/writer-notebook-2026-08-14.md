# CompanyHub CRM writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no product test
- Current price and email behavior checked 2026-08-14
- G2: 4.7 from 45; Capterra: 4.7 from 22; Trustpilot: 4.2 from 12
- Main limit: almost no meaningful independent community use discussion

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a small sales team that needs records beyond contacts, companies, and deals
- Plain example: a property team can keep buildings, units, owners, payments, and deals linked without buying an enterprise CRM
- Cost of failure: custom records are built badly, salespeople stop updating them, or email history arrives too late

## Product facts

- Custom fields, names, columns, filters, reports, record types, and links between records
- Deal pipelines, follow-up suggestions, email, forms, automation, quotes, products, and access rules
- Email supports Google, Microsoft, Exchange, and IMAP but not POP3
- Initial email import covers six months; normal sync runs every few hours
- Productivity: $15 per user monthly on annual billing or $21 monthly
- Automation and Accelerate rise to $36 and $64 per user on monthly billing
- Trial lasts 14 days with no card

## Experience records

### A business needed records that a normal CRM did not have

- Source: official capability and repeated G2 pattern
- Action: create a custom table such as Property, Project, Course, or Payment and connect it to contacts and deals
- Result: the CRM follows the business instead of forcing everything into a note
- Consequence: CompanyHub is strongest when the team can name the custom records it truly needs

### Salespeople needed to know whom to call next

- Source: recent detailed G2 review
- Result: follow-up suggestions brought overdue deal work to the front
- Consequence: judge the daily list with real deals, not an empty demo

### Email history arrived after a delay

- Source: official documentation
- Result: messages sync every few hours and sent mail may be absent until the next run
- Consequence: not suitable when the team expects every fresh email to appear at once

## Feature translation

### Custom table

- What it does: creates a new kind of record besides a person, company, or deal
- Example: a school can track courses; a property firm can track buildings and units

### Pipeline

- What it does: shows each deal at a stage such as new, quoted, negotiating, or won

### Field-level access

- What it does: hides a sensitive field, such as margin or commission, from selected users

## Conflict ledger

- The interface is easy to follow but looks less polished than larger rivals.
- Deep customization is useful, but a badly designed data structure creates more admin work.
- Email sync removes copying but is not real time.

## Missing evidence

- No direct community account from a current long-term user
- No data import or custom table build
- No email or permission test
- No automation, app-removal, mobile, or support test

## Spoken language bank

- store the records your business actually uses
- show the salesperson who needs a follow-up today
- email history may be a few hours behind
- simple to navigate does not mean modern-looking
- build one real record from import to report
