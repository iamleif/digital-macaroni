# CompanyHub CRM private argument card

- Buyer: a small sales team whose work does not fit standard contact and deal records
- Job: store the right business records, keep sales follow-up visible, and report without a large CRM project
- Thesis: CompanyHub offers unusually flexible records for a smaller CRM, but the team should accept a plain interface and test email delay and integrations
- Main reason to buy: custom record types and reports adapt to a specialized business without code
- Main reason to hesitate: thin community evidence, dated presentation, and email that syncs every few hours
- When it is unnecessary: ordinary contacts and deals already fit a cheaper or more polished CRM
- Decision rule: import 100 real records, build one custom record type, connect it to a deal, hide a sensitive field, sync a real mailbox, and create the weekly sales report during the trial
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on test and little independent community discussion
