# CompanyHub CRM spoken review brief

## What is it?

CompanyHub keeps contacts, companies, and sales deals, but it also lets a small business create its own kinds of records. A property firm could add buildings and units. A training company could add courses and enrollments.

## Who will find it useful?

A small sales team that has outgrown a spreadsheet because its customers, deals, payments, projects, or other records need to stay linked.

## What is good?

The custom records and reports are more flexible than many small CRMs. Follow-up suggestions also put the deals that need attention in front of the salesperson.

## What is wrong?

The interface works but can look dated. Email history syncs every few hours rather than at once. Integration depth is hard to judge, and the product has very little independent community discussion.

## What does it cost?

Productivity is $21 per user monthly or $15 with annual billing. Higher plans add automation and other sales tools, with monthly pricing up to $64 per user. The trial lasts 14 days.

## Our answer

Import 100 real records and build the one custom record type the business cannot live without. Link it to a deal, make a report, hide one sensitive field, and sync the real mailbox. Ask salespeople to run their day from it. Choose CompanyHub if the custom setup stays easy and a few hours of email delay is acceptable.
