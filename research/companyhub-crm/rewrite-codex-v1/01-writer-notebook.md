# CompanyHub CRM writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on setup. Nine sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. CompanyHub combines contacts and deals with custom record types, reports, email sync, forms, automation, and access rules. Productivity was $21 per user monthly or $15 with annual billing. Email sync runs every few hours and initially imports six months.

The buyer is a small sales team whose work does not fit standard contacts and deals. Custom records and follow-up views are the main strengths. A dated interface, modest integration evidence, and delayed email history are the limits. The positive sample is modest and community discussion is sparse.

Plain terms: a custom record is a new kind of item such as a property, course, or payment. A linked record connects that item to the right contact or deal. Missing evidence covers imports, permissions, email, automation, mobile use, app removal, and support.
