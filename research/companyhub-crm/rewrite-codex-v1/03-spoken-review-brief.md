# Spoken review brief

CompanyHub is a sales CRM that lets a small business create its own kinds of records. A property firm can track buildings and units beside contacts. A training business can track courses and enrollments.

That is the reason to choose it. If contacts and deals already cover the work, a simpler and more polished CRM makes more sense. CompanyHub also syncs email only every few hours, so it is a poor fit for someone who expects a live mailbox view.

I would tell a friend to build one custom record from real data, connect it to a deal, and create the weekly report. Then let the sales team use the follow-up view for several days. CompanyHub is worth keeping only if it replaces a spreadsheet without creating another admin job.
