# Private argument card

- Buyer: a small team replacing sales spreadsheets.
- Job: keep unusual business records beside contacts and deals.
- Thesis: CompanyHub is useful when custom records remove a real spreadsheet; ordinary sales teams can find a cleaner CRM.
- Reason to buy: flexible records, follow-up views, and a fair starting price.
- Reason to hesitate: email history is delayed and the interface or connections may feel dated.
- Decision rule: build one real custom record and the weekly report during the trial. Continue only if salespeople use it without rebuilding the spreadsheet.
- Evidence limit: modest public sample and no hands-on test.
