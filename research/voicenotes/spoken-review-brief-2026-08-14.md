# Voicenotes spoken review brief

## What is it?

Voicenotes is a recorder for thoughts and meetings. You speak, it makes a transcript and summary, and you can search the recording later or turn it into another piece of writing.

## Who will find it useful?

Someone who loses ideas because opening a notes app and typing feels too slow. It is also useful for short meetings, reminders, and voice memos that would otherwise sit in a folder and never get opened again.

## What is the best part?

The recording does not remain a mystery audio file. You get text, a summary, and a way to ask questions about what you said.

## What is the main problem?

Some detailed App Store accounts describe recordings stopping or disappearing on iPhone, Mac, and Apple Watch. One person lost a 54-minute note and a separate two-hour meeting. Another said recordings worked in an office but stopped during client walks outside.

Voicenotes says newer versions save recordings locally before processing. We have not tested that change.

## What does it cost?

The free plan gives 100 minutes of transcripts and summaries each week and shows 30 days of history. Pro costs $9 per user each month and removes those limits.

## Our answer

Use Voicenotes for everyday thoughts, tasks, and meetings that can be repeated. Do not make it the only recording of an interview, client meeting, or anything else you cannot recreate.
