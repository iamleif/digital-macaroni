## Claims

- Voicenotes launched on May 13, 2024. [verified; fact; product-hunt]
- Voicenotes records notes and meetings, creates transcripts and summaries, supports search and AI questions, and can reuse captured material. [verified; fact; official-website]
- The official guide lists a free Basic plan with 100 minutes of weekly transcripts and summaries, and a Pro plan at $9 per user per month when checked. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Fast capture, searchable transcripts, summaries, simple access across devices, and reuse are repeated positive themes. [supported; reported-pattern; product-hunt, app-store, g2, reddit-current-use]
- Recording loss, failed uploads, cut-off transcription, invented output, and missing meeting features are material reliability concerns even though they are not the majority view. [supported; reported-pattern; product-hunt, app-store, reddit-reliability, reddit-current-use]
- Users value Voicenotes for quick notes and tasks but do not consistently trust it as the only record of important meetings. [supported; reported-pattern; reddit-reliability, app-store]
