## Buyer question

Can Voicenotes safely become the place where someone records, searches, and reuses spoken thoughts and meetings?

## Current facts

- Basic includes 100 minutes of transcripts and summaries each week, unlimited raw recordings, and access to 30 days of history.
- Pro costs $9 per user each month and adds unlimited transcripts, summaries, dictation, history, imports, storage, and advanced integrations.
- Apple lists version 2.1.5, released 2026-08-17, with a 4.77 rating from 6,591 ratings.
- Official documentation says raw recordings remain saved after the weekly transcript allowance is used.

## Supported opinion

Voicenotes is worth using for everyday thoughts and low-stakes meetings because it makes recordings searchable and reusable. It should not be the only recorder for an interview, client meeting, or other conversation that cannot be repeated. The large positive rating does not remove the cost of the detailed missing-recording cases.

## Limits

- Digital Macaroni did not test recording reliability.
- The research cannot establish how often recordings fail.
- Product Hunt, G2, and Trustpilot samples were captured during the earlier research pass; direct extraction was blocked during this refresh.
