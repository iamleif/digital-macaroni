# Voicenotes spoken review brief

Voicenotes is a recorder that gives you a transcript, a summary, and a way to search what you said later. That is much more useful than an audio file with a date for a name.

I would use it for daily thoughts, reminders, rough drafts, and ordinary meetings. The app has a large positive rating, and people like being able to find and reuse something they said weeks ago.

I would keep another recorder running for an interview, client meeting, or site visit. A few detailed accounts describe long recordings disappearing or stopping, including one case where desk use worked and walking around a property did not.

The free plan is good enough to test this. It has 100 minutes of transcripts and summaries each week, plus unlimited raw recordings. Pro is $9 a month. Pay when you need unlimited use and the app has already survived the sort of recording you care about.

The answer is straightforward: good for daily capture, unsafe as the only copy of something you cannot repeat.
