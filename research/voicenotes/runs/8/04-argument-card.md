# Voicenotes argument card

- Buyer: someone who captures thoughts and meetings by speaking.
- Job: make audio searchable and reusable without building a folder of forgotten recordings.
- Thesis: Voicenotes is worth using for daily capture, but its reliability record makes a backup recorder necessary for anything irreplaceable.
- Reason to buy: transcripts, summaries, search, and note questions make a recording useful after it ends.
- Reason to hesitate: detailed accounts describe long recordings disappearing or stopping.
- Decision rule: use Basic until long recordings survive the exact device and conditions; keep a second recorder whenever the conversation cannot be repeated.
- Evidence limit: no Digital Macaroni product test and no measured failure rate.
