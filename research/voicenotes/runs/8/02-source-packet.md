# Voicenotes source packet

1. Official site, https://voicenotes.com/
   - Recording for memos and meetings, transcription, summaries, search, questions, and reuse.
2. Pricing guide, https://help.voicenotes.com/en/articles/15389129-pricing-and-billing
   - Basic: 100 weekly transcript and summary minutes, unlimited raw recordings, 30-day history. Pro: $9 per user monthly with unlimited use and advanced integrations.
3. Apple App Store, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/voicenotes-ai-notes-meetings/id6483293628?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews
   - Current metadata: 4.77 from 6,591 ratings; version 2.1.5 released 2026-08-17. Earlier detailed sample contains praise for capture and serious missing-recording cases.
4. Product Hunt, https://www.producthunt.com/products/voicenotes
   - Stored sample: 31 voices; praise for capture, transcripts, summaries, search, and reuse; complaints about crashes, lost notes, and limited integrations.
5. Trustpilot, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/voicenotes.com
   - Stored sample covers ease of use, service, support, and billing.
6. G2, https://www.g2.com/sellers/voicenotes
   - Small visible sample praised access across phone, browser, desktop, and watch.
7. Reddit reliability discussion, https://www.reddit.com/r/Voicenotesai/comments/1kh4qt4/having_used_a_few_different_tools_in_the_past_i/
   - A paid customer found the product useful for quick notes and tasks but not dependable enough for regular transcription without a backup recording.
8. Reddit current-use discussion, https://www.reddit.com/r/Voicenotesai/comments/1vg0rib/all_infinally/
   - Current users praise improvement while continuing to discuss meeting, calendar, dictation, and speaker-name gaps.

## Detailed accounts retained from the sampled App Store material

- A two-week user described a paused 54-minute iPhone note disappearing, a separate two-hour Mac meeting disappearing, and Watch recordings ending unexpectedly.
- A landscape architect said office and Zoom recordings worked, while recordings made walking around client properties stopped and left short fragments.
- A frequent user praised capture but described stalled summaries, weak organization, and tedious one-by-one exports.
