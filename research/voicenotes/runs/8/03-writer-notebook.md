# Voicenotes writer's notebook

## Reader and job

- Reader: someone who thinks out loud and wants spoken material to remain useful later.
- Job: record an idea or conversation, find it by searching the transcript, and turn it into a summary, task list, or draft.
- Failure cost: a casual thought can be repeated; an interview, client meeting, or site visit may be impossible to recreate.

## Product facts

- Records memos and meetings, then creates transcripts and summaries.
- Lets the user search old material, ask questions about it, and reuse it in other writing.
- Basic includes 100 weekly transcript and summary minutes, unlimited raw recordings, and 30 days of visible history.
- Pro costs $9 monthly per user and removes those limits.
- Apple currently shows version 2.1.5 and a 4.77 rating from 6,591 ratings.

## Experience records

### Long recordings disappeared

- Source: sampled App Store review.
- Context: two-week user on iPhone, Mac, and Apple Watch.
- Events: a paused 54-minute note disappeared; a separate two-hour Mac meeting vanished; Watch recordings stopped.
- Consequence: keep a second recorder for any conversation that cannot be repeated.

### Field recording broke while desk recording worked

- Source: sampled App Store review.
- Context: landscape architect recording client conversations while walking properties.
- Result: office and Zoom use worked; mobile field recordings stopped and left short audio fragments.
- Consequence: test movement, screen locking, pausing, and the exact device rather than assuming a successful desk recording proves the field case.

### Paid user still kept a backup

- Source: Reddit.
- Context: paid customer using several transcription products.
- Result: strong for quick notes and tasks; not dependable enough for regular transcription work.
- Consequence: the useful capture habit and the reliability warning can both be true.

## Repeated patterns

- The product becomes useful after recording because the audio gains text, a summary, search, and reuse.
- The large positive rating supports the everyday-capture recommendation.
- Missing or cut-off recordings have much higher consequences than a mediocre summary.
- A new version number and official saving claim are reasons to retest, not proof that every older failure is fixed.

## Feature translation

- Transcription: a written copy that lets someone search instead of replaying audio files.
- Ask your notes: a question-based way to find something without remembering which recording contains it.
- Summary: a shorter account of a conversation; useful only when the source audio is complete.
- History: the Basic account stores older recordings but hides material beyond 30 days until the user upgrades.

## Conflict ledger

- A 4.77 rating can coexist with detailed recording failures because most captures may work while a small number of failures carry a large cost.
- Voicenotes can be a very good home for daily thoughts and a poor sole recorder for an irreplaceable conversation.
- Official documentation can promise saved raw recordings while the earlier user record still justifies a current backup test.
