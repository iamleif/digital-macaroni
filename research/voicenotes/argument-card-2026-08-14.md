# Voicenotes argument card

- **Buyer:** Someone who wants spoken thoughts to become searchable notes.
- **Job:** Capture an idea or conversation quickly, then find and reuse it later.
- **Thesis:** Voicenotes is excellent for everyday thoughts, but it has not earned the right to be the only recorder in the room.
- **Reason to buy:** Recording, transcription, summaries, search, and reuse stay together across devices.
- **Reason to hesitate:** Detailed accounts describe long recordings stopping or disappearing.
- **Decision rule:** Use it freely for notes you can repeat. Keep a second recorder for anything you cannot.
- **Evidence limit:** No hands-on test confirmed the latest recording changes.
