# Argument card

- Thesis: Voicenotes is excellent for low-stakes daily capture and should not be the only recorder for work that cannot be repeated.
- Best for: thoughts, reminders, drafts, tasks, and ordinary meetings.
- Skip: sole recording of interviews, legal talks, client meetings, and field visits.
- Price anchor: Basic 100 weekly transcript minutes; Pro $9 per user monthly during research.
- Verdict: Useful for daily thoughts, unsafe as your only recorder.
