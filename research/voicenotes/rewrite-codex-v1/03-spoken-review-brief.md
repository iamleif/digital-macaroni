# Spoken review brief

Voicenotes turns spoken notes into searchable text, summaries, and reusable writing. That is much better than a folder of audio files with date names.

Recommend it for daily thoughts and low-stakes meetings. Keep a second recorder for anything that cannot be repeated because detailed public accounts include lost, cut-off, and failed recordings.

Use the free plan to test long sessions, pauses, phone locks, app switches, and movement. State that no new hands-on test was run.
