# Writer notebook

- Product: records voice notes and meetings, then creates transcripts, summaries, search, questions, and reusable text.
- Buyer: someone who captures thoughts by voice and wants to find them later.
- Strength: quick capture becomes searchable and reusable across devices.
- Limit: reported lost, cut-off, or failed recordings make it unsafe as the only copy of important audio.
- Evidence limit: no new recording or transcription test was run; failure rates are unknown.
- Test: use Basic for long, paused, locked, moving, and interrupted recordings while keeping a second recorder.
