# Akiflow spoken review brief

## What is it?

Akiflow pulls tasks from apps such as Slack, Gmail, Todoist, Asana, and ClickUp into one inbox. You decide which ones matter, then drag them onto your calendar at the time you plan to do them.

## Who will find it useful?

Someone whose work is spread across several tools and who plans each day by the hour. It is not worth the price for a simple checklist.

## What is good?

The planning loop is clear. Collect first, choose what matters, and give the work a real time. The desktop app and keyboard controls are commonly praised for making that fast.

## What is wrong?

Akiflow depends on outside connections. Reviews and recent changelogs include sync and setup problems. The mobile app has improved, but it still needs a real test. The price is also high for one person's planner.

## What does it cost?

Pro is $34 month to month or $19 per month when billed for a year. The trial lasts seven days.

## Our answer

Import 30 real tasks from two work apps. Plan three real days and move unfinished work. Complete some tasks in Akiflow and some in the source app. Check that both sides agree. Then capture and reschedule from the phone. Pay only if this replaces daily app switching and the connections stay accurate.
