Akiflow is a productivity tool designed to help users manage tasks and calendars in one place. It was launched publicly on June 3, 2021, and is positioned as a solution for people who need to organize work across multiple platforms. The app imports tasks from email, messaging, project, and task apps into a centralized inbox, then allows users to place them on a calendar. This approach is meant to simplify daily planning by combining tasks and time blocks. The product is available as a single plan, priced at $34 per month or $19 per month billed yearly after a seven-day trial. No discounts or tiered options are mentioned in official sources. The app is available for desktop and mobile, though the mobile experience has been described as less polished compared to the desktop version. Recent updates have focused on fixing calendar sync issues, Gmail settings, and connections with apps like Asana, ClickUp, Microsoft To Do, and Todoist. These changes suggest ongoing maintenance and efforts to improve integration reliability. Akiflow’s onboarding process is considered clear, though importing tasks from multiple sources without clutter requires careful setup. The product itself is praised for its desktop planning capabilities and quick task capture, but sync bugs and a less developed mobile experience can undermine user confidence. Support is generally described as responsive and helpful, though no direct testing was conducted. The billing model is straightforward but may feel expensive for a personal planner. Overall, Akiflow is best suited for individuals who rely heavily on calendar-based planning and need to consolidate tasks from various apps into one place. While the app has strengths in ease of use and support, its price and mobile limitations may make it less appealing for others. The app’s effectiveness depends on how well users can adapt to its workflow and whether the integration benefits outweigh the potential drawbacks.
