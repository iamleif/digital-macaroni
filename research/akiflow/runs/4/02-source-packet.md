# Akiflow source packet — enforced run 4

## Official evidence

1. Akiflow pricing — https://akiflow.com/pricing — checked 2026-08-18
   - Seven-day trial.
   - Pro Monthly: $34 per month.
   - Pro Yearly: $19 per month billed yearly.
   - Lists tasks, meetings, integrations, Aki, time blocking, desktop/mobile, and two-way sync.

2. Integrations collection — https://product.akiflow.com/en/help/collections/1999346-integrations — checked 2026-08-18
   - Lists Google and Outlook calendars, Gmail, Outlook email, Slack, Teams, Notion, Microsoft To Do, Asana, Linear, Jira, GitHub, Trello, Zoom, Todoist, and ClickUp.

3. Inbox guide — https://product.akiflow.com/help/articles/5284502-your-inbox — checked 2026-08-18
   - New and imported tasks enter the Inbox.
   - Inbox work is separated from the Today schedule until it is planned.

4. Mobile guide — https://product.akiflow.com/en/help/articles/3431876-mobile-app — checked 2026-08-18
   - Mobile exposes the calendar, Inbox, task/event/time-slot creation, planning, widgets, notifications, and selected account connections.
   - Some integrations must still be connected on web or desktop.

5. Changelog — https://product.akiflow.com/changelog — checked 2026-08-18
   - July 2026 entries include fixes for Google and Outlook calendars, Gmail, Todoist, Asana, ClickUp, Microsoft To Do, Linear, mobile background sync, recurring tasks, and time zones.
   - The evidence supports active maintenance and also establishes that integration reliability is an active engineering surface.

## Independent and community evidence

6. Trustpilot — https://www.trustpilot.com/review/akiflow.com — checked 2026-08-18
   - Displayed 225 reviews and a 4.4 score.
   - Its recent-review summary emphasizes consolidation, interface quality, integrations, daily organization, and responsive support.
   - The same summary notes billing/trial complaints, onboarding overwhelm, and requests for broader calendar synchronization.

7. Critical Reddit account — https://www.reddit.com/r/ProductivityApps/comments/1md184r/my_unfiltered_review_of_akiflow/ — checked 2026-08-18
   - One paying user described duplicate time slots, tasks becoming detached during moves, limited Aki behavior, and poor support response.
   - Treat as a serious failure mode, not a prevalence estimate.

8. Todoist/Akiflow Reddit discussion — https://www.reddit.com/r/ProductivityApps/comments/1hvestq/todoist_vs_akiflow_is_akiflow_worth_the_shift_to/ — checked 2026-08-18
   - A trial user credited Akiflow's time blocking, daily ritual, Notion integration, and planning limits with reducing over-scheduling.
   - The same user found even a discounted annual price hard to justify; commenters also framed the normal price as expensive.
