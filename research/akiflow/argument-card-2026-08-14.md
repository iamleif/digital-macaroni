# Akiflow private argument card

- Buyer: a busy professional who plans by calendar and receives tasks in several apps
- Job: collect those tasks, choose what matters, and reserve time to do them
- Thesis: Akiflow is a strong daily planner for people committed to time blocking, but its price and reliance on outside connections make it a poor casual to-do app
- Main reason to buy: one planning inbox and one calendar replace repeated switching between work apps
- Main reason to hesitate: an import or sync failure can hide or duplicate work, and the phone experience has drawn complaints
- When it is unnecessary: tasks already live in one list, or the user does not plan work by time
- Decision rule: import 30 real tasks from two sources, plan three days, reschedule unfinished work, complete tasks from both Akiflow and a source app, and repeat capture and planning on the phone
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on test
