# Private argument card

- Buyer: someone whose work arrives in several apps and who plans most days on a calendar.
- Job: collect that work, decide what matters, and give each important task a realistic place in the day.
- Thesis: Akiflow is excellent at turning scattered tasks into a daily calendar plan, but its price only makes sense when that planning habit is already important.
- Reason to buy: it removes copying and app switching from a routine the buyer repeats every day.
- Reason to hesitate: the plan depends on integrations staying correct, while sync trouble and mobile gaps remain recurring concerns.
- Decision rule: use the trial with the two sources that create the most work. Pay only if tasks remain accurate in both directions and the daily plan saves enough time to justify $19 to $34 a month.
- Evidence limit: Digital Macaroni did not test the product. The evidence supports the overall pattern, but it does not establish a failure rate or prove that older mobile complaints still apply.
