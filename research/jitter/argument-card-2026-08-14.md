# Jitter argument card

- **Buyer:** A designer or marketer who already has visual assets and needs simple, polished movement.
- **Job:** Animate text, logos, charts, social graphics, or product screens without learning a full professional motion tool.
- **Thesis:** Jitter is a useful middle step between a static Figma file and a full After Effects project.
- **Reason to buy:** It carries Figma layers into a browser timeline and makes common movement quick to build.
- **Reason to hesitate:** It does not supply taste, pacing, sound, or the deeper control needed for complex motion work.
- **Decision rule:** Build one real project on Free; pay only when the watermark or export quality blocks delivery.
- **Evidence limit:** No Digital Macaroni hands-on test was performed, and feedback outside Product Hunt is thin.
