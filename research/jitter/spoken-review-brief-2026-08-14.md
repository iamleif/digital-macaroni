# Jitter spoken review brief

## What is it?

Jitter is a browser tool for making text, logos, charts, and app screens move. You can bring a layered Figma design into it, add movement, and save the result as a video, GIF, or web animation.

## Who will find it useful?

A designer or marketer who needs a short product animation or social post but does not want to build it in After Effects.

## What is good?

The Figma handoff and ready-made movement are the useful parts. Existing layers stay editable, and common text or screen animation does not require a long technical setup.

## What is wrong?

Jitter makes the controls easier. It does not make the creative choices. Bad pacing, unnecessary movement, weak sound, and cluttered design still produce a bad result. Complex motion also needs a deeper tool.

## What does it cost?

Free is enough to learn and test, but exports have a watermark and stop at 720p 30fps. Pro removes the watermark and adds 1080p. Max is for 4K, transparent, or frame-by-frame output. Check the live page for current per-editor prices.
