# Jitter spoken review brief

Jitter imports a Figma design with editable layers and lets a designer animate it in the browser. It is good for short product clips, logo movement, social posts, and simple web motion.

The controls are easy, but they do not make the motion good. Show the export to someone without explaining it. If their eye goes to the wrong place, remove movement.

I would use Free with a real project. I would pay only when the watermark, three-file limit, resolution, or export type blocks delivery.
