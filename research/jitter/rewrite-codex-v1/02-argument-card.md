# Jitter argument card

## Buyer

A designer or small marketing team that starts in Figma and needs simple motion without a full professional package.

## Decision

Use Free for a real project. Upgrade only when the watermark, file limit, resolution, background, or export format blocks delivery.

## Why

Editable Figma import removes rebuilding work, and the browser controls are approachable. Jitter cannot supply good timing, sound, or editing judgment.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if the viewer misses the message, the work needs complex effects or long edits, or the required output does not justify the paid tier.

## Verdict

Easy motion design without learning complex tools first.
