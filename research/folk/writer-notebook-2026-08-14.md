# folk writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no hands-on CRM setup
- Current pricing and mobile availability checked 2026-08-14
- Review sources: 327 G2, 10 Capterra, 20 Trustpilot, and two community discussions

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a small agency, startup, partnership team, recruiter, investor, or sales group managing relationships that do not fit a rigid call-center process
- Plain example: capture a person from LinkedIn, see prior emails and meetings, add them to a pipeline, assign the next step, and follow up without losing the relationship history
- Cost of failure: duplicate contacts split the history, private interactions are shared, an email sends to the wrong group, or management cannot see the pipeline it needs

## Product facts

- People and company records, groups, pipelines, enrichment, browser extension, email/calendar/WhatsApp sync, campaigns, reminders, AI fields and assistants
- Current mobile app documented on all plans
- Standard: $24 per member monthly billed yearly, or $30 month to month
- Premium: $48 yearly or $60 monthly; adds custom objects and deals, sequences, roles, dashboards, API, and full interaction history
- Enterprise: from $80 yearly or $100 monthly; adds deeper security, permissions, onboarding, and support
- Fourteen-day trial without card; account is blocked after trial until upgrade or extension
- Credits and message limits vary by plan

## Experience records

### A small team left a spreadsheet

- Simple setup and modern interface are repeated strengths
- Consequence: adoption may improve when normal users can update contacts without CRM training

### A relationship was captured from the browser

- Enrichment and browser capture are common reasons to choose folk
- Consequence: test duplicates, source accuracy, and history ownership

### A growing team needed deeper reporting or controls

- Analytics, price, and complex fit are recurring concerns
- Consequence: build the management report and permission case before buying Premium for everyone

## Feature translation

### Enrichment

- Fills missing public details such as work email, company, or job title

### Pipeline

- A set of stages showing where each relationship or deal stands

### Sequence

- A timed series of follow-up emails that stops or changes when the person responds

### Custom object

- A record type beyond people and companies, such as a deal, property, candidate, or partnership

## Conflict ledger

- Easy for a small team can mean too little control for a large one.
- Automatic enrichment saves research and can create wrong or duplicate data.
- Syncing private communication improves context and raises sharing questions.
