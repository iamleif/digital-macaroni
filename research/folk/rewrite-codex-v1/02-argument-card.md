# folk argument card

## Buyer

A small team that wins business through personal relationships and needs shared contact history plus one next action.

## Decision

Choose folk when normal teammates keep contacts current and managers can run the weekly meeting from it. Skip it for complex sales operations.

## Why

folk is easier to adopt than a heavy CRM and adds useful enrichment, browser capture, and message history. Simplicity limits deep forecasts, permissions, and specialist reports.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if duplicate cleanup is heavy, sensitive messages are too visible, campaigns do not stop correctly, or managers rebuild reports elsewhere.

## Verdict

A useful contact manager for small relationship-focused teams.
