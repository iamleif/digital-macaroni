# folk spoken review brief

folk is a shared contact list with deal steps, email history, notes, and reminders. It is useful for a small team that needs to know who owns a relationship and what happens next.

The trial should use messy contacts. Check duplicates, old jobs, and two email addresses. Connect two inboxes with one sensitive thread, then make sure the access rules work. Rebuild the weekly report too.

I would choose folk for an agency, recruiter, partnership team, or small sales group. I would skip it when managers need deep forecasts or complex controls.
