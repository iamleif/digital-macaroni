# folk spoken review brief

## What is it?

folk is a light CRM. It keeps people, companies, email and meeting history, deal or relationship stages, reminders, and follow-up work in one place.

## Who will find it useful?

A small sales, agency, partnership, recruiting, or investment team that finds a spreadsheet too loose and a large CRM too heavy.

## What is good?

It is quick to approach, can capture contacts from the browser, enrich missing details, and sync communication. The interface feels closer to a shared list than traditional sales software.

## What is wrong?

Per-seat cost adds up. Dashboards, email sequences, roles, API access, full history, and custom record types need Premium. Complex reporting and sales controls need careful testing.

## What does it cost?

With annual billing, Standard is $24 per member monthly and Premium is $48. Month-to-month prices are $30 and $60. Enterprise starts at $80 annually or $100 monthly per member.

## Our answer

Import a dirty sample of 100 contacts. Test duplicates, enrichment, private email visibility, a small campaign, mobile updates after a meeting, and the weekly management report. Choose folk if normal users keep one clear next step without creating cleanup work.
