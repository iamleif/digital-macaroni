# Frill spoken review brief

## What is it?

Frill gives software companies a public place for customers to suggest and vote on ideas, see a roadmap, and read product updates.

## Who will find it useful?

A small team that wants a focused feedback portal without buying a full customer support suite.

## What is good?

The board is clean, setup is quick, support is praised, and idea, roadmap, announcement, survey, widget, and integration tools sit together. Paid plans allow unlimited admins.

## What is wrong?

The independent evidence is small and very positive. Some features and integrations may be missing. Privacy and white labeling require the $149 Growth plan.

## What does it cost?

Startup is $25 a month for 50 active ideas. Business is $49 for unlimited ideas. Growth is $149 with privacy, unlimited surveys, and white labeling. Enterprise is $349. The trial lasts 14 days.

## Our answer

Import real ideas. Merge duplicates, check identity and private data, change a public status, publish an update, and confirm every requester receives the right message. Choose Frill only if someone owns that full loop each week.
