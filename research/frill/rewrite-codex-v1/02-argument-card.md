# Frill argument card

## Buyer

A small software team that needs one focused place for ideas, roadmap status, and shipped updates.

## Decision

Choose Frill when the full request-to-update loop is easy and someone owns it weekly. Skip it when feedback already lives well in another tool.

## Why

The portal is clean, quick to set up, and easy for several teams to share. Repeated ideas, private details, roadmap promises, and notifications require regular work.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if merges lose customer history, identity or privacy fails, roadmap changes confuse followers, update emails miss people, or Growth pricing is needed only for one control.

## Verdict

A focused feedback board that requires regular upkeep.
