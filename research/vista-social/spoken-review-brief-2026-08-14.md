# Vista Social spoken review brief

## What is it?

Vista Social is for teams that do more than schedule posts. It can also collect comments and private messages, handle client approval, track online reviews, watch brand mentions, and build reports.

## Who will find it useful?

An agency or marketing team with many profiles and clients. It is too much for one person who only wants a posting calendar.

## What is good?

The team can keep a client's posts, approvals, incoming messages, public reviews, and reports in one account. That can replace several smaller tools.

## What is wrong?

There is more to set up and learn. Profile linking problems recur in feedback. The phone apps do not match every web task, and the inbox can only pull what each social network allows. Billing also needs care because X and wider listening cost extra.

## What does it cost?

Monthly prices are $79 for 15 profiles, $149 for 30, and $349 for 70. The live page showed two, four, and eight included users, but a help page showed higher counts. Check the final checkout. X costs another $29 a month and wider listening costs $75.

## Our answer

Use the trial with one real client. Connect every profile, prepare and approve a week of posts, answer a message, reply to a review, and make the report the client expects. Check the same tasks on a phone. If that replaces several tools without missed work, Vista Social makes sense. If the team only likes the calendar, choose something smaller.
