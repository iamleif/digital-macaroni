# Vista Social private argument card

- Buyer: an agency or marketing team managing many profiles and more than just post scheduling
- Job: plan, approve, publish, answer, monitor reviews, and report without moving between several tools
- Thesis: Vista Social is useful when a team will use its breadth; the same breadth is wasted on a solo scheduler and creates more setup to prove
- Main reason to buy: publishing, inbox, reviews, permissions, and reports live together at a lower price than some large rivals
- Main reason to hesitate: profile connections, mobile gaps, add-ons, and conflicting user-count documentation
- When it is unnecessary: one person needs only a post calendar
- Decision rule: run one real client through a full week from draft and approval to inbox replies, review response, and report; test every profile and phone; save the final checkout total
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on test
