## Claims

- Vista Social combines post scheduling, approvals, reports, a shared message inbox, social listening, online review replies, and agency tools. [verified; fact; official-pricing, official-permissions, official-reviews, official-inbox]
- The live monthly pricing page lists Professional at $79 for 15 profiles, Advanced at $149 for 30, and Scale at $349 for 70. It also lists a $29 monthly X add-on and $75 monthly wider-listening add-on. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- The live pricing page showed two, four, and eight included users, while a separate help article showed three, six, and ten. [verified; fact; official-pricing, official-user-limits]
- Broad capability, centralized posting, the shared inbox, and responsive support are common strengths. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, capterra, trustradius, reddit]
- The number of tools creates a learning curve, while profile links, mobile use, and some reports cause recurring friction. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, trustpilot, reddit]
- Professional marketplaces are exceptionally positive, while Trustpilot and community sources contain more billing and reliability complaints. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, capterra, trustradius, trustpilot, reddit]
