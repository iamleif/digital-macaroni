# Vista Social writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no product test
- Live pricing, roles, review management, and inbox limits checked 2026-08-14
- G2: 4.8 from 1,076; TrustRadius: 9.6 from 44; Trustpilot: 4.2 from 78
- Main conflict: professional marketplaces are nearly all positive, while Trustpilot and community posts expose more friction

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: an agency or marketing team that publishes, answers comments, manages online reviews, and sends client reports for many profiles
- Plain example: schedule a client post, get approval, answer the comments from one inbox, reply to a Google review, and send the client a report
- Cost of failure: the wrong person publishes, a profile disconnects, a message is missed, or the bill includes an unexpected add-on

## Product facts

- Professional: $79 monthly, 15 profiles; live page showed two users
- Advanced: $149 monthly, 30 profiles; live page showed four users
- Scale: $349 monthly, 70 profiles; live page showed eight users
- Annual totals are discounted
- X publishing and analytics costs $29 monthly
- Wider listening across social, web, and news costs $75 monthly; tracking owned profiles is free
- Roles can be account-wide, limited to a profile group, or restricted by feature
- Contributors can prepare posts that require review before publishing
- Review management supports Facebook, Google Business, Trustpilot, Yelp, TripAdvisor, OpenTable, App Store, and Google Play
- Initial review import covers only the last 30 days

## Experience records

### An agency kept several jobs in one tool

- Source: repeated G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, and community pattern
- Action: plan posts, approve them, answer messages, watch reviews, and report from one account
- Result: less switching between a scheduler, inbox, and reporting app
- Consequence: breadth matters when the team uses several parts each week

### A profile link failed or needed attention

- Source: repeated G2 and outside-marketplace complaint
- Result: LinkedIn or Facebook connection trouble blocked part of the work
- Consequence: trial every important profile and decide who owns reconnection notices

### A team member used the phone

- Source: mixed G2, community, and official mobile documentation
- Result: some web functions and reports were not as complete on mobile
- Consequence: test the real after-hours tasks on a phone before assuming the app can run the account

## Feature translation

### Unified inbox

- What it does: collects supported comments and private messages from connected profiles in one list
- Main limit: networks expose different message types and history; old items may not be imported

### Listening

- What it does: watches for chosen words or brand mentions
- Main limit: tracking owned profiles is included, but wider web and news monitoring is an add-on

### Profile group

- What it does: keeps one client or brand's social profiles together and limits who can access them

## Conflict ledger

- The wide feature set can replace several tools, but it also makes setup and training heavier.
- Reviews praise ease, while learning curve and linking issues recur in the same large G2 sample.
- The live price page and help center disagree about included user counts.
