# Guidde writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no hands-on capture
- Pricing checked 2026-08-14
- 109 Product Hunt, 82 G2, one Chrome Store voice, and editorial context

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: support, training, operations, or product teams that make many short software walkthroughs
- Plain example: record how to add a user, turn it into steps with captions and voice, blur customer data, share it, then update it after the button moves
- Cost of failure: sensitive information appears, a step is wrong, captions or voice confuse the viewer, or an old guide teaches the wrong process

## Product facts

- Browser, desktop, and mobile capture depending on plan
- AI step creation, captions, voice, editing, brand kits, links, export, analytics, privacy
- Free: 25 guides
- Pro: $19 per creator annually or $29 monthly; unlimited guides, watermark removal, brand kit, exports, manual blur
- Business: $39 annually or $59 monthly; desktop capture, unlimited text-to-voice, file-to-video, privacy, analytics
- Enterprise: custom; premium voice, translation, automatic private-data redaction, SSO, review and version controls
- Seven-day Business trial; viewers do not use paid seats

## Experience records

### A support person turned a workflow into a guide

- Time saving and ease are repeated praise
- Consequence: Guidde is valuable for frequent short guides, not one rare company video

### An AI voice sounded mechanical

- Voice quality is a recurring limit
- Consequence: test names, numbers, acronyms, and the languages the audience uses

### The software screen changed after publication

- Maintenance risk follows from the format even when not a dominant review complaint
- Consequence: assign owner and review date to every guide

## Feature translation

### Redaction

- Covers names, email addresses, account numbers, or other private details in the recording

### Brand kit

- Saved logo, colors, fonts, intros, and other visual rules

### Creator seat

- A paid user who can make or manage guides; viewers can watch without a paid seat

### SCORM

- A package format used to put training content into a learning-management system

## Conflict ledger

- Fast AI creation still needs human accuracy and privacy review.
- Video is easier to follow and harder to keep current than a small text edit.
- Free viewers help distribution, while creator and add-on prices can grow.
