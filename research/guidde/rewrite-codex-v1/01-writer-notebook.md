# Guidde writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on capture, edit, voice, redaction, export, update, report, or support test. Eight sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Guidde records a software process, turns it into a step-by-step video, adds captions and voice, and exports links or files on paid plans.

The buyer is a support, training, product, or operations team that publishes many short software guides. Faster guide creation, an easy interface, annotations, and shareable output are the main strengths. Robotic AI voices, styling limits, private-data risk, and uncertain support depth are the limits.

The recommendation depends on the second edit. A real guide must handle hard words and fake private data, help a new user finish the task, then remain fast to update after two source screens change.
