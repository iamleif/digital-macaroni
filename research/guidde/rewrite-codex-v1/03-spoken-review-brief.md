# Guidde spoken review brief

Guidde records a software task and turns it into a short guide with captions and voice. It is useful when a team makes many support or training walkthroughs and does not want to edit every one from scratch.

The first draft is the easy part. Test the real script with company names and prices, hide fake private data, and let a new user follow the guide. Then move a button, remove a step, and time the repair.

I would use Guidde when the second edit stays fast. I would skip it for polished marketing video or a six-line process that is easier to keep as text.
