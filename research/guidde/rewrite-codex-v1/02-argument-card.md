# Guidde argument card

## Buyer

A support or training team that makes many short software walkthroughs and needs faster first drafts.

## Decision

Choose Guidde when reviewed guides save time and remain easy to update. Skip it for deep creative video work or simple steps better kept as text.

## Why

Capture and AI can speed the first version. Voice, redaction, access, exports, and product changes still require careful human work.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if hard words stay unclear, private details leak, sharing rules fail, a new user cannot follow the guide, or updates take longer than the old method.

## Verdict

Fast software guides that still require careful editing.
