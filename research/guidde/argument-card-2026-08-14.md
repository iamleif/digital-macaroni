# Guidde private argument card

- Buyer: a team that repeatedly documents software steps for customers or staff
- Job: turn a screen process into a clear guide quickly and keep it correct after the product changes
- Thesis: Guidde can save real production time for a library of short software guides, but the purchase should be decided by update speed, voice quality, and redaction rather than the first AI draft
- Main reason to buy: fast step creation without full video editing skill
- Main reason to hesitate: mechanical voices, design limits, private-data risk, creator costs, and stale content
- When it is unnecessary: the company makes few guides, needs polished creative video, or a short text article is easier to maintain
- Decision rule: record a real process, include private data and hard names, publish video and document versions, then change two screens and time the update; ask a new user to complete the task without help
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on capture or viewer test
