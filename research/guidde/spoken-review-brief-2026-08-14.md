# Guidde spoken review brief

## What is it?

Guidde records a software process and turns it into a step-by-step video with captions, voice, annotations, links, and document exports.

## Who will find it useful?

A support, training, operations, or product team that publishes many short guides showing how software works.

## What is good?

It can make the first version much faster than manual video editing. The interface, annotations, and sharing are praised often.

## What is wrong?

AI voices can sound mechanical, design control has limits, and private details need careful redaction. A video library also becomes stale when the product changes.

## What does it cost?

Free supports 25 guides. Pro is $19 per creator monthly annually or $29 monthly. Business is $39 annually or $59 monthly. Viewers are free. The Business trial lasts seven days.

## Our answer

Record a process with private details, hard names, and several steps. Publish video and document versions. Then change two screens and time the update. Choose Guidde if a new user completes the task and the team can keep the library current.
