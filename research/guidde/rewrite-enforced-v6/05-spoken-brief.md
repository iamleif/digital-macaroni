Guidde is a tool that lets teams create short, step-by-step video guides from screen recordings. It adds captions, voiceovers, and supports exporting guides in video, presentation, or PDF formats. The free version lets users make 25 guides, while paid plans offer more features and team management tools. It's meant for teams that need to create many guides quickly, especially for software walkthroughs. The main benefit is speed, users say it's fast to make guides and the interface is simple. However, the AI voice feels robotic, and there aren't many customization options. Support and billing are not as strong as other aspects. If a team needs to publish many short guides and has a clear process, Guidde can work well. But if they need deep customization or strong support, it might not be the best fit. Before paying, check if re-recording workflows is a viable alternative. Also, consider whether the AI voice and limited customization will require extra work.
