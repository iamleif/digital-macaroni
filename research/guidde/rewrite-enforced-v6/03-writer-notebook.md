Guidde is a video and audio tool that records a process, turns it into a step-by-step video guide, adds captions and voice, and supports exports to video, presentation, and PDF formats on paid plans. It launched publicly on November 14, 2022, and is available through its official website and the Chrome Web Store. The product is positioned as a tool for creating repeatable software walkthroughs, rather than a full video editor or an automatic source of truth.

The free plan supports 25 guides, while the Pro plan costs $19 per creator monthly, billed annually or $29 monthly. The Business plan is $39 annually or $59 monthly, with a seven-day trial. These pricing details are based on official sources.
