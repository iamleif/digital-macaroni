  "body": "Guidde is designed for teams that need to create many short, step-by-step video guides quickly. It allows users to record screen activity, convert it into a guide with captions and voiceovers, and export the result in various formats like video, presentation, or PDF. The main tradeoff is that while the tool excels at speed and simplicity, it lacks deep customization and robust support. If your team needs to publish many short guides with a clear process, Guidde can be a strong fit. However, if you require extensive customization or reliable support, it may not be the best choice. Before paying, consider whether re-recording workflows is a viable alternative and whether the AI voice and limited customization will require extra work."
