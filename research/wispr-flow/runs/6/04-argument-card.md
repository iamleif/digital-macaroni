# Wispr Flow argument card

- Buyer: a Mac user who writes for hours and can speak comfortably at work.
- Job: put clean text into the email, document, chat, or AI tool already open without a copy-and-paste step.
- Thesis: Wispr Flow is worth trying because its cleanup can replace real typing, but paying makes sense only after the exact device, room, language, and privacy settings have worked in the free plan.
- Reason to buy: it removes filler and false starts while formatting the finished text for the app under the cursor.
- Reason to hesitate: speech always uses cloud processing, Context Awareness is on by default on desktop, and the detailed device record is strongest on Mac.
- Decision rule: pay when the free plan consistently leaves less editing than typing and the data controls are acceptable.
- Evidence limit: no Digital Macaroni product test; one direct support case; mixed public accounts outside Mac.
