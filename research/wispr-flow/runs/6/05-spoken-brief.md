# Wispr Flow spoken review brief

Wispr Flow lets you hold a key and talk into whatever app you already have open. It puts the words where the cursor is, removes filler, and can understand when you correct yourself halfway through a sentence.

The cleanup is why I would tell a Mac user to try it. Ordinary dictation often creates a transcript that needs another edit. Wispr Flow can leave much less repair. That is useful when typing is the slow part of the job.

I would not tell someone to subscribe on the strength of Mac praise if they mainly use Windows, an iPhone, or an iPad. The detailed accounts are much more mixed there. Language matters too. One multilingual user described English, German, and Spanish being mixed in the same output.

The privacy settings need a plain explanation. Your speech goes to the cloud at every setting. Privacy Mode stops retention and model training. Context Awareness is a separate feature that can send text around the cursor, app details, screenshots, and conversation context. It is on by default on Mac and Windows.

The free plan gives a desktop user 2,000 words each week. That is enough to try the shortcut, microphone, language, apps, and corrections. Pro costs $15 month to month or $12 a month with annual billing.

My answer is yes for the right Mac user. Try it in the actual room and apps where you work. Pay if it saves a meaningful amount of typing and the cloud settings are acceptable. Skip it when work must stay on the device.
