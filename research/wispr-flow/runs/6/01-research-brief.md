## The buyer question

Is Wispr Flow worth paying for when someone wants to speak into the email, document, chat, or other app already open on their computer?

## Facts that changed since the published review

- Pro is $15 per user each month when paid monthly. The $12 figure applies when billed annually.
- The free limits remain 2,000 words a week on desktop and 1,000 on iPhone. Android dictation is unlimited.
- Context Awareness is on by default on Mac and Windows. It may send nearby text, on-screen content, screenshots, and conversation context with a dictation request.
- Privacy Mode stops retention and model training. Dictation still runs in the cloud.
- The official pricing page now includes a Mac-only meeting notetaker. The review remains focused on dictation because that is where the user evidence is strongest.

## Supported opinion

Wispr Flow is worth trying for a Mac user who writes for hours and can comfortably speak at work. Its cleanup and system-wide shortcut can remove a real amount of typing. The recommendation is weaker on other devices, and anyone handling sensitive material must understand the cloud and context settings before using it.
