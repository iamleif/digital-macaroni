# Wispr Flow source packet

## Official product and pricing

1. Wispr Flow official site
   - URL: https://wisprflow.ai/
   - Checked: 2026-08-18
   - Supports: dictation across existing apps, automatic cleanup, context-aware formatting, 100-plus languages.

2. Wispr Flow pricing
   - URL: https://wisprflow.ai/pricing
   - Checked: 2026-08-18
   - Supports: Free plan; 2,000 desktop words per week; 1,000 iPhone words per week; unlimited Android dictation; Pro at $15 monthly or $12 per month billed annually; unlimited Pro dictation.

3. Privacy Mode and Private Cloud Sync documentation
   - URL: https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/4709791908-understanding-privacy-mode-and-cloud-sync
   - Checked: 2026-08-18
   - Supports: transcription always uses the cloud; Privacy Mode controls training and retention; local storage and private cloud sync are separate settings; Share Data is preselected during onboarding.

4. Context Awareness documentation
   - URL: https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/4678293671-feature-context-awareness
   - Checked: 2026-08-18
   - Supports: on by default on Mac and Windows; reads the active app and nearby text; context may include screenshots and conversation content; standard macOS password fields are excluded; custom web password fields may be read as ordinary fields.

## Direct support record

Digital Macaroni contacted Wispr Flow support four times over one month. The first two contacts did not solve the issue. The third response requested an explanation that had already been supplied. Help arrived after the fourth contact, and the last follow-up was left unread.
