# Argument card

- Thesis: Wispr Flow is worth trying for Mac users when cleanup saves real typing and cloud handling is acceptable.
- Best for: heavy Mac writing across email, documents, and chat.
- Skip: speech or screen context that cannot leave the device.
- Price anchor: Free has word limits; Pro was $12 per user monthly during research.
- Support limit: Digital Macaroni needed four contacts for help.
- Verdict: Fast voice writing, with privacy settings you must check.
