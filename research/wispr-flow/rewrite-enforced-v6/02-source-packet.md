## Claims

- Wispr Flow launched on September 30, 2024. [verified; fact; product-hunt]
- Wispr Flow provides system-wide AI dictation, automatic editing, context-aware formatting, and multilingual use across desktop and mobile. [verified; fact; official-website]
- Wispr Flow Pro costs $12 per user each month. The free plan allows 2,000 words per week on desktop, 1,000 on iPhone, and unlimited dictation on Android. [verified; fact; official-pricing]
- Wispr Flow always processes dictation in the cloud. Privacy Mode controls model training, while Private Cloud Sync separately controls whether transcription data is stored on Wispr's servers. [verified; fact; official-privacy-controls]
- Context Awareness can read nearby text and app information. Its screen OCR option is off by default and only captures a screenshot when both context controls are enabled. [verified; fact; official-context-awareness]
- Fast writing, strong dictation accuracy, useful cleanup, and cross-app use are repeated positive themes. [supported; reported-pattern; product-hunt, g2, app-store, reddit]
- The Mac experience is repeatedly described as stronger and easier than the Windows, iPhone, and iPad experiences. [supported; reported-pattern; app-store, kintal]
- Privacy, cloud processing, screenshot context, resource use, device friction, and free-tier limits are recurring concerns. [supported; reported-pattern; product-hunt, app-store, reddit, kintal]
- Digital Macaroni emailed Wispr Flow support four times over one month. The third contact received a template asking for information already supplied, support assisted after the fourth contact, and the final follow-up was left unread. [verified; hands-on-observation; ]
