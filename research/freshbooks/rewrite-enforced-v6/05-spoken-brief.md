# Spoken review brief

FreshBooks is very good at the work around a service invoice. A freelancer can track hours, add expenses, send the bill, and follow up without learning a heavy accounting system.

The price grows faster than the business may expect. Plans cap billable clients, team members cost extra, and deeper reports or stock work can push the buyer elsewhere.

I would run one real billing cycle, ask the accountant to open every required report, and export the records. Keep FreshBooks only when that whole path works and the next year's client and staff count still fits the budget.
