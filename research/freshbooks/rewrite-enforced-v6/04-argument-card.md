# Private argument card

- Buyer: a freelancer or service business centered on client billing
- Job: move from completed work to a paid invoice with little bookkeeping friction
- Thesis: FreshBooks makes service billing easy, but client caps and add-ons weaken its value as the business grows
- Reason to buy: approachable invoices, time tracking, expenses, reminders, and mobile work
- Reason to hesitate: client limits, paid team seats, weaker inventory and custom reporting, and exit uncertainty
- Decision rule: keep it when one real billing cycle and the accountant's required reports work at a fair twelve-month cost
- Evidence limit: no Digital Macaroni hands-on or support test
