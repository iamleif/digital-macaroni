# FreshBooks writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Review type: research-based; no hands-on product access
- Official sources: product site, pricing, and pricing help
- Independent sources: G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, and a community pricing discussion
- Main limit: support and cancellation behavior were not tested directly

## Reader and job

- Buyer: a freelancer or small service firm
- Job: track time and expenses, send invoices, collect payment, and hand clean records to an accountant
- Cost of failure: rising client and staff fees or incomplete reports after migration

## Decision evidence

- Reviewers repeatedly praise setup, invoicing, time tracking, mobile access, and human support.
- Inventory, detailed reporting, customization, and search are weaker fits.
- Regular monthly prices are $23 Lite, $43 Plus, and $70 Premium.
- Lite and Plus cap billable clients at five and 50; standard plans charge $11 per added team member.
- A reported cancellation concern makes export testing part of the decision.

## Evidence boundary

The article treats the cancellation account as one experience and recommends verifying access and exports rather than claiming data loss is universal.
