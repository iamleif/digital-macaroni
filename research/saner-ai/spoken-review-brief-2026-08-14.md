# Saner.AI spoken review brief

## What is it?

Saner.AI is a place for notes and tasks with an assistant on top. You can dump a messy thought into it, ask it to make reminders, and search things you saved without remembering the folder.

## Who will find it useful?

Someone who keeps notes in several places and has trouble turning them into a plan. The product is built around ADHD, so it tries to reduce the amount of sorting and setup.

## What is good?

The brain-dump idea makes sense. Some users say it turns messy thoughts into reminders and gives them a useful plan each morning.

## What is wrong?

One paid user tried to change tasks through the assistant and ended up with duplicate versions that had to be deleted by hand. The assistant could not move or delete tasks, missed subtasks, and did not keep completed items in sync between the web and phone.

That is a serious problem for this audience. An organization app for someone who already feels overloaded cannot keep adding cleanup.

## What does it cost?

The current page lists a free plan, Starter at $8 a month, and Standard at $16. It also shows a $12 early-user offer for Standard.

## Our answer

Try the free plan if the brain-dump idea sounds useful. Create, edit, move, finish, and delete several tasks before connecting email, calendar, and a full note library. Do not pay if those basic steps still create duplicates or need manual repair.
