# Saner.AI argument card

- **Buyer:** A person with scattered notes and tasks who wants help starting and staying organized.
- **Job:** Capture thoughts, find saved information, and keep a usable task list.
- **Thesis:** Saner.AI has the right idea for an overwhelmed user, but basic task mistakes still make it another system to supervise.
- **Reason to buy:** Brain dumps can become reminders, and the assistant can search notes and connected accounts.
- **Reason to hesitate:** Task edits, deletion, duplication, crashes, and sync have produced detailed complaints.
- **Decision rule:** Test the basic task actions before importing a large note collection or connecting work accounts.
- **Evidence limit:** No hands-on test was performed, and public samples are small.
