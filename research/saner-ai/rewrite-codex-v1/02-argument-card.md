# Saner.AI argument card

## Buyer

Someone who needs help turning unstructured thoughts into notes and tasks without organizing them first.

## Decision

Treat Saner.AI as an experiment until the same tasks can be edited, deleted, completed, and synced without duplicates.

## Why

Its capture idea can lower the effort needed to begin. The maintenance layer remains too fragile for a trusted daily system.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if tasks duplicate, completion drifts across devices, the assistant cannot change existing items, navigation hides work, or export and backup are incomplete.

## Verdict

Great capture, too fragile for a trusted daily system.
