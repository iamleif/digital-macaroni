# Saner.AI spoken review brief

Saner.AI is for someone who wants to dump thoughts into one place and have the software turn them into notes and tasks. That can make starting much easier.

The harder job is keeping those tasks correct. Duplicate items, confusing changes, sync problems, and limits on editing through the assistant show up often enough to stop us trusting it as the only daily system.

I would use the free plan with disposable work. Create, edit, delete, reschedule, and complete the same items on web and mobile. Keep the real backup elsewhere until every change remains true.
