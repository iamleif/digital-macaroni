# Saner.AI writer's notebook

Research-based review with no new hands-on test. Eight sources were checked August 13, 2026. Saner.AI describes itself as an ADHD-focused assistant that brings notes, tasks, email, calendar, drive, Slack, and saved knowledge into one place.

The buyer is someone who struggles to turn a brain dump into useful notes and tasks. Quick capture, task extraction, and bringing scattered information together are the main strengths. Bugs, confusing navigation, duplicate tasks, weak assistant editing, sync, backup, and fast product changes are the main concerns.

The recommendation is cautious and direct. Use the free plan for a disposable 30-item trial. Edit, delete, complete, reschedule, and sync the same tasks across devices. Do not trust Saner.AI with the only copy of important work until basic maintenance stays correct.
