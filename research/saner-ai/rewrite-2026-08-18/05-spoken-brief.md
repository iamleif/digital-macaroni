Saner.AI aims to help people with ADHD or information overload by turning messy notes into clear tasks. It sounds perfect on paper. Early reviews love it. But closer look at user reports reveals a different story. People report duplicate tasks, sync errors between phone and computer, and an assistant that cannot delete items. The app is great for capturing thoughts. It struggles to keep those thoughts organized without human intervention. If you need a reliable system, wait. If you want to experiment with AI capture, try the free plan.
