Saner.AI is an AI-powered tool designed to help people organize their thoughts, tasks, and information, particularly those with ADHD. It allows users to input raw ideas, often called brain dumps, and turns them into structured tasks or notes. The tool also helps consolidate scattered information from emails, calendars, and other sources into a cohesive system. The free version includes basic note-taking and task management, while paid plans add email, calendar, and Slack integration. Saner.AI is still in early development, and users report several issues, including bugs, confusing navigation, and incomplete features. Despite these problems, many users praise its ability to manage ADHD-related challenges, such as staying focused and organizing scattered thoughts. However, reliability remains a concern, with some users experiencing duplicate tasks or limited editing options. Support is mixed, with some users finding the founder responsive and others facing slow or unhelpful responses. The overall score of 7.2 suggests it’s promising but not yet stable. If you’re someone who needs help with ADHD-related organization and are willing to experiment, Saner.AI could be worth trying. But be cautious about relying on it for daily use until it becomes more reliable. Before paying, check if the free version meets your needs and whether the paid features align with your workflow. Also, consider the product’s ongoing development and how quickly it might evolve.
