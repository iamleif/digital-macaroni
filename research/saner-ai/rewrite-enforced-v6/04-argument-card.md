Saner.AI is a tool for people who need help organizing thoughts, tasks, and information, especially those with ADHD. It uses AI to turn brain dumps into actionable items and consolidate scattered data. The free plan includes note-taking and task management, while paid tiers add email, calendar, and Slack integration. The product is still in early development and has notable bugs, confusing navigation, and incomplete features. Users praise its ability to help with ADHD-related organization but report reliability issues like duplicate tasks and editing limits. Support is mixed, with some users finding the founder helpful and others facing slow responses. The overall score of 7.2 suggests it's promising but not yet reliable. I recommend Saner.AI for those willing to experiment and provide feedback as it evolves, but caution against relying on it for daily use until it stabilizes.
