# Saner.AI Review

### The Short Answer

Saner.AI is an AI-powered assistant designed to help people, especially those with ADHD, organize notes, tasks, emails, and calendars into a single system. It aims to simplify information overload by turning scattered thoughts into actionable tasks and retrievable knowledge. The product has potential, but it’s still early in its development. While some users praise its ability to bring order to chaos, others find it buggy and incomplete. If you’re willing to tolerate occasional frustrations and want a tool focused on ADHD support, Saner.AI might be worth a try. However, if you need a polished, reliable system, this isn’t it yet.

### What It’s Good At

Saner.AI excels at turning unstructured information into organized tasks and notes. Users repeatedly highlight its ability to capture brain dumps and transform them into actionable items. The integration with email, calendar, and Slack helps centralize information, making it easier to manage daily workflows. For people with ADHD, this focus on reducing mental clutter is a standout feature. The product also offers a free plan, allowing users to test its fit without commitment. Its emphasis on knowledge retrieval means you can quickly find stored information without digging through multiple apps.

### Where It Gets in the Way

The biggest drawback is its lack of polish. Users report bugs, confusing navigation, and duplicate tasks. Editing or deleting items through the assistant can be frustrating, and some basic actions feel incomplete. Backup and sync options are limited, and multilingual support is lacking. These issues make Saner.AI feel more like a work in progress than a finished product. Additionally, while the free plan is a plus, paid tiers require careful consideration because the core experience is still evolving. Some users have also complained about slow cancellation processes and unresolved bugs.

### What Using It Is Likely to Feel Like

Using Saner.AI can feel like having a helpful but inconsistent assistant. On good days, it simplifies your workflow by centralizing notes, tasks, and emails. On bad days, bugs and navigation issues can make it feel like more trouble than it’s worth. The learning curve is steeper than expected, especially for users already feeling overwhelmed. Quick capture and knowledge retrieval work well, but editing and task management often require extra effort. If you’re patient and willing to adapt to its quirks, Saner.AI can be useful. If you prefer a seamless experience, this might not be the right fit.
