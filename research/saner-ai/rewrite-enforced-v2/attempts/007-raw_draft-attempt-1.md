Saner.AI is an AI assistant designed for people with ADHD, aiming to help them manage tasks, notes, emails, and calendars by turning chaotic brain dumps into organized action. It’s meant for someone who feels overwhelmed by distractions and struggles to keep track of their responsibilities. The main feature is its ability to take unstructured ideas and convert them into structured tasks, which can be a big help for those who find it hard to focus or remember what they need to do.
