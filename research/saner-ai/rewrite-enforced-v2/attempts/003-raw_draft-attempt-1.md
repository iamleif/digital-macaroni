Saner.AI is an AI assistant tailored for people with ADHD, promising to transform chaotic brain dumps into structured tasks, notes, and calendar entries. Its core value lies in reducing cognitive load by automating the initial step of organizing unstructured ideas. For users who feel overwhelmed by distractions and struggle to maintain focus, the ability to capture thoughts without prior formatting could be a significant relief. However, the product's early-stage development introduces a major tradeoff: its promise of streamlining daily life is undermined by frequent bugs, confusing navigation, and incomplete features. These issues make it unreliable for consistent use, even as its ADHD-focused design appeals to a specific audience. The question is whether the potential benefits outweigh the current limitations, especially for users who are willing to experiment with a nascent tool.
