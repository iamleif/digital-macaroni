Saner.AI is an AI assistant designed for people with ADHD, aiming to help them manage tasks, notes, emails, and calendars by turning chaotic brain dumps into organized action. It’s meant for someone who feels overwhelmed by distractions and struggles to keep track of their responsibilities. The main feature is its ability to take unstructured ideas and convert them into structured tasks, which can be a big help for those who find it hard to focus or remember what they need to do. Some users praise its potential, especially for organizing information and capturing tasks quickly, which aligns with its ADHD-focused promise. However, the product is still in early development, and several users report bugs, confusing navigation, and incomplete features. These issues make it less reliable for daily use. A few users have shared that the tool helped them feel more in control of their work, but others found it frustrating or incomplete. If you’re someone who needs an ADHD-friendly tool and is willing to experiment with a nascent product, Saner.AI might be worth trying. But before paying, check if you can try it for free first and look for updates on its reliability and feature completeness.
