Saner.AI is a relatively new AI tool that positions itself as an ADHD-focused assistant. It integrates notes, tasks, email, and calendar functions, with the goal of helping users bring scattered information together. The product is available in a free plan and paid tiers, with features like email and calendar connections, task management, and knowledge retrieval. The company's official website describes its product as a way to help users manage their mental load through AI assistance.

User reports highlight the product's ability to help with task capture and information organization, which are often praised as useful for people who feel overwhelmed. However, several recurring issues have been noted, including bugs, confusing navigation, and limits on editing or deleting through the assistant. Some users also report that the product feels incomplete, with features that are still in development or not fully functional.

The product has received mixed reviews. Product Hunt users have given it high praise, while App Store ratings are lower. Trustpilot has too few reviews to be meaningful. These differences suggest that the product may have evolved since its public release, and that user experiences vary depending on the version they are using.

Saner.AI is a product that aims to simplify complex mental tasks through AI. It has potential, but its current state suggests it is still early in development. For users who are looking for a more polished, reliable tool, Saner.AI may not yet be the right fit. However, for those who are willing to experiment with a nascent product and are interested in an AI assistant tailored to ADHD needs, it could be worth exploring.
