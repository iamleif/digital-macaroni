# Quo argument card

## Buyer

A small service business where several people share calls and texts with the same customers.

## Decision

Use Quo after real calls, messages, support, and a written number-port plan all pass.

## Why

The shared conversation is easy for a small team to understand. A phone product still fails its main job when audio, messages, or a live number become unreliable.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if calls lag or drop at a required location, messages arrive late, support cannot own an urgent case, port records remain unclear, or the full quote exceeds the value of shared history.

## Verdict

Excellent shared phones after reliability and porting prove safe.
