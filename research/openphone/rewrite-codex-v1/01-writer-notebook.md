# Quo writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on number, call, text, port, support, emergency-call, or billing test. Eleven sources were checked August 14, 2026. OpenPhone launched in 2018 and later became Quo. It gives a small team shared business calls, texts, contacts, notes, and routing across phones and computers.

The buyer is a small service business where two or more people answer the same customers. The clean shared history and easy setup are the main strengths. Call quality, message delivery, number porting, urgent support ownership, and extra charges are the main risks.

The recommendation is positive after a temporary-number trial across every real device, network, location, and busy hour. Finish messaging registration, test support, plan the number port, and keep the old line active until incoming calls and texts work. Unstable audio at an unavoidable location is a walk-away condition.
