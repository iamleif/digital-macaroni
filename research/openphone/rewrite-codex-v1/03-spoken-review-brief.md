# Quo spoken review brief

Quo gives a small team one business number for calls and texts. Everyone can see the customer history, leave a private note, and know who should reply. It is much easier to grasp than an old office phone system.

The app's clean design cannot rescue delayed audio, a missed message, or a number stuck during a move. Those failures appear often enough to make a real trial essential.

I would use a temporary number across every phone, network, work site, and busy hour. I would also test support and keep the old carrier active until the number port and both-way messages work.
