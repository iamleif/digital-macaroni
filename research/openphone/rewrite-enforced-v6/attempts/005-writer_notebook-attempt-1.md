  "content": "Quo is a business phone system that allows small teams to share phone numbers, manage calls and texts, and collaborate on messages. It was originally known as OpenPhone and launched publicly in January 2018. Quo rebranded in 2021 with a focus on small businesses, offering plans for individual users, teams, and scaled operations. The core product includes shared call and text lines, internal collaboration tools, and integration with other business software. Quo’s pricing structure is straightforward, with plans starting at $19 per user per month for the Starter tier, $33 for Business, and $47 for Scale. Additional numbers cost $5 each, and taxes and fees may apply. The product emphasizes ease of use, with a clean interface and mobile access, though the actual performance depends on carrier networks, local infrastructure, and the service itself.\n\nThe evidence shows that Quo is generally well-received for its setup process and user experience. Many users find the system intuitive, especially for small teams looking to share phone lines and manage communications from multiple devices. However, the same reviews also mention recurring issues such as connection problems, dropped calls, and message delays. These are often attributed to carrier dependencies rather than the product itself. Support is a mixed area, with praise for helpful staff in routine situations but criticism for slow or unhelpful responses during critical issues like number porting or billing disputes.\n\nThe review highlights that Quo is a strong option for small businesses seeking a modern, feature-rich phone system. However, the product’s performance in real-world scenarios depends heavily on external factors beyond its control. Users are advised to consider their specific needs, including call quality, porting requirements, and support expectations, before committing. While Quo offers a compelling set of tools, its success ultimately hinges on the reliability of the underlying network and the responsiveness of its support team during unexpected challenges."
