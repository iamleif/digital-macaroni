# Trae writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Review type: research-based; no hands-on product access
- Official sources: product site, documentation, and current pricing
- Independent sources: Product Hunt, G2, and a community pricing discussion
- Main limit: the public review sample is small, especially for support

## Reader and job

- Buyer: a developer or small team considering a lower-cost AI code editor
- Job: complete edits and larger coding tasks without losing control of the code
- Cost of failure: confident but broken changes, leaked private code, or time lost repairing generated work

## Decision evidence

- Editor and SOLO modes let the developer change the level of agent control.
- Product Hunt and G2 accounts praise the interface and project awareness.
- Independent accounts also report failed fixes, lost context, hallucinations, and weak support.
- Current plans range from Free through $3 Lite, $10 Pro, $30 Pro+, and $100 Ultra.
- Basic Usage allowances mean the subscription price alone does not show the working limit.

## Evidence boundary

The article attributes reported failures and privacy objections. It does not claim a failure rate or that Trae mishandled private code.

## Spoken language bank

Use: clean diff, known bug, run the tests, private code, usage allowance, review every change.

Avoid: revolutionary coding, seamless autonomy, enterprise-grade security.
