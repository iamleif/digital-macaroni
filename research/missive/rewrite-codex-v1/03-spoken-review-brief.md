# Missive spoken review brief

Missive is built for the moment when two people reply to the same customer, or everyone waits because nobody owns the message. It lets a team assign an email, discuss it in private, and write the reply together beside the customer thread.

Search is the main test. Some users report weak or slow results for older mail, and behavior can depend on the email provider. Rules and phone alerts also take care during setup.

I would choose Missive for a team that truly shares email work. I would trial one live address with twenty messages, search for known old mail, and check every phone before moving the rest.
