# Missive argument card

## Buyer

A support, sales, or agency team where several people regularly handle the same email conversations.

## Decision

Use Missive when clear ownership and private context stop duplicate replies, provided search works with the team's provider.

## Why

Assignment and comments solve a costly shared-inbox problem. Search and notification friction can undermine the record when email history matters.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if known old messages do not appear, phone states disagree, rules hide work, the team rarely shares conversations, or the per-user cost exceeds the time saved.

## Verdict

Strong shared email once search proves reliable.
