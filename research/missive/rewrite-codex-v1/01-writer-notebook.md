# Missive writer's notebook

Research-based review with no new hands-on inbox, assignment, comment, search, rule, mobile, support, or billing test. Nine sources were checked on August 13 and 14, 2026. Missive combines personal email with shared inboxes, assignment, private team comments, and joint replies.

The buyer is a support, sales, or agency team where several people handle the same email conversations. Ownership and context are the main strengths. Search, notification choices, mobile read state, and the learning curve are the main limits. Solo users receive little value from the team features.

The recommendation is strong after a narrow trial. Connect one real shared address, process twenty messages, test known old mail, check every phone, and confirm the plan cost against the time saved. A failed search test is a walk-away condition for teams that use email as a customer record.
