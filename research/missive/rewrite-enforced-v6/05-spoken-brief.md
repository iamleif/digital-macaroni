Missive is a shared email tool that lets teams use one inbox together. It lets people assign messages, add private comments, and prepare replies as a group. This helps avoid duplicate replies and keeps context in one place. It’s best for small teams that need to work on emails as a group. If your team often has to reply to the same message multiple times or lose track of who’s responding, Missive could save time. But it’s not perfect. Some users say searching for old messages is slow, especially if they use email providers other than Gmail or Outlook. Also, the number of options can make it tricky to learn. If your team is small and collaboration is key, Missive might be good enough. But if you need advanced features or plan to grow, you might want to look elsewhere. A friend should check if their team really needs shared email tools and whether their email provider works well with Missive’s search. Also, consider if the cost is worth it for a small team versus using existing tools with shared inboxes.
