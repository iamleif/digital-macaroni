## Claims

- Missive launched publicly by May 19, 2017. [verified; fact; product-hunt]
- Missive lets teams share an inbox, assign a message, add private comments, and prepare a reply together. [verified; fact; official-assignment]
- The free plan is limited to three users, two shared accounts, and 15 days of visible history. [verified; fact; official-billing]
- Annual prices are $14 per user monthly for Starter, $24 for Productive, and $36 for Business. Monthly billing costs more. [verified; fact; official-pricing, official-billing]
- Assignment and internal comments commonly reduce duplicate replies and lost context for teams working in the same inbox. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, product-hunt, capterra]
- Search quality and speed are repeated complaints, especially for older messages and providers outside Gmail and Outlook. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, product-hunt]
- The number of options creates a learning curve, while mobile read state and notifications can require adjustment. [supported; reported-pattern; g2, product-hunt]
