# Missive private argument card

- Buyer: a small support, sales, or agency team sharing one or more email addresses
- Job: show who owns each message and keep the team's discussion beside the customer email
- Thesis: Missive fixes the common mess of forwarded emails and duplicate replies, but a team should not move its daily work until search and mobile notifications pass a real test
- Main reason to buy: assignment, private comments, and shared drafts make responsibility visible
- Main reason to hesitate: finding old messages is too important to accept on reputation alone
- When it is unnecessary: one person handles the inbox, or team members rarely touch the same conversations
- Decision rule: use one shared inbox for 20 real messages, search for three known old emails, and test read state and notifications on every phone before paying for the full team
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on use or support test
