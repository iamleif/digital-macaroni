# Missive spoken review brief

## What is it?

Missive is an email app for teams. If three people answer support@company.com, it lets them see the same email, put one person's name on it, and discuss the answer privately beside it.

## Who will find it useful?

Support, sales, and agency teams where inbox work overlaps. It is much less useful when one person handles email alone.

## What is good?

The assignment and comments solve a real problem. Everyone can see who should reply, and the reason behind the answer does not get lost in Slack or a forwarded chain.

## What is wrong?

Search is the part to test. Some customers have trouble finding old messages quickly, especially outside Gmail and Outlook. The many sharing, rule, and notification options also take time to set correctly.

## What does it cost?

Free is limited to three users, two shared accounts, and 15 days of visible history. Annual billing is $14 per user monthly for Starter, $24 for Productive, and $36 for Business. Most teams that need automatic sorting or outside integrations will look at Productive.

## Our answer

Use one real shared inbox during the trial. Assign 20 messages, add private comments, draft together, and check the same account on every team member's phone. Then search for three old messages you know exist. Buy it only if all of that works without special help.
