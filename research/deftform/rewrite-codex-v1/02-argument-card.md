# Deftform argument card

## Buyer

Freelancers, agencies, and small teams that need intake, lead, survey, application, payment, or file-upload forms.

## Decision

Try Deftform for a normal form when fixed pricing matters. Do not replace an important form until reliability, export, connections, and support pass a real trial.

## Why

The builder covers a broad core and customers often praise its speed and clean design. Unlimited responses and one-time offers are compelling. The evidence is concentrated on one deal marketplace, while recent complaints expose enough operational risk to demand testing.

## Walk-away conditions

Leave if an embedded form breaks, a failed connection loses or hides an answer, export is incomplete, exact branding requires too much custom code, or support cannot resolve a real problem promptly.

## Verdict

Cheap capable forms that demand a serious reliability test.
