# Deftform writer's notebook

Research-based review with no hands-on form build. Nine sources were checked August 13 and 14, 2026. Deftform launched publicly in April 2024. It supports unlimited responses, embeds, file uploads, Stripe payments, logic, teams, webhooks, notifications, and API access.

The buyer is a freelancer, agency, or small team that wants capable forms without an ongoing response bill. Quick setup, clean design, easy embedding, and one-time pricing are the strongest themes. Advanced layout, workflow, and data controls remain common requests. Most substantial customer evidence comes from AppSumo. A small recent complaint cluster raises questions about bugs and support.

The article should recommend a trial for ordinary intake and lead forms. It should not recommend immediate use for a business-critical workflow. The deciding test is whether a hard real form works on the live site, keeps every submission, recovers from a failed connection, exports cleanly, and gets useful support.
