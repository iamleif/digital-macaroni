# Deftform spoken review brief

## What is it?

Deftform builds online forms for client intake, leads, surveys, applications, payments, and file collection. It can hide questions that do not apply and send each answer to email, Slack, a webhook, or another tool.

## Who will find it useful?

A freelancer or small team that wants more than a contact form but does not want to pay for every answer or teammate.

## What is good?

Normal forms are quick to make, the designs are clean, and paid packages include unlimited responses. Logic, files, Stripe, custom domains, and outside connections cover many small-business jobs.

## What is wrong?

Most reviews come from AppSumo. Advanced layout and data controls still draw requests. Recent users also describe bugs and weak support, including an SSL problem that stopped a form image from loading.

## What does it cost?

Prepaid forms have cost €15 for five. The current AppSumo lifetime offer is $49. A Business add-on is €18 a month, and a smaller Extended add-on is €19 once. The first form is free to try.

## Our answer

Build the hardest form the business needs, not a demo. Submit it from several phones and networks. Test files, logic, payment, notices, and the outside connection. Break the webhook once and see how the answer is recovered. Export all submissions. Use Deftform if that works cleanly; keep critical forms elsewhere if support and recovery are not good enough.
