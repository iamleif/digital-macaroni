# Deftform writer's notebook

## Research scope

- Research-based review; no product test
- Current feature, add-on, and AppSumo offer checked 2026-08-14
- AppSumo: 4.8 from 95 in review page and 97 on product page; other platforms provide almost no volume
- Main limit: concentrated evidence and a recent reliability complaint

## Reader and job

- Primary reader: a freelancer or small team that needs intake, lead, feedback, application, order, or survey forms without paying per response
- Plain example: send a client onboarding form, collect text and files, show follow-up questions when they apply, and send the result to Slack or another system
- Cost of failure: an image, upload, payment, notice, or submission fails on a live form

## Product facts

- Drag-and-drop and AI-assisted form creation
- Unlimited responses, team members, and file uploads on the main paid packages
- Custom domain, logo, styles, CSS, JavaScript, logic, multi-page forms, Stripe, webhooks, Slack, Discord, API
- Prepaid forms in groups of five for €15; current AppSumo lifetime $49
- Business add-on €18 monthly for data controls, email checks, sales features, and branding; Extended €19 once for a subset
- Germany/EU hosting and annual 99.9% uptime claim

## Experience records

### A client intake form was ready quickly

- Source: detailed 2026 AppSumo account
- Action: add branding, domain, questions, and file fields
- Result: the reviewer reported a usable form in about 20 minutes
- Consequence: strong fit for normal forms that should not need a tutorial

### A live header image failed to load

- Source: recent verified AppSumo complaint and founder response
- Result: a delivery-network SSL incident broke the image and support resolution became contentious
- Consequence: run the final form on another device and set a backup and export routine

### More exact design control required CSS

- Source: detailed AppSumo accounts and feedback requests
- Result: normal brand changes were easy, while fine layout or spacing changes could need code
- Consequence: a non-technical buyer should reproduce the hardest design before purchase

## Feature translation

### Conditional logic

- What it does: shows or skips a question based on an earlier answer

### Webhook

- What it does: sends each submission to another service as soon as it arrives
- Main limit: failures need a visible retry or manual recovery path

### Partial submission

- What it does: saves answers from someone who did not finish
- Main limit: confirm the feature and data rules on the selected package

## Conflict ledger

- The product is praised as simple, while advanced styling and workflow requests remain open.
- A one-time price avoids subscriptions, but business controls can still require a monthly add-on.
- Most customers praise support, while recent low reviews describe serious delay and frustration.

## Missing evidence

- No hands-on embedded form, upload, payment, logic, or webhook
- No response export or backup test
- No uptime or support test
- No large independent marketplace sample

## Spoken language bank

- build a normal form without reading a manual
- unlimited answers do not matter if one live form fails
- test the hardest field, not the easiest contact form
- submit from another phone and another network
- export the answers before the form becomes important
