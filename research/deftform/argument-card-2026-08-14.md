# Deftform private argument card

- Buyer: a small team that wants capable forms and unlimited answers without a large subscription
- Job: collect reliable customer or client information and pass it to the right place
- Thesis: Deftform offers a lot of normal form capability for a low one-time price, but concentrated AppSumo praise and recent reliability complaints require a production-style trial
- Main reason to buy: quick building, unlimited responses, useful logic, uploads, payments, and connections
- Main reason to hesitate: thin independent evidence, advanced-control requests, and recent asset and support complaints
- When it is unnecessary: the free plan of a more established form tool already covers the job, or the form is business-critical and cannot tolerate uncertain support
- Decision rule: reproduce the hardest real form, submit it 25 times from varied devices, test files, logic, payment, notices, webhook failure, export, and support before moving live
- Evidence limit: research-based; no hands-on test
