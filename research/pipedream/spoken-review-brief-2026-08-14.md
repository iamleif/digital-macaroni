# Pipedream spoken review brief

## What is it?

Pipedream receives an event from one app, runs a series of actions or code, and sends the result to other APIs. It handles the servers and many app logins.

## Who will find it useful?

A developer who wants more control than a fixed visual automation tool but does not want to build and host a small integration service.

## What is good?

Start with a ready-made action, inspect its output, and add or replace code where the workflow needs something unusual. Webhooks, schedules, authentication, logs, and many integrations remove routine setup.

## What is wrong?

Nontechnical owners can struggle to read and maintain code. Niche triggers may be missing. Credits depend on time, memory, branches, and resumed work, so the bill is not a simple count of events.

## What does it cost?

There is a limited free plan. Deployed workflows use credits, with one credit covering each started 30 seconds of a segment at 256 MB. Paid plan fees and allowances should be confirmed in the live workspace because the pricing table is dynamic.

## Our answer

Send the same event twice, send it late and out of order, expire a login, force an API limit, and replay the failed run. Keep Pipedream if the result stays correct, a second person can repair it, and the worst normal week has an acceptable credit cost.
