  "content": "Pipedream is a no-code and low-code automation platform that enables developers to build workflows by integrating APIs with custom code in Node.js, Python, Go, or Bash. It is designed for developers seeking control over integration services without server management. The platform uses 'workflow credits' to measure and bill for execution segments, compute time, and memory. A single segment at 256 MB uses one credit for each started 30 seconds. Development and test runs do not consume credits, but paid plans include a credit allowance and charge for additional use. The public pricing page is client-rendered, so exact fees and allowances must be confirmed in the buyer's workspace. The product publicly launched its beta on October 16, 2019, and has since gained traction among developers and businesses looking for flexible automation solutions."
