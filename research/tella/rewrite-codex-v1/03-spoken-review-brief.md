# Spoken review brief

Open with the reason clips matter: one bad sentence should not force a full restart. Explain that Tella records the screen, camera, and voice as rearrangeable pieces, then adds styling, captions, exports, and sharing.

Keep the recommendation narrow. It suits frequent short demos and tutorials. It is too much for a throwaway message and too shallow for a long, layered production.

Make the test concrete. Record five minutes with deliberate errors. Replace a clip, check every join, listen for volume changes, test recovery, export to real destinations, open a private link while signed out, and hand the project to a teammate.

State the evidence limit plainly. No new hands-on test was run, and public review samples are small.

End with the buying rule: keep Tella only when the complete route is faster and another person can revise the project without a tour.
