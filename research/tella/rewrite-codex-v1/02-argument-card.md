# Argument card

- Thesis: Tella earns its place when replaceable clips make frequent short videos faster without making edits visible.
- Best for: creators and small teams producing polished product demos, tutorials, and updates.
- Skip for: quick disposable messages and long productions that need detailed audio, many tracks, or complex effects.
- Strength: replacing one bad section instead of repeating a full take.
- Risk: recording reliability, awkward joins, and inconsistent sound can erase that saving.
- Price anchor: Pro was $13 per user monthly and Premium $19 with annual billing during research.
- Decisive test: complete one imperfect five-minute project and time the full route through revision and export.
- Verdict: Polished short demos work when clip retakes save time.
