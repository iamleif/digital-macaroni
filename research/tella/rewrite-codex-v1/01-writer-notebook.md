# Writer notebook

## Product and buyer

Tella is a screen, camera, and audio recorder built around short clips. The likely buyer makes product demos, tutorials, sales videos, or creator updates and wants a finished video without rebuilding every mistake in a full editor.

## Strongest case

Clips let the creator replace a weak section without repeating the whole recording. Styling, captions, zoom, export, and sharing can turn those pieces into a presentable short video.

## Main limit

The clip method only saves time when joins, sound, recording recovery, and later revisions remain manageable. Tella is not a substitute for a deep multitrack editor.

## Evidence boundaries

Digital Macaroni did not record, edit, export, share, test billing, or contact support. Independent review samples are small. Recording failures and learning-curve complaints are reported patterns, not measured failure rates.

## Buying test

Record one real five-minute tutorial with three planned mistakes. Replace one clip, repair a pause, export every needed format, share it privately, and ask another person to revise it.
