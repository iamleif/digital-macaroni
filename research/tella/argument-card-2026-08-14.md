# Tella private argument card

- Buyer: a person who often records polished short videos and dislikes restarting after one mistake
- Job: build a finished video from replaceable screen and camera clips
- Thesis: Tella is worth paying for when clip replacement shortens the whole production job, not merely when templates make the first frame attractive
- Main reason to buy: fast retakes, styling, sharing, and export in one tool
- Main reason to hesitate: recording reliability, learning curve, per-user cost, and editing limits
- Decision rule: record five minutes with three deliberate errors, replace one clip, repair audio, export three formats, share privately, and complete a reviewer change from another computer
- Evidence limit: research-based; no Digital Macaroni recording
