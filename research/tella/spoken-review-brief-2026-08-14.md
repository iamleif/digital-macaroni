# Tella spoken review brief

Tella records screen, camera, and voice in separate clips. A creator can replace a bad section without restarting the whole video, then add layouts, zoom, captions, and sharing. That is useful for short demos and tutorials. It is not proof of recording reliability or enough editing depth for every job. Pro is $13 per user monthly on annual billing; Premium is $19. Record five minutes with deliberate errors, fix them, export every real format, share privately, and make one later revision. Keep it if the complete job is faster.
