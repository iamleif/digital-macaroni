# Tella writer's notebook

- Reader: creator or small team making product videos, tutorials, courses, or recorded updates
- Job: record a five-minute explanation in clips, replace mistakes, export, share, and revise it
- Cost of failure: lost take, broken audio, slow export, awkward cut, wrong permissions, or a link that stops working
- Pro: $13 per user monthly billed yearly; unlimited videos, 4K, collaboration, analytics
- Premium: $19 per user monthly billed yearly; branding, custom domain, domain limits, 60 FPS, deeper analytics
- Thesis: Tella is useful when clips make correction faster; polish alone is not enough
- Evidence limit: no hands-on test and small independent samples
- Decision language: make the mistake on purpose; replace one clip; time record to final link; revise from another computer
