What software can an AI phone agent connect to?

By Leif Johansen, founder of Digital Macaroni · Updated October 11, 2026

Short answer

Almost anything your business already runs. We connect agents to calendars, CRMs, practice and case management, field-service, booking, commerce, accounting and messaging tools. If your software has an API, we're happy to look at it and show you how the agent can work inside it. And if what you need doesn't exist yet, we can build it.

If it has an API, we can usually connect it

An API is the door a piece of software opens for other programs. If your software has one, even if it’s niche or built for your industry, send us its name: we’ll look at what it allows and tell you exactly what the agent can read and write there before any work begins.

No API? There are other ways in

  • Zapier and similar tools, which reach thousands of apps without custom code.
  • Email or a shared inbox, for systems that accept new work by email.
  • A spreadsheet or database your team already works from.

Or build something new

Sometimes the right answer is a tool that doesn’t exist yet: a simple dashboard, an intake form or a small booking system made for how you work. We build those too, or connect what you have and build only what’s missing.

A ready-made agent includes one integration of your choice; custom agents connect to as many systems as the job needs.

Common questions

Can an AI phone agent connect to my CRM?

Yes. We connect agents to CRMs such as HubSpot, Salesforce, Keap, GoHighLevel and Zoho, so every call finds or creates the contact and logs what happened.

What if my software isn't on your list?

If it has an API, send us its name and we'll check what the agent can do there. If it doesn't, Zapier, email or a shared spreadsheet usually works, or we can build what's missing.

How many integrations are included?

A ready-made agent includes one integration of your choice, such as your calendar, CRM or any software with an API. Custom agents connect to as many systems as the job needs.
