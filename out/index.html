How much does an AI phone agent cost?

Off-the-shelf AI receptionists start at about $29 a month. Agency-built agents typically cost $1,500 to $5,000 to set up plus $300 to $800 a month, and large custom builds run far higher. At Digital Macaroni, a core agent is $2,500 and a custom agent starts at $6,000, plus a running plan from free to $749 a month.

What software can an AI phone agent connect to?

Almost anything your business already runs. We connect agents to calendars, CRMs, practice and case management, field-service, booking, commerce, accounting and messaging tools. If your software has an API, we're happy to look at it and show you how the agent can work inside it. And if what you need doesn't exist yet, we can build it.

Read the full answer
What happens when an AI phone agent gets something wrong?

A well-built AI phone agent catches its own mistakes before they matter. It reads details back and waits for a yes before booking, lets callers correct it mid-call, texts written confirmations, and hands off to a person when it can't help. Every step is logged, so you can see what happened on each call.

Read the full answer
Can I keep my business phone number with an AI phone agent?

Yes. You can port your existing number over so the agent answers it directly, forward calls from your current number to the agent all the time or only after hours, or keep your phone system and route calls to the agent. We work with Twilio, Telnyx or the phone system you already use.

Read the full answer
Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

It depends: on your choices, and on where you and your callers are. Your agent can introduce itself as an AI assistant or not, and calls can be recorded or not. But the law may decide for you: in the EU, people must be told they're talking to AI, several US states require everyone's consent to record a call, and AI voices on outbound calls fall under US robocall rules.

Read the full answer
Who maintains an AI phone agent: hosted, managed or self-hosted?

Every AI phone agent needs a phone line, call minutes and someone to keep it working. With hosted, we run it and you request changes as needed. With managed, we run it and make changes, tune it from real calls and report monthly. With self-hosted, we build it in your own accounts and your team runs it.

Read the full answer
Custom AI phone agent vs. a free template build: what's the difference?

A template build can answer a call and book a slot in an afternoon, and for a simple test that may be enough. A production agent adds what keeps real customers happy: read-backs and corrections, your rules, connections to your systems, texting registration, handoffs, call logs and someone maintaining it.
