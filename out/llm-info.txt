## What we build

- AI voice agents: agents that answer calls and complete the work behind them, in the business’s own voice and in 32 languages.
- Automations and integrations: connections to the tools a business already runs, so a conversation ends in a booking, an order, a record or a message to the right person.
- Business software and dashboards: internal tools and back-office software shaped around how a team works, with every conversation and action in one place.
- Apps: web and mobile apps for customers and teams, from a first prototype to the App Store.

## How we work

A conversation is where the work starts, not where it ends. Our agents connect to the tools a business already runs and carry each request through to done: updating records, coordinating schedules and teams, triggering the next step in a workflow, and closing the loop with the customer.

Every project is built for one business, around its customers, its rules and its systems. The business describes what it needs, we come back with questions and ideas, and it gets a proposal with scope, price and timeline before any work begins.

## Use cases by business type

- Home services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical): books and reschedules visits into real availability, collects what the technician needs, sends emergencies to the on-call team and keeps the job record up to date in tools like ServiceTitan, Jobber and Housecall Pro.
- Roofing and restoration: handles the rush after a storm, qualifies leads, books inspections, gathers insurance claim details and asks callers for photos, feeding it all into tools like AccuLynx and JobNimbus.
- Auto repair: books service appointments, answers “is my car ready?” from the shop system, takes messages for the service advisor and sends confirmations, with tools like Shopmonkey and Tekmetric.
- Law firms: runs new-client intake, screens for the practice areas the firm handles, books consultations and routes existing clients to the right person in tools like Clio, MyCase and PracticePanther.
- Vet clinics and pet care: books appointments, grooming and boarding, takes refill and callback requests and points after-hours emergencies to the right place, with tools like ezyVet, Vetspire and MoeGo.
- Clinics and appointment-based practices: manages bookings, reminders, follow-ups and messages for the team across the practice’s calendar and records.
- Shops and e-commerce: answers product questions, checks stock, holds items for pickup, looks up orders and opens support requests in commerce tools like Shopify, Square and WooCommerce.
- Travel and bookings: searches options, builds bookings with the customer and sends the itinerary, stopping at the point the business chooses.
- Multi-location and hospitality teams: coordinates schedules, shifts and messages across locations so every team works from the same information.
- Any team with a repeatable workflow: if a person does it after a conversation, an agent can be built to do it during one.
