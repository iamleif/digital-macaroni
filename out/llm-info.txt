## What we do

Digital Macaroni builds AI voice agents that get to work and grow your revenue: they answer every call, book the job, update your systems and hand off to your team.

- AI voice agents: phone agents that answer every call, day or night, understand what the caller needs and complete the work behind it, in the business’s own voice and in 32 languages.
- Connected to the business’s tools: each agent works inside the systems the business already runs, so a call ends in a booking, an order, an updated record or a message to the right person.
- Call dashboard: every call’s transcript, summary and actions in one place for the team.
- Voice AI consulting: for companies putting voice AI into a product or moving their phones and support to AI agents. (https://digitalmacaroni.io/consulting/)

## How we work

A phone call is where the work starts, not where it ends. Our voice agents connect to the tools a business already runs and carry each request through to done: booking the visit, updating the record, sending the confirmation and handing off to the team when a person should take over.

Every agent is built for one business, around its callers, its rules and its systems. The business describes what it needs, we come back with questions and ideas, and it gets a proposal with scope, price and timeline before any work begins.

## Use cases by business type

- Home services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical): books and reschedules visits into real availability, collects what the technician needs, sends emergencies to the on-call team and keeps the job record up to date in tools like ServiceTitan, Jobber and Housecall Pro.
- Roofing and restoration: handles the rush after a storm, qualifies leads, books inspections, gathers insurance claim details and asks callers for photos, feeding it all into tools like AccuLynx and JobNimbus.
- Auto repair: books service appointments, answers “is my car ready?” from the shop system, takes messages for the service advisor and sends confirmations, with tools like Shopmonkey and Tekmetric.
- Law firms: runs new-client intake, screens for the practice areas the firm handles, books consultations and routes existing clients to the right person in tools like Clio, MyCase and PracticePanther.
- Vet clinics and pet care: books appointments, grooming and boarding, takes refill and callback requests and points after-hours emergencies to the right place, with tools like ezyVet, Vetspire and MoeGo.
- Clinics and appointment-based practices: answers calls, books and moves appointments, sends reminders and takes messages for the team, in the practice’s own calendar and records.
- Shops and e-commerce: answers product questions, checks stock, holds items for pickup, looks up orders and opens support requests in commerce tools like Shopify, Square and WooCommerce.
- Travel and bookings: searches options, builds bookings with the customer and sends the itinerary, stopping at the point the business chooses.
- Multi-location businesses: answers for every location, routes each caller to the right site and team, and keeps every location’s calendar in step.
- Any business that lives on the phone: if a person does it after a call, an agent can be built to do it during one.
