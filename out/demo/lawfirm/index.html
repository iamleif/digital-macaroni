Conversation

Tell the story of a made-up accident. The agent takes the details, checks for conflicts, decides whether the firm can take the case, and books a free consultation with an attorney, while the firm’s screen fills in. Alder & Finch is a fictional personal-injury firm; its agent answers as Nora.

Try saying
  • “I was rear-ended on the freeway last week and my neck hurts.”
  • “No, I haven’t got a lawyer yet.”
  • “Tomorrow afternoon works. Can we do it by phone?”
  • “What’s my case worth?”
Make up the accident and your details; don’t share real health or case information. Alder & Finch and its attorneys are fictional. Nobody will call you, and nothing here is legal advice.
