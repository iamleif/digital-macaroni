Conversation

Talk to Linda, Waypoint Travel’s agent. Tell her where and when you’d like to fly: she searches live airline fares, talks you through the options and takes you right up to booking.

Try saying
  • “I’d like to fly from London to New York next Friday.”
  • “Just me, coming back a week later.”
  • “Tell me more about the cheapest one.”
  • “Let’s book it.”
Waypoint Travel is fictional. Fares come from an airline booking system’s test environment, so prices are illustrative and nothing is ever booked or charged. Any name will do, such as Alex Taylor.
