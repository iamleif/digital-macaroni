# AI-writing patterns relevant to Digital Macaroni

This is a practical, paraphrased reference derived from Wikipedia's “Signs of AI writing” field guide and Digital Macaroni's own failed drafts. Wikipedia describes tendencies, not proof of authorship, and warns against merely hiding surface signs while leaving the underlying writing weak.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing (consulted 2026-08-18)

## High-value checks for software reviews

### Statistical smoothing

Models tend to replace sharp, unusual facts with claims broad enough to fit almost any product. Keep the price, workflow, named integration, failure mode, buyer, and consequence. Cut the generic gloss around them.

### Copula avoidance and missing glue

AI text often avoids plain `is`, `are`, and `has`, or compresses ordinary noun phrases until they sound like notes. Human prose commonly includes articles, qualifiers, and isolated wordy constructions.

Flag:

- `serves as` where `is` works;
- `offers` or `features` where `has` works;
- missing `a`, `an`, `the`, `that`, or `of`;
- adjective stacks that read like a verdict label rather than a sentence.

Do not delete every filler word. Repeated filler is a problem; an occasional extra word is cadence.

### Negative parallelism

Models lean on `not X, but Y`, `not only`, `rather than`, and paired negations. The construction is legal English. Density is the tell.

In a review, keep the contrast that changes the buying decision. Rewrite the rest directly.

### Rule-of-three completeness

Three examples or adjectives can make a thin point look comprehensive. Use the number supplied by the evidence, even when that number is two, four, or seven.

### Elegant variation

Models avoid repeating nouns because of repetition penalties. Reviews need stable terms. If the product is a task manager, call it a task manager again instead of cycling through `planning hub`, `productivity layer`, and `daily command center`.

### Paragraph choreography

The most important Digital Macaroni failure is larger than a phrase. Watch for paragraphs assembled as:

- claim, evidence, caveat, takeaway;
- old complaint, concession, new evidence, principle, conclusion;
- praise, criticism, balanced verdict;
- feature, benefit, warning, buyer test.

When the same sequence repeats, the article feels generated even if no banned word appears. Make paragraphs depend on one another. Do not close every thought cleanly.

### Meaning-telling

Sentences such as `This matters because`, `The distinction is important`, or `This is where the product succeeds` often announce an interpretation that the facts should already support. Keep one only when it changes the reader's frame.

### Formal neutrality

Models default to polished, neutral exposition. Digital Macaroni reviews need a defensible decision. A real opinion can include one unresolved concern without constructing a perfectly balanced debate around every feature.

## Useful signs of ordinary human prose

- simple `is` and `has` sentences;
- stable repetition of the correct noun;
- contractions;
- a lone qualifier or intensifier;
- occasional wordiness;
- uneven paragraph length;
- a definitive statement when the evidence supports it;
- an editorial choice the writer can explain.

None of these should be injected mechanically. They are permission to stop sanding every sentence smooth.
